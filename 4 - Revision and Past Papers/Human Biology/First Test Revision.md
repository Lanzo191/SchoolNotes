
## 1.[[Eukaryotic Cells Simplified]] Organelles

### Nucleus
- **Structure:** Double membrane (nuclear envelope) with pores, chromatin ([[DNA]] + [[Proteins]]), nucleolus.  
- **Functions:**  
  - Stores genetic material ([[DNA]]).  
  - Controls cell activities via gene expression.  
  - Nucleolus produces [[rRNA]] and assembles [[Ribosomes]].  
- **Exam tip:** Look for “control centre” or “contains [[DNA]]” in MCQs.

### Mitochondria
- **Structure:** Double membrane; inner membrane forms cristae; matrix contains enzymes, [[DNA]], 70S [[Ribosomes]].  
- **Functions:**  
  - Aerobic respiration → [[ATP]] production.  
  - Self-replicating; some [[Protein Synthesis]].  
- **Exam tip:** “Powerhouse of the cell” often appears.

### Rough Endoplasmic Reticulum (RER)
- **Structure:** Flattened sacs with [[Ribosomes]].  
- **Functions:**  
  - [[Protein Synthesis]] for secretion or membranes.  
  - Transport to Golgi.  

### Smooth Endoplasmic Reticulum (SER)
- **Structure:** Tubular membranes, no [[Ribosomes]].  
- **Functions:** Lipid/steroid synthesis, detoxification.

### Golgi Apparatus
- **Structure:** Stack of cisternae.  
- **Functions:**  
  - Modifies [[Proteins]] (glycoproteins, lipoproteins).  
  - Packages into vesicles, produces [[Lysosomes]].  

### Ribosomes
- **Structure:** 80S in cytoplasm, 70S in [[Mitochondria]]/chloroplasts, no membrane.  
- **Functions:** [[Protein Synthesis]].

### Lysosomes
- **Structure:** Spherical vesicles with digestive enzymes.  
- **Functions:** Break down waste, old organelles, pathogens.

### Plasma Membrane
- **Structure:** Phospholipid bilayer, [[Proteins]], cholesterol, glycoproteins.  
- **Functions:**  
  - Selective permeability.  
  - Signalling.  
  - Maintains homeostasis.

### Cytoskeleton
- **Structure:** Microfilaments, intermediate filaments, microtubules.  
- **Functions:** Shape, support, movement, transport.

### Vacuoles
- **Structure:** Fluid-filled sacs, surrounded by tonoplast (plants).  
- **Function:** Storage, turgor pressure.

## 2. [[Prokaryotic Cells]]

- **Size:** 0.1–5 µm.  
- **No nucleus:** DNA in nucleoid.  
- **Circular DNA & plasmids:** Carry genetic info and extra genes.  
- **70S ribosomes.**  
- **Cell wall:** Peptidoglycan (murein).  
- **Capsule:** Protection, adhesion.  
- **Flagella:** Motility.  
- **Pili:** DNA transfer (conjugation).  
- **Cytoplasm:** Metabolism site.  

**Functions:**  
- Protein synthesis → ribosomes.  
- Genetic control → DNA/plasmids.  
- Protection → cell wall/capsule.  
- Energy → plasma membrane infoldings.  
- Reproduction → binary fission.  
- Gene exchange → pili.

**Prokaryote vs Eukaryote Key Differences:**  
- No nucleus, smaller ribosomes (70S), no organelles, circular DNA, peptidoglycan wall, smaller size.

## 3. [[Microscopy]]

### Light Microscope
- Visible light, lenses.  
- Resolution ~200 nm.  
- Magnification up to ×2000.  
- Living cells visible.

### TEM
- Electron beam through specimen.  
- Resolution ~0.1 nm.  
- Magnification ×1,000,000+.  
- 2D, dead specimen.

### SEM
- Electron beam scans surface.  
- Resolution ~20 nm.  
- Magnification ×100,000+.  
- 3D surface, dead specimen.

### IAM Calculations
- Formula: **Image size (I) = Actual size (A) × Magnification (M)**  
- Rearranged:  
  - **A = I ÷ M**  
  - **M = I ÷ A**  

**Examples:**  
1. Cell measures 50 mm at ×1000 → actual size = 50 µm.  
2. Bacteria 2 µm, image 40 mm → magnification ×20,000.

**Exam tip:** Convert units consistently (µm ↔ mm ↔ nm).

## 4. Human [[Specialised cells]]

| Cell | Structure | Function |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **Neutrophil** | Multi-lobed nucleus, lysosome-rich | Phagocytosis, destroy pathogens |
| **Erythrocyte** | Biconcave, no nucleus, haemoglobin | Transport O₂/CO₂, buffer pH |
| **Sperm** | Flagellum, midpiece mitochondria, acrosome | Fertilise egg, deliver DNA |
| **Egg/Ovum** | Large cytoplasm, zona pellucida, cortical granules | Female gamete, supports embryo, prevents polyspermy |
| **Lymphocyte** | Large nucleus, little cytoplasm | B cells → antibodies; T cells → coordinate/kill |
| **Eosinophil** | Bilobed nucleus, granules | Parasite defence, modulate allergic response |
| **Monocyte** | Kidney-shaped nucleus | Phagocytosis, antigen presentation |
| **Neurone** | Dendrites, axon, myelin | Transmit impulses, integrate signals |
| **Hepatocyte** | Polygonal, lots mitochondria, smooth ER | Metabolism, detox, bile, glycogen storage |
| **Renal Tubule Epithelium** | Microvilli, mitochondria | Reabsorption, secretion, electrolyte balance |
| **Rods & Cones** | Photopigments, synapses | Rods → low light; Cones → colour, high acuity |
| **Ciliated Epithelium** | Motile cilia, goblet cells | Move mucus/pathogens |
| **Squamous Epithelium** | Thin, flat | Gas exchange, diffusion, reduce friction |
| **Skeletal/Striated Muscle** | Multinucleate, striated, mitochondria | Voluntary movement, posture, heat |
| **Gastric Pit Epithelium** | Mucus/chief/parietal cells | Secrete mucus, pepsinogen, HCl/intrinsic factor |

## 5. Stem Cells

- **Totipotent:** All cell types + placenta.  
- **Pluripotent:** All body cells (not placenta).  
- **Multipotent:** Limited range of related cell types.

## 6. Quick Recall

1. **Double-membrane organelle with [[DNA]]/[[Ribosomes]]:** Mitochondrion  
2. **Organelle producing [[Lysosomes]]:** [[Golgi Apparatus]]  
3. **RER [[Ribosomes]] function:** [[Protein Synthesis]] for secretion/membrane  
4. **Cell with many [[Lysosomes]]:** [[Neutrophil]]  
5. **Stem cell for all cell types + [[Placenta]]:** Totipotent
