**The small intestine** is the primary site for digestion and absorption of nutrients.

## Structure
- Divided into [[Duodenum]], jejunum, and [[Ileum]].  
- Inner lining contains villi and [[Microvilli]], which form the brush border.  
- [[Smooth Muscle]] layers in the wall facilitate peristalsis and segmentation.  

## Function
- Completes digestion of macronutrients using enzymes from the [[Pancreas]] and brush border.  
- Absorbs nutrients into blood [[Capillaries]] ([[Carbohydrates]], [[Amino Acids]], water-soluble vitamins) and lacteals (lipids and fat-soluble vitamins).  
- Segmentation mixes chyme to maximize contact with absorptive surfaces.  
- Hormones (secretin, cholecystokinin, motilin) regulate digestive secretions and motility.
