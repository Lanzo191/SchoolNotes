## Starter - 

[[Proteins|Protein]] in urine indicates what? - proteinuria which can mean [[Kidney]] failure/disease 

[[Carbohydrates|Carbohydrates]] in urine indicates what? - glycosuria is due to high amounts of [[Glucose]] typically due to [[Diabetes]] 

# Tests - 

## Benedict’s Test (Reducing Sugars)

| Sample   | Steps | Observation | Explanation |
|----------|-------|-------------|-------------|
| Glucose  | Add Benedict’s reagent, heat in water bath. | Blue → brick-red precipitate. | Glucose is a reducing sugar; reduces Cu²⁺ → Cu⁺ (Cu₂O). |
| Sucrose  | Add Benedict’s reagent, heat in water bath. | Stays blue (no change). | Sucrose is non-reducing; no free aldehyde group. |

---

## Fehling’s Test (Aldehydes / Reducing Sugars)

| Sample   | Steps | Observation | Explanation |
|----------|-------|-------------|-------------|
| Glucose  | Add Fehling’s I + II, heat in water bath. | Brick-red precipitate. | Glucose reduces Cu²⁺ → Cu⁺. |
| Sucrose  | Add Fehling’s I + II, heat in water bath. | No change. | Sucrose is non-reducing. |

---

## Biuret Test (Proteins)

| Sample                                 | Steps                       | Observation           | Explanation                            |
| -------------------------------------- | --------------------------- | --------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Protein                                | Add NaOH, then CuSO₄ drops. | Blue → purple/violet. | Cu²⁺ forms complex with peptide bonds. |
| Others (Glucose, Sucrose, Starch, Oil) | Same steps.                 | Stays blue.           | No peptide bonds present.              |

---

## Emulsion Test (Lipids)

| Sample                                     | Steps                                | Observation            | Explanation                                               |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------ | ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Oil (Like vegetable Oils)                  | Add ethanol, shake, pour into water. | White cloudy emulsion. | Lipids dissolve in ethanol but not water → droplets form. |
| Others (Glucose, Sucrose, Protein, Starch) | Same steps.                          | Solution stays clear.  | No lipids present.                                        |

---

## Sudan III Test (Lipids)

| Sample                                     | Steps                              | Observation           | Explanation                                |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------- | --------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Oil (Like vegetable Oils)                  | Add Sudan III stain, shake gently. | Oil layer stains red. | Sudan III dissolves in lipids (non-polar). |
| Others (Glucose, Sucrose, Protein, Starch) | Same steps.                        | No red layer.         | No lipids present.                         |

---

## Iodine Test (Starch)

| Sample   | Steps | Observation | Explanation |
|----------|-------|-------------|-------------|
| Starch   | Add iodine solution. | Brown → blue-black. | Iodine forms complex with starch helix. |
| Others ([[Glucose]], Sucrose, Protein, Oil) | Same step. | Stays brown. | No starch present. |
