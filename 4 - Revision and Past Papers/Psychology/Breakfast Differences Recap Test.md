### Q3) One strength  /3
**Model Answer:** A [[Field Experiment]] can be used as it has higher ecological [[Validity]] due to it being in a naturalistic environment this means that the children can be in a comforting environment and don't feel the need to lie/impress the researcher ([[Demand Characteristics]]).

**Mark: 2/3**
*   **Feedback:** The question usually asks for a strength of the *Design* (Repeated Measures) identified in Q2, not the *Method* (Field Exp). Ecological [[Validity]] is a strength of the method, not the design.
*   **Correction (for Repeated Measures):** "[[Participant Variables]] are controlled (1) because the same children are used in both breakfast weeks (1). This increases [[Validity]] as individual differences in natural happiness levels won't affect the results (1)."

### Q5) outline how the study has been designed to support the view that psychology is a science.  /6
**Model Answer:** The study has been designed to support the view that psychology is a science this is because the researcher has used a [[Field Experiment]], this allows behaviour to be measured objectively while maintaining a naturalistic setting.  Additionally data was collected in quantitative form allowing for unbiased statistical analysis to be done.

**Mark: 4/6**
*   **Feedback:** You correctly identified **Objectivity** and **[[Quantitative Data]]**. However, arguing that a *[[Field Experiment]]* makes it scientific because it's naturalistic is incorrect; Science prefers *Control* (Lab) over naturalism.
*   **Improvement:** Focus on **[[Hypothesis Testing]]** (deductive model), **[[Replicability]]** (standardised procedures), or **Falsifiability**.
