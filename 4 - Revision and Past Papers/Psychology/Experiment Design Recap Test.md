### What is a lab experiment (1)
A [[Lab Experiment]] is an experiment that takes place in a controlled environment where the [[Independent Variable]] (IV) is manipulated and the [[Dependent Variable]] (DV) is measured.

### 3 Experiment Designs (3)
- Independant Measures
- Dependent Measures

### Outline how you look conduct a independent measures design looking at gender and how rates of happiness after watching a 3D animated cartoon vs 2D (3)

One group of participants would rate their happiness on a scale of one to ten and then they would watch the 3D cartoon and rate their happiness on the same scale. Another group would do the same but with the 2D cartoon

## For the above scenario what type of experiment would you use (3)

I would use a [[Field Experiment]] as it is quick and allows for higher ecological [[Validity]] 

## How you conduct a matched pairs design (2)

To conduct a [[Matched Pairs Design]], first identify key characteristics to match participants on, such as age or gender. Then, pair participants based on these characteristics

## One weakness of a Quasi Experiment (2)

Much less control as the IV is found naturally so there are more [[Extraneous Variables]] 

## Benefit of Independent measures design (2)

Allows for the participant to give feelings without comparison to the other groups task. Stops things like fatigue and boredom
