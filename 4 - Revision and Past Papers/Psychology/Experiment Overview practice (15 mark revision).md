### Set the scene (Intro)

I will be using [[Opportunity Sampling]] outside a cinema asking people to fill out a questionnaire after they watch the specific movie scene. 


## RF 1 

To carry out this study I will use [[Opportunity Sampling]] to collect a sample. This method involves selecting people at random who are around a given area at a specific time - I would be going to a cinema from 4-7PM and using the people there at those hours to conduct the study. I would use [[Opportunity Sampling]] as it's a cost effective way to gain a large sample and given the area people would be more interested in watching the specific scenes. When I did my personal study on correlation between amount of hours studied (per gender) vs GCSE test results I also used [[Opportunity Sampling]]

## RF 2 

I will use a [[Field Experiment]] design to conduct the experiment, This means that it is taking place in real time and in an actual uncontrolled environment. I will use this method as it gives high ecological value and less [[Demand Characteristics]]. I used this in the same personal study before as I was asking people in the canteen area of my school. 

## RF 3 

I will use a [[Repeated Measures Design]] in this experiment this means that one person will watch both the aggressive and non aggressive movie scenes. This measure will allow me to collect more data from one individual person and thus speak to less people overall. I saw this happen in another personal experiment where people were asked to listen to both "happy" music and rate 1/10 how different they felt after, they then listened to "sad" music and gave another rating 

---- 

I will use an independent measures design in the experiment, This means that one person will only watch one out of two scenes and then asked to take the questionnaire this measure allows the participant to experience, this design ensures that participants experience the condition without any influence or comparison from the other scene. It also avoids order effects, such as boredom, fatigue....

## RF 4 

I will use a questionnaire about behaviour, this means that the participant will take a short personality quiz that overviews altruistic/helping behaviour. This will allow the participant to give more personal answers they wouldn't share with the experimenter out of fear of being judged. I don't have personal experience with this but in [[Bocchiaro et al]] the participants took a personality quiz after the debrief was given

The participants will be gathered via [[Opportunity Sampling]] where they were asked to memorise a list of words or look at specific pictures then recall them / recognise the pictures they have already seen. In my personal experiment [[Opportunity Sampling]] allowed me to gain a large and willing sample in a very quick amount of time
