### Example written out.

The individual/situational debate refers to how we explain behaviour. The individual side explains behaviour by looking at internal factors such as genetics and personality whereas the situational explanation uses external stimuli such as behavioural consequences or other people to understand thoughts, feelings, and behaviour. 

One area that supports the individual side of the debate is the biological area for example in Casey Et Al.'s study in the delay of gratification was explained by the activity in the inferior frontal gyrus and the ventral striatum - showing that low delayers at four years old were still low delayers as adults. This shows that when psychology focusses on the internal factors such as brain function and structure it is supporting the individual side of the debate 

However one area that supports the situational explanation is the social area, the social area explains that human behaviour is determined by external factors such as upbringing, social context and environment. This can be shown in Milgram's study into obedience, By having researchers instruct participants to bring physical harm (in the form of an electric shock) 65% of participants delivered a "fatal" shock to the informant this demonstrates that the presence of an authority figure shaped their behaviour even if it was making the participants sweat, vomit, and in one case faint.  and thus confirms that behaviour can be explained by looking at situational factors. 

A strength of the Individual position would be practical applications such as CBT (Cognitive Behavioural Therapy) this allows for holistic and detailed insight into a person by being able to break down and understand past experiences in a way that is personable, However, 
A Weakness of the Individual position would be the risk of labelling and stigma on the person by only focusing on internal flaws which can cause stereotyping and create social divisions

A Strength of the situational explanation is its wide scale practical applications, for example reducing crime rates by tackling poverty. It also is highly scientific as it allows for controlled experiments and manipulation of the environment to establish cause and effect. A weakness would be that its deterministic, by minimising personal responsibility and freewill it suggests human behaviour is entirely controlled by external forces.
