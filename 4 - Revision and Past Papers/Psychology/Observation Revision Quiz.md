## Observation Revision Quiz (28th Nov 2025)

### Q1) Identify four types of Observation.
**Model Answer:**
1.  **Covert** vs. Overt.
2.  **Participant** vs. Non-Participant.
3.  **Structured** vs. Unstructured.
4.  Naturalistic vs. Controlled.

### Q2) What is a "Structured Observation"?
**Model Answer:**
An observation where the researcher uses a pre-made coding scheme or checklist to systematically record specific behaviours.

### Q3) What is a "Covert Observation"?
**Model Answer:**
An observation where the participants are unaware that they are being studied. This reduces **[[Demand Characteristics]]**.

### Q4) Identify one strength of a Participant Observation.
**Model Answer:**
It provides high **Ecological [[Validity]]** and deeper insight into the participants' perspective, as the researcher is experiencing the environment first-hand.

### Q5) Identify one weakness of an Unstructured Observation.
**Model Answer:**
It is prone to **Observer Bias**. Because there are no fixed categories, the observer's recording is subjective and difficult to analyse statistically.

### Q6) How would you conduct an observation on people in a cafe?
**Model Answer:**
I would conduct a **Covert, Non-Participant Observation**. I would sit in the cafe acting as a customer and record data using a structured coding scheme. This ensures natural behaviour.

### Q7) Identify one ethical weakness of the cafe observation.
**Model Answer:**
It involves a **lack of informed consent** and a violation of privacy, as the people in the cafe do not know their data is being recorded.
