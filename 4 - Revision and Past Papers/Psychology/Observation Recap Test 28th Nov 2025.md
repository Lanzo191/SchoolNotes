#### 4 Types of Observations (4)
- Covert
- Participant 
- Non - Participant 
- Structured
- Unstructured 

#### What is a Structured Observation (1)
- Using a pre-made plan to collect data on behaviour 

#### What is a covert observation (1)
- An observation where the participant is not aware they are being observed 

#### Strength of Participant observation (1)
- Increased Ecological [[Validity]] 

#### One Weakness of unstructured Observation (2)
- observer bias because it lacks  guidelines there for making the observer's interpretations subjective
  
#### How to conduct an observation on people in a cafe in the morning (2/3)
- Covertly, You can sit in the cafe and act as a customer and then record the needed data whilst at a table yourself, this is the easiest way to record data thats high in ecological [[Validity]] 

#### One weakness of that method (1/3)
- Considered unethical as there is no data protection nor are they signing up.
