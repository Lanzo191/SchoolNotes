## Culture Revision Quiz

### Q1) Define "Culture".
**Model Answer:**
Culture is the shared values, practices, norms, and beliefs that make up the way of life for a specific group or society.

### Q2) Identify two weaknesses of the Functionalist theory of culture.
**Model Answer:**
1.  It can give the government/institutions too much power over the individual.
2.  The focus on "Value Consensus" can destroy individualism and ignore the diversity within a society.

### Q4) Define "Socialisation".
**Model Answer:**
[[Socialisation]] is the lifelong process of learning the norms, values, and customs of one's society (e.g., language, manners, and laws).

### Q5) What is a "Social Construct"?
**Model Answer:**
A social construct is a concept, practice, or category created by society rather than being a natural biological fact (e.g., gender roles, childhood, or the concept of "time").

### Q6) Are humans controlled by instinct or culture?
**Model Answer:**
While humans have basic biological instincts (e.g., fight or flight, need for water), sociologists argue that the *way* we express these is shaped by culture. For example, we all need food, but *what* and *how* we eat is a cultural choice.

### Q7) Define "False Class Consciousness".
**Model Answer:**
A Marxist term referring to a state where the working class (Proletariat) are misled by the ruling class (Bourgeoisie) into believing that society is fair or that their interests align with those of the rich, preventing them from revolting.
