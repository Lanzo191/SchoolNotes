## Socialisation Recap Quiz (5th Nov 2025)

### Q1) Define Values.
**Model Answer:**
Values are individualistic morals or personal rules that guide behavior. They are the ideas and beliefs that people in a society consider important (e.g., honesty, respect for elders).

---
### Q2) Identify three agents of Secondary Socialisation.
**Model Answer:**
1. Peer groups (friends).
2. Education (schools).
3. The workplace.
4. (Also: Media and Religion).

---
### Q3) Define the "Superstructure".
**Model Answer:**
In [[Marxism]], the **superstructure** refers to the cultural, ideological, and institutional aspects of society (politics, law, religion, art, media) that arise from the **economic base**. Its primary function is to justify and maintain the power of the ruling class (**Bourgeoisie**).

---
### Q4) What must you include in a 15-mark question (CRIS)?
**Model Answer:**
CRIS stands for:
*   **C**oncepts (Key sociological terms).
*   **R**esearch (Studies or evidence).
*   **I**nterpretation (Explaining how the evidence supports the point).
*   **S**ociological Perspectives ([[Functionalism]], [[Marxism]], etc.).

---
### Q5) Define Culture.
**Model Answer:**
Culture is the entire way of life of a group or society, including its social behavior, institutions, norms, knowledge, beliefs, arts, and laws.

---
### Q6) Define Social Control.
**Model Answer:**
[[Social Control]] refers to the mechanisms (regulations, sanctions, and laws) used by society and its agents (like the police or family) to ensure conformity to societal norms and values.
