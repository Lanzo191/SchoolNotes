## Task 4: Additional Paragraph (Uses and Gratifications)

In contrast to passive models, the Uses and Gratifications model, proposed by Blumler and McQuail, suggests that the audience is active and uses media to meet specific needs. For example, an individual might watch the news for 'Surveillance' to understand world events, or engage with a reality TV show for 'Personal Relationships' to have something to talk about with colleagues. This theory is evaluated positively for recognising human agency; however, critics argue it is too individualistic and ignores how media owners still shape the 'menu' of choices available to the audience.
