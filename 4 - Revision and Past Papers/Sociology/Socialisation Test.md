### Question 1: With reference to the item and sociological knowledge, explain the meaning of the term multi-cultural. 

Multiculturalism is the appreciation of diversity and varying cultures / ethnic groups that live inside a given area. In reference to the item multiculturalism is a value that can be gained via primary and/or [[Secondary Socialisation]], any agent of [[Socialisation]] can educate you on values of acceptance and appreciation of all human beings. This can be done via means of role modelling and sanctions. 

If you observe people being kind and having altruistic behaviour then that can be passed down onto you (and if everyone shared those values then it would become a societal norm)

It can also be due to sanctions. If you are "disciplined" due to hateful/rude behaviour you will associate that punishment correlates to said behaviour this means you will eventually stop repeating that behaviour in the first place. (Pavlov's study) 

Education is better than a form of punishment as it allows an individual to grow and reinforce their personal moral compass instead of not repeating a behaviour out of fear
