### **Mass Spectrometry (MS)**

- **What it does:** Identifies and measures chemicals based on their mass.

- **How it works:** The separated compounds (from GC or LC) are ionized (turned into charged particles), then passed through a detector that measures their _mass-to-charge ratio (m/z)_. This produces a spectrum that acts like a fingerprint to identify each molecule and even give clues about its structure.
