#Biology #Genetics #MolecularBiology #Blotting

Blotting is a laboratory method used to **transfer molecules from a gel onto a membrane** for detection and analysis.  
It allows specific [[DNA]], [[RNA]], or [[Proteins]] to be identified using probes or antibodies.

### Principle -  
After separation by **[[Electrophoresis]]**, molecules are transferred from a gel onto a solid **nitrocellulose or nylon membrane**, which can then be probed to detect a specific target.

### Types of Blotting -  

- **Southern Blot -**  
  Used to detect **specific [[DNA]] sequences**.  
  Process:  
  1. [[DNA]] is cut with restriction enzymes and separated by gel [[Electrophoresis]].  
  2. [[DNA]] is transferred to a membrane (usually via capillary action).  
  3. A **[[DNA]] probe** complementary to the target sequence is added.  
  4. The probe binds to the target, which can be visualized (e.g., via radioactive or fluorescent labels).  

- **Northern Blot -**  
  Used to detect **specific [[RNA]] molecules** to study gene expression.  
  Process is similar to Southern blot, but [[RNA]] is separated on a gel, then transferred to a membrane and hybridized with a complementary [[RNA]] or [[DNA]] probe.  

- **Western Blot -**  
  Used to detect **specific [[Proteins]]**.  
  Process:  
  1. [[Proteins]] are separated by gel [[Electrophoresis]] (usually SDS-PAGE).  
  2. Transferred to a membrane.  
  3. A **specific antibody** binds to the target protein.  
  4. The bound antibody is visualized using a reporter (e.g., enzyme or fluorescent tag).  

- **Eastern Blot -**  
  Less common; used to detect **[[Post-Translational Modifications]]** of [[Proteins]] (e.g., lipids, [[Carbohydrates]]).

### Applications -  
- **Southern Blot:** Genetic fingerprinting, identifying gene sequences, detecting mutations.  
- **Northern Blot:** Measuring gene expression levels, detecting [[RNA]] transcripts.  
- **Western Blot:** Protein identification, verifying protein expression, disease diagnostics.  
- **Eastern Blot:** Studying protein modifications and functional analysis.

### Key Points -  
- Each blotting type is named for the molecule it detects: [[DNA]] (Southern), [[RNA]] (Northern), Protein (Western), modifications (Eastern).  
- The method involves **separation by [[Electrophoresis]]**, **transfer to a membrane**, and **detection with a specific probe or antibody**.  
- Useful for research, diagnostics, and confirming results of molecular biology experiments.
