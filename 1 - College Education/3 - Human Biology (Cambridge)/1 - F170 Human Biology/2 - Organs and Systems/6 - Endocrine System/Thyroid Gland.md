#Biology #HumanBiology #EndocrineSystem #OCR #ThyroidGland

The thyroid gland produces hormones that regulate metabolism and [[Calcium]] levels.

## Structure
- Butterfly-shaped, located anterior to the [[Trachea]].  
- Composed of **follicles** containing colloid (thyroglobulin).

## Hormones
- **Thyroxine (T4) and Triiodothyronine (T3)** → regulate metabolism, growth, and development.  
- **[[Calcitonin]]** → lowers blood [[Calcium]] by inhibiting osteoclast activity.

## Regulation
- Controlled by **TSH** from anterior pituitary, which is regulated by **TRH** from [[Hypothalamus]].

## Clinical Relevance
- Hypothyroidism → fatigue, weight gain.  
- Hyperthyroidism → weight loss, heat intolerance.
