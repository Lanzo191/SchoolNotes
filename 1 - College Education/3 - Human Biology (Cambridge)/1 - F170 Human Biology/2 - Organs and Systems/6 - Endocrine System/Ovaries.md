#Biology #HumanBiology #EndocrineSystem #OCR #Ovaries

Female reproductive glands producing gametes and hormones.

## Hormones
- **Oestrogen** → secondary sexual characteristics, menstrual cycle regulation.  
- **Progesterone** → prepares endometrium for implantation, maintains pregnancy.  
- **Inhibin** → inhibits FSH secretion.

## Clinical Relevance
- [[Menopause]] → decline in oestrogen and progesterone.  
- Polycystic ovary syndrome → hormone imbalance affecting fertility.
