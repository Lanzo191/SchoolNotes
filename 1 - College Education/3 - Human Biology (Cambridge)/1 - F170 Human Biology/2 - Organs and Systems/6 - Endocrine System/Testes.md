#Biology #HumanBiology #EndocrineSystem #OCR #Testes

Male reproductive glands producing gametes and hormones.

## Hormones
- **Testosterone** → secondary sexual characteristics, sperm production.  
- **Inhibin** → inhibits FSH secretion.

## Clinical Relevance
- Hypogonadism → low testosterone, reduced fertility.  
- Puberty → rise in testosterone triggers development of male sexual characteristics.
