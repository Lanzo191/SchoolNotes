The pituitary gland is the **“master gland”**, controlling other endocrine glands. It has two main parts: anterior (adenohypophysis) and posterior (neurohypophysis).

## Anterior Pituitary
- Produces **tropic hormones**: ACTH, TSH, LH, FSH.  
- Produces **non-tropic hormones**: GH, prolactin.  

### Function
- Stimulates growth, thyroid function, adrenal function, reproductive organs.  

## Posterior Pituitary
- Stores and releases hormones made by [[Hypothalamus]]: **ADH, oxytocin**.  

## Clinical Relevance
- Hypopituitarism → deficiency of one or more hormones.  
- Hyperpituitarism → excess GH → gigantism or acromegaly.
