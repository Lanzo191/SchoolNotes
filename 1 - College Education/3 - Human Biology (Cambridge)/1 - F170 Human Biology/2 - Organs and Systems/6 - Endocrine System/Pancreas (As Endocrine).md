#Biology #HumanBiology #EndocrineSystem #OCR #[[Pancreas]]

The [[Pancreas]] has both exocrine and endocrine functions. Endocrine cells are located in **islets of Langerhans**.

## Hormones
- **[[Insulin]]** → lowers blood [[Glucose]], promotes glycogen storage.  
- **Glucagon** → raises blood [[Glucose]] via glycogenolysis and gluconeogenesis.  
- **[[Somatostatin]]** → inhibits [[Insulin]] and glucagon release.

## Clinical Relevance
- Type 1 [[Diabetes]] → autoimmune destruction of beta cells → lack of [[Insulin]].  
- Type 2 [[Diabetes]] → [[Insulin]] resistance.
