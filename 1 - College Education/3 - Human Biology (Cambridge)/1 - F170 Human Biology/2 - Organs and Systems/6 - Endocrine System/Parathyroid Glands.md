#Biology #HumanBiology #EndocrineSystem #OCR #ParathyroidGlands

Small glands located on the posterior surface of the thyroid, important in [[Calcium]] homeostasis.

## Hormone
- **Parathyroid hormone (PTH)** → raises blood [[Calcium]] by:
  - Stimulating [[Osteoclasts]] to release [[Calcium]] from bone.  
  - Increasing [[Calcium]] reabsorption in kidneys.  
  - Increasing activation of vitamin D for intestinal [[Calcium]] absorption.

## Clinical Relevance
- Hypoparathyroidism → low blood [[Calcium]], muscle spasms.  
- Hyperparathyroidism → hypercalcemia, [[Kidney]] stones, osteoporosis.
