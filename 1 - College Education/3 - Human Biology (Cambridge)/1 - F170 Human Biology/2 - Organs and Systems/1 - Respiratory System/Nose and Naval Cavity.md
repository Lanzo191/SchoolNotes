The **nose and nasal cavity** are the entry point for air into the respiratory system and play a key role in protecting the [[Lungs]] and optimising gas exchange.

## Structure
- Composed of external nostrils, nasal septum, and internal nasal passages.  
- Lined with **mucous membrane** containing **goblet cells** that secrete mucus.  
- Covered by **cilia**, which move trapped particles toward the [[Pharynx]].  
- Rich blood supply warms the inhaled air.  
- **Olfactory receptors** in the nasal cavity allow the sense of smell.  

## Function
- **Filters** dust, pathogens, and other particulates from inhaled air.  
- **Humidifies** and **warms** air to body temperature before it reaches the [[Lungs]].
