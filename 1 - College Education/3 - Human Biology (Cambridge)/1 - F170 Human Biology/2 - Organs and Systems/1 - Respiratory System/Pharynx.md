The **pharynx** is a muscular tube that connects the nasal cavity and [[Mouth]] to the [[Larynx]] and [[Oesophagus]], serving as a pathway for both air and food.

## Structure
- Divided into three regions: **nasopharynx**, **oropharynx**, and **laryngopharynx**.  
- Lined with **epithelium**: ciliated for the respiratory pathway and stratified squamous for the oropharynx (protection from abrasion).  

## Function
- **Conducts air** from the nasal cavity to the [[Larynx]].  
- **Passes food and drink** to the [[Oesophagus]].  
- Works with the **epiglottis** to prevent food from entering the airway.  
- Participates in **swallowing reflexes** and contributes to **speech resonance**.
