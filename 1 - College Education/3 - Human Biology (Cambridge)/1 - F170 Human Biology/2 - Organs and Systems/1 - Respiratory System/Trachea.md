The **trachea** is a rigid tube that connects the [[Larynx]] to the [[Bronchi]], allowing air to reach the [[Lungs]] efficiently.

## Structure
- Reinforced by **C-shaped cartilage rings** to prevent collapse while allowing flexibility.  
- Lined with **ciliated pseudostratified epithelium** and **mucus-secreting goblet cells**.  
- Approximately 10–12 cm long in adults and divides into the **left and right [[Bronchi]]**.

## Function
- **Conducts air** to the [[Bronchi]] and [[Lungs]].  
- **Filters air** via mucus trapping particles and ciliary movement toward the [[Pharynx]].
- Maintains **open airway** under varying pressures during breathing.  
- Participates in **cough reflex** to expel irritants.
