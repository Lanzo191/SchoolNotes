#Biology #HumanBiology #RespiratorySystem #OCR  

**Gas transport** in the blood delivers oxygen to tissues and removes carbon dioxide.

## Oxygen Transport
- Mostly bound to **haemoglobin** in red blood cells (~98.5%), some dissolved in plasma (~1.5%).  
- Each haemoglobin molecule binds **four oxygen molecules** forming **oxyhaemoglobin**.  
- Oxygen release depends on **partial pressure gradients**, pH, CO₂ levels, and temperature (**Bohr effect**).  

## Carbon Dioxide Transport
- ~70% transported as **bicarbonate ions (HCO₃⁻)** in plasma.  
- ~23% bound to haemoglobin as **carbaminohaemoglobin**.  
- ~7% dissolved in plasma.  
- CO₂ is produced by [[Cellular Respiration]] and transported to [[Lungs]] for exhalation.

## Importance
- Maintains **[[Cellular Respiration]]** by delivering oxygen.  
- Removes CO₂ to **regulate blood pH** (~7.35–7.45).  
- Efficient transport relies on **respiratory rate, ventilation, and cardiac output**.
