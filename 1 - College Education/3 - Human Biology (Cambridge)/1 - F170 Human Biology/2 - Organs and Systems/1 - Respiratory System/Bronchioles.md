**Bronchioles** are small airways that branch from the tertiary [[Bronchi]] and lead to the [[Alveoli]], controlling airflow and gas distribution.

## Structure
- Lack cartilage and are composed mainly of **[[Smooth Muscle]]** and **epithelium**.  
- Diameter is less than 1 mm.  
- Terminal bronchioles lead into **respiratory bronchioles**, which connect to **alveolar ducts** and **[[Alveoli]]**.  

## Function
- **Regulate airflow** to specific regions of the lung through [[Smooth Muscle]] contraction.  
- **Conduct air** to the [[Alveoli]] where gas exchange occurs.  
- Participate in **bronchoconstriction or bronchodilation** in response to neural or hormonal signals.
