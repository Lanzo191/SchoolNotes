**Breathing** moves air into and out of the [[Lungs]], driven by pressure changes in the thoracic cavity.

## Inspiration
- Active process using **[[Diaphragm]]** and **external intercostals**.  
- Thoracic volume increases, **intrapulmonary pressure drops** below atmospheric pressure.  
- Air flows into [[Lungs]], [[Alveoli]] expand, surfactant prevents collapse.  

## Expiration
- Passive at rest; active during exercise.  
- [[Diaphragm]] and intercostals relax, **elastic recoil** reduces thoracic volume.  
- In forced expiration, internal intercostals and abdominal muscles contract.  
- Air moves out due to higher intrapulmonary pressure.  

## Ventilation Parameters
- **Tidal volume (TV):** ~500 mL at rest.  
- **Vital capacity (VC):** ~4–5 L maximum exhalation.  
- **Residual volume (RV):** ~1–1.5 L remains after forced expiration.  
- **Respiratory rate:** 12–20 breaths/min at rest.
