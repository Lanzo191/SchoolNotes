The **bronchi** are the primary airways that branch from the [[Trachea]] and direct air into the [[Lungs]].

## Structure
- Each bronchus contains **cartilage plates**, **[[Smooth Muscle]]**, and **ciliated epithelium**.  
- Divides repeatedly into **secondary (lobar) and tertiary (segmental) bronchi**.  
- Surrounded by **[[Connective Tissue]]** for support.  

## Function
- **Conducts air** from the [[Trachea]] into each lung.  
- **Filters, warms, and moistens air** as it passes through.  
- [[Smooth Muscle]] allows **constriction and dilation** to control airflow resistance.  
- Mucus and cilia remove trapped particles and pathogens.
