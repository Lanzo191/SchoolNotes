The **larynx**, or voice box, is a cartilaginous structure that protects the lower respiratory tract and produces sound.

## Structure
- Composed of several cartilages: **thyroid, cricoid, and arytenoid cartilage**.  
- Contains **vocal cords (true and false)** and is lined with **ciliated epithelium**.  
- The **epiglottis** covers the larynx during swallowing.  

## Function
- **Protects the airway** by preventing food and drink from entering the [[Trachea]].  
- **Produces sound** by vibration of the vocal cords.  
- Provides a passageway for **air movement** to and from the [[Lungs]].  
- Helps regulate **cough reflex** to remove foreign particles.
