**Alveoli** are tiny air sacs at the end of [[Bronchioles]] where **gas exchange** occurs between air and blood.

## Structure
- Thin-walled, balloon-like structures surrounded by **[[Capillaries]]**.  
- Lined with **squamous epithelium (type I pneumocytes)** for minimal diffusion distance.  
- **Type II pneumocytes** secrete **surfactant**, reducing surface tension to prevent alveolar collapse.  
- The total alveolar surface area is approximately **70–100 m²** in adults.  

## Function
- **Gas exchange**: oxygen diffuses into [[Capillaries]], carbon dioxide diffuses out.  
- **Maintains efficient diffusion** due to thin walls, large surface area, and rich blood supply.  
- Surfactant allows alveoli to remain **inflated** with minimal effort.  
- Plays a role in **pulmonary defence** through macrophages removing pathogens and debris.
