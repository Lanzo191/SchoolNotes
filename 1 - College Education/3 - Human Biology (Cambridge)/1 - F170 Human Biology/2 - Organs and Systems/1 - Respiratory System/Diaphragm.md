The **diaphragm** is a dome-shaped [[Skeletal Muscle]] that separates the thoracic cavity from the abdominal cavity and is essential for breathing.

## Structure
- Dome-shaped sheet of **[[Skeletal Muscle]]** with a central tendon.  
- Attached to lower ribs, sternum, and lumbar vertebrae.  
- Innervated by the **phrenic nerve** (C3–C5).  

## Function
- **Contracts and flattens** during inspiration to increase thoracic volume and reduce lung pressure.  
- **Relaxes** during expiration, reducing thoracic volume and pushing air out.  
- Works with **intercostal muscles** for efficient ventilation.  
- Maintains **pressure gradients** necessary for airflow.
