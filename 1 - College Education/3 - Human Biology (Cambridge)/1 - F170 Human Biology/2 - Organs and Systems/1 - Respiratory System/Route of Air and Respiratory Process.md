The **route of air** describes the pathway air takes from the environment into the [[Alveoli]], and the subsequent transport of gases in the blood.

## 1. Entry through the Nose/Nasal Cavity
- Air enters via the **nostrils** and passes into the nasal cavity.  
- **Filtered** by hairs and mucus, **warmed** by blood vessels, and **humidified** to prevent drying of lung tissue.  
- **Cilia** move trapped particles toward the [[Pharynx]] to be swallowed or expelled.  

## 2. Pharynx
- Air passes through the **nasopharynx**, **oropharynx**, and **laryngopharynx**.  
- Food and air share the [[Pharynx]], but the **epiglottis** ensures air moves toward the [[Larynx]].  

## 3. Larynx
- Air moves into the **[[Larynx]] (voice box)**, where vocal cords can adjust to produce sound.  
- The **[[Larynx]]** protects the airway from food entry and helps initiate **cough reflexes** if needed.  

## 4. Trachea
- Air travels down the **[[Trachea]]**, a rigid tube supported by **C-shaped cartilage**.  
- Lined with **ciliated epithelium** and mucus, trapping remaining dust or pathogens.  

## 5. Bronchi
- The [[Trachea]] splits into **left and right primary [[Bronchi]]**, entering each lung.  
- Each bronchus divides into **secondary (lobar) and tertiary (segmental) [[Bronchi]]**.  
- [[Smooth Muscle]] and cartilage maintain open airways and regulate airflow.  

## 6. Bronchioles
- Tertiary [[Bronchi]] branch into **smaller [[Bronchioles]]**, lacking cartilage.  
- **[[Smooth Muscle]]** in [[Bronchioles]] allows constriction/dilation, controlling air distribution.  
- Terminal [[Bronchioles]] lead to **respiratory [[Bronchioles]]**, then **alveolar ducts**.  

## 7. Alveoli
- Air reaches **[[Alveoli]]**, tiny sacs with thin walls (~0.5 µm) surrounded by [[Capillaries]].  
- Oxygen diffuses **from [[Alveoli]] (PO₂ ≈ 100 mmHg)** into blood (PO₂ ≈ 40 mmHg).  
- Carbon dioxide diffuses **from blood (PCO₂ ≈ 45 mmHg)** into [[Alveoli]] (PCO₂ ≈ 40 mmHg).  
- **Type II pneumocytes** secrete surfactant to reduce surface tension and prevent collapse.  

## 8. Lungs
- Air fills [[Alveoli]] within the **[[Lungs]]**, which expand during inspiration due to **[[Diaphragm]] and intercostal muscles**.  
- Elastic recoil and compliance aid efficient expiration.  

## 9. Gas Transport in Blood
- **Oxygen**: 98.5% bound to haemoglobin as oxyhaemoglobin; 1.5% dissolved in plasma.  
- **Carbon dioxide**: 70% as bicarbonate ions, 23% bound to haemoglobin, 7% dissolved in plasma.  
- Partial pressure gradients, pH, and Bohr effect control **oxygen release** at tissues.  
- Continuous blood flow maintains **steep concentration gradients** for efficient gas exchange.  

## 10. Exhalation
- **[[Diaphragm]] relaxes**, intercostals relax, and thoracic volume decreases.  
- Elastic recoil of lung tissue pushes air out of [[Alveoli]] through **[[Bronchioles]], [[Bronchi]], [[Trachea]], [[Larynx]], [[Pharynx]], and nose/[[Mouth]]**.  
- Exhaled air contains **CO₂ and water vapor**, removing metabolic waste.  

## Summary
- Air moves through a **highly ordered pathway**: nose → [[Pharynx]] → [[Larynx]] → [[Trachea]] → [[Bronchi]] → [[Bronchioles]] → [[Alveoli]].  
- Gas exchange occurs at the **[[Alveoli]]-capillary interface**, with oxygen entering the blood and carbon dioxide leaving.  
- Blood transport and ventilation are tightly regulated to maintain **homeostasis and pH balance**.
