The **lungs** are paired organs responsible for gas exchange and ventilation, housed within the thoracic cavity and protected by the rib cage.

## Structure
- Each lung is divided into **lobes**: the right lung has three lobes, the left lung has two lobes to accommodate the heart.  
- Surrounded by a **double-layered pleura**: the **visceral pleura** covers the lung surface, and the **parietal pleura** lines the thoracic cavity.  
- The pleural cavity contains **pleural fluid**, reducing friction during breathing and helping maintain lung inflation.  
- Internally, the lungs consist of **[[Bronchi]], [[Bronchioles]], and [[Alveoli]]**, forming the **bronchial tree**.  
- Highly vascularised with **pulmonary [[Arteries]] and [[Veins]]** for oxygen and carbon dioxide transport.  

## Function
- **Gas exchange** occurs in [[Alveoli]]: oxygen diffuses from inhaled air into blood, while carbon dioxide diffuses from blood into [[Alveoli]].  
- **Ventilation**: lungs expand and contract during breathing to move air in and out.  
- Provide **a large surface area (~70–100 m²)** and **short diffusion distance** for efficient gas exchange.  
- Serve as a **buffer system** for blood pH by removing carbon dioxide.  
- Protect against pathogens and particulates via **mucus, cilia, and alveolar macrophages**.

## Mechanics
- Lungs are **passive organs**: they do not have their own muscles but expand due to **[[Diaphragm]] and intercostal [[Muscle Contractions]]**.  
- **Elastic recoil** of lung tissue helps push air out during expiration.  
- **Compliance** of lung tissue allows expansion without excessive effort, influenced by surfactant and [[Connective Tissue]] structure.
