The **Immune System** is a complex network of cells and [[Proteins]] that defends the body against infection. It is divided into **Non-specific (Innate)** and **Specific (Adaptive)** immunity.

## 1. Non-Specific Immunity (Innate)
The first and second lines of defense. They respond the same way to all pathogens.
- **Physical Barriers**: Skin (keratinized layer), mucous membranes, and cilia.
- **Chemical Barriers**: [[Stomach]] acid (HCl), lysozymes in tears/saliva.
- **Phagocytosis**: White blood cells like **neutrophils** and **macrophages** engulf and digest pathogens.
- **Inflammation**: Triggered by **histamine**; increases blood flow and vessel permeability to bring immune cells to the site.

## 2. Specific Immunity (Adaptive)
The third line of defense. It is specific to a particular **antigen** and provides long-term **immunological memory**.

### T Lymphocytes (Cell-Mediated Response)
Mature in the **[[Thymus]]**.
- **Helper T Cells**: Release cytokines to activate B cells and phagocytes.
- **Killer (Cytotoxic) T Cells**: Directly destroy infected host cells.
- **Memory T Cells**: Remain in the blood to provide a faster response upon re-infection.

### B Lymphocytes (Humoral Response)
Mature in the **bone marrow**.
- **Plasma Cells**: Produce and secrete specific **Antibodies**.
- **Memory B Cells**: Provide long-term immunity.

---

## 3. Antibody Structure
Antibodies (immunoglobulins) are Y-shaped **[[Proteins]]** produced by B cells.
- **Variable Region**: The tips of the "Y" which have a specific 3D shape complementary to a specific antigen.
- **Constant Region**: The stem of the "Y" which is the same for all antibodies of a certain class.
- **Hinge Region**: Allows flexibility when binding to antigens.
- **Disulfide Bridges**: Hold the heavy and light polypeptide chains together.

---

## 4. Types of Immunity
- **Natural Active**: Immunity from catching a disease.
- **Artificial Active**: Immunity from a **Vaccine**.
- **Natural Passive**: Antibodies passed via breast milk or placenta.
- **Artificial Passive**: Injection of pre-made antibodies (e.g., anti-venom).
