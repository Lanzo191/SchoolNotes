The **Lymphatic System** is a secondary [[Circulatory System]] that plays a vital role in fluid balance, fat absorption, and the **[[Immunity]]**.

## 1. Structure
- **Lymph**: A clear fluid derived from **tissue fluid**.
- **Lymph [[Capillaries]]**: Tiny, blind-ended tubes that absorb excess tissue fluid.
- **Lymph Nodes**: Small, bean-shaped organs that filter lymph and house **lymphocytes**.
- **Thoracic Duct**: The largest lymphatic vessel which returns lymph to the blood (subclavian vein).

---

## 2. Key Functions
1. **Fluid Balance**: Drains excess tissue fluid from the tissues and returns it to the blood, preventing **oedema** (swelling).
2. **Fat Transport**: Specialized lymph vessels in the small intestine called **lacteals** absorb and transport fats (lipids).
3. **Defense**: Lymph nodes filter out pathogens, and lymphocytes inside the nodes trigger an immune response.

---

## 3. Comparison: Blood vs. Lymph

| Feature | Blood | Lymph |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Red Blood Cells** | Present. | Absent. |
| **White Blood Cells** | Present. | Present (mostly lymphocytes). |
| **Proteins** | High concentration (Plasma proteins). | Low concentration. |
| **Movement** | Pumped by the heart. | Moved by muscle contraction and valves. |
