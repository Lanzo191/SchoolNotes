The **[[Liver]]** is the most metabolically active organ in the body. It performs over 500 functions, primarily related to processing nutrients, detoxifying the blood, and producing essential [[Proteins]].

## 1. Liver Structure and Blood Supply
The [[Liver]] has a unique dual blood supply:
- **Hepatic Artery**: Supplies oxygenated blood from the heart.
- **[[Hepatic Portal Vein]]**: Carries nutrient-rich blood directly from the **[[Digestive System]]** (intestines).
- **Hepatic Vein**: Carries deoxygenated and processed blood away from the [[Liver]] back to the heart.
- **Bile Duct**: Carries bile from the [[Liver]] to the **[[Gallbladder]]** or **[[Duodenum]]**.

## 2. Key Functions of the Liver

| Function | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Deamination** | The removal of the amino group from excess **[[Amino Acids]]**, converting them into **Ammonia** and then **Urea** (to be excreted by the kidneys). |
| **Detoxification** | Breaking down toxins like alcohol and drugs into harmless substances. |
| **Glycogen Storage** | Converting excess **[[Glucose]]** into **Glycogen** for storage (stimulated by **[[Insulin]]**). |
| **Bile Production** | Producing bile to emulsify fats in the **[[Small Intestine]]**. |
| **Protein Synthesis** | Producing plasma [[Proteins]] like **Albumin** and clotting factors (e.g., Fibrinogen). |
| **Transamination** | Converting one amino acid into another to meet the body's needs. |

---

## 3. Liver Disorders
- **Cirrhosis**: Scarring of the liver tissue, often caused by long-term alcohol abuse or hepatitis.
- **Jaundice**: A yellowing of the skin and eyes caused by a buildup of **bilirubin** (a breakdown product of red blood cells).
