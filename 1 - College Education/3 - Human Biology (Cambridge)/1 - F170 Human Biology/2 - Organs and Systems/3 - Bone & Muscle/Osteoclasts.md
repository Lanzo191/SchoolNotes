**Osteoclasts** are large, multinucleated cells that resorb bone tissue.

## Structure
- Formed by fusion of monocytes.  
- Ruffled border increases surface area for resorption.  
- Secrete acids and enzymes to break down mineral and organic bone components.  

## Function
- Release [[Calcium]] and phosphate into the blood.  
- Maintain bone homeostasis.  
- Allow bone remodelling in response to stress or injury.
