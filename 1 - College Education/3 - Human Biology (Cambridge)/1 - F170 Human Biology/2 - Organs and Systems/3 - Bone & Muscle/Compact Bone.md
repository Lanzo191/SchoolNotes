**Compact bone**, also known as cortical bone, forms the dense outer layer of bones and provides structural strength and rigidity.

## Structure
- Composed of repeating units called **osteons (Haversian systems)**, each containing a **central canal (Haversian canal)** for blood vessels and nerves.  
- **Lamellae** are concentric rings of mineralised matrix surrounding each canal.  
- **Lacunae** between lamellae house osteocytes, which maintain the bone tissue.  
- **Canaliculi** connect lacunae, allowing communication and nutrient diffusion between cells.  
- Covered externally by the **periosteum** and internally by the **endosteum**.  

## Function
- Provides mechanical strength and resistance to bending and compression.  
- Protects the inner [[Spongy Bone]] and marrow cavity.  
- Facilitates the passage of blood vessels and nerves through the **Volkmann’s canals**, connecting osteons laterally.  
- Constantly remodelled by [[Osteoblasts]] and [[Osteoclasts]] to adapt to stress and repair microdamage.
