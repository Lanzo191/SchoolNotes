**Spongy bone** is porous bone found at the ends of long bones and inside flat bones.

## Structure
- **Trabeculae**: Thin, branching plates aligned along lines of mechanical stress.  
- **Red bone marrow**: Fills spaces and is the site of haematopoiesis.  
- **Osteocytes**: Receive nutrients by diffusion; no osteons.  
- **Vascularisation**: Highly vascular to support metabolism.  

## Function
- Provides framework for blood cell formation.  
- Absorbs mechanical stress and reduces bone weight.  
- Allows rapid bone remodelling in response to stress or injury.
