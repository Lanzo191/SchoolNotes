**Osteoblasts** are bone-forming cells responsible for synthesising and mineralising bone matrix.

## Structure
- Found on bone surfaces.  
- Cuboidal shape, 20–30 µm in diameter.  
- Secrete osteoid, which mineralises to form bone.  
- Become osteocytes once embedded in the matrix.  

## Function
- Essential for bone growth during development.  
- Contribute to bone remodelling and maintain skeletal strength.
