**The ileum** is the final section of the [[Small Intestine]], specialised for absorption of nutrients not absorbed by the [[Duodenum]] and jejunum.

## Structure
- Long, coiled tube with a lining of villi and [[Microvilli]] for maximum surface area.  
- Contains Peyer’s patches, clusters of lymphoid tissue for immune surveillance.  
- Richly supplied with blood vessels and lacteals for nutrient transport.

## Function
- Absorbs vitamin B12 and bile salts, which are recycled to the [[Liver|Liver]].  
- Completes absorption of [[Carbohydrates]], [[Proteins]], lipids, vitamins, and minerals.  
- Moves undigested material toward the large intestine by peristalsis.
