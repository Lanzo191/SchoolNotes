**The mouth** is the entry point of the digestive system where both mechanical and chemical digestion begin.

## Structure
- **Teeth**: Hard structures that cut, tear, and grind food into smaller pieces, increasing the surface area for enzyme action.  
- **Tongue**: Muscular organ that manipulates food for chewing and swallowing; contains taste buds for sensory input.  
- **Salivary glands**: Include parotid, submandibular, and sublingual glands; produce saliva that contains water, mucus, and enzymes.  
- **Saliva composition**: Contains salivary amylase, which begins the hydrolysis of starch into maltose, and lysozyme to kill some bacteria.  

## Function
- Performs **mechanical digestion** through chewing (mastication) to create a bolus suitable for swallowing.  
- Begins **chemical digestion of [[Carbohydrates]]** using salivary amylase.  
- Moistens food and lubricates it for passage down the [[Pharynx]] and [[Oesophagus]].
