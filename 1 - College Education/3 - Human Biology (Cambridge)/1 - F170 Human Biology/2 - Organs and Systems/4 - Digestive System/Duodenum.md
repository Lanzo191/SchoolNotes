**The duodenum** is the first section of the [[Small Intestine]], immediately following the [[Stomach]], and is the main site for chemical digestion.

## Structure
- Approximately 25–30 cm long and C-shaped, surrounding the head of the [[Pancreas]].  
- Lined with mucosa containing villi and [[Microvilli]] to increase surface area for absorption.  
- Receives chyme from the [[Stomach]], bile from the [[Liver]] and [[Gallbladder]], and pancreatic secretions.  
- Brunner’s glands in the submucosa secrete alkaline mucus to protect the duodenal lining from acidic chyme.

## Function
- Neutralizes acidic chyme from the [[Stomach]] using bicarbonate from Brunner’s glands and the [[Pancreas]] (pH ~7–8).  
- Bile emulsifies fats, increasing the surface area for lipase action.  
- Pancreatic enzymes digest [[Carbohydrates]], [[Proteins]], and lipids into absorbable molecules.  
- Hormones such as secretin and cholecystokinin regulate bile release, pancreatic enzyme secretion, and [[Stomach]] emptying.
