## Overview
The **liver** is the largest internal organ, located in the **upper right abdomen** beneath the [[Diaphragm]].  
It plays essential roles in **metabolism**, **detoxification**, **digestion**, **storage**, and **immune defence**.  
It is directly connected to the [[Gallbladder]] and receives blood from the **hepatic artery** and the **[[Hepatic Portal Vein]]**.

## Blood Flow Through the Liver (IMPORTANT: Double Blood Supply)
- **Hepatic artery** → brings **oxygenated blood**
- **[[Hepatic Portal Vein]]** → brings **nutrient-rich blood** from the intestines  
- Both blood supplies mix inside **sinusoids**  
- Blood drains into the **central vein**  
- Central [[Veins]] join to form the **hepatic vein** → drains to the **inferior vena cava** → heart

## Structure
- Divided into **right** and **left** lobes.
- Contains many hexagon-shaped **lobules**, each built around a:
  - **Portal triad**  
    - branch of hepatic artery  
    - branch of portal vein  
    - branch of bile duct  
  - **Central vein**

## Microscopic Structure

### Hepatocytes
- Main functional cells of the liver.
- Perform **metabolism**, **detoxification**, **bile production**, **[[Protein Synthesis]]**, and **glycogen storage**.
- Arranged in plates surrounding the sinusoids for maximum blood contact.

### Sinusoids
- Wide, leaky **capillary-like channels**.
- Carry mixed blood from the portal triad toward the central vein.
- Allow slow blood flow so hepatocytes can process nutrients and toxins.

### Kupffer Cells
- Specialised macrophages located inside sinusoids.
- Remove **bacteria**, **old red blood cells**, and **debris**.

### Bile Canaliculi
- Tiny channels **between hepatocytes** where bile is first secreted.
- Canaliculi → join into bile ductules → join larger ducts → eventually form the **common hepatic duct** → stored in [[Gallbladder]].

## Key Functions

## 1. Metabolism

### Carbohydrate Metabolism
- Converts **glucose → glycogen** (glycogenesis)
- Converts **glycogen → glucose** (glycogenolysis)
- Produces glucose from non-carbohydrates (gluconeogenesis)

### Lipid Metabolism
- Synthesises **cholesterol**, **lipoproteins**, and **fatty acids**
- Breaks down fatty acids (β-oxidation)

### Protein Metabolism
- **Deamination** of amino acids → produces ammonia  
- Converts ammonia → **urea**  
- Synthesises **plasma proteins** (e.g., albumin, clotting factors)

---

## 2. Detoxification
- Breaks down **drugs**, **alcohol**, and **toxins**
- Converts harmful ammonia → urea
- Processes **bilirubin** from red blood cell breakdown

---

## 3. Digestion & Bile Production
- Produces **bile** (bile salts, pigments, cholesterol)
- Bile stored in [[Gallbladder]]
- Released into the [[Duodenum]] to:
  - **Emulsify fats**
  - Increase surface area for lipase action

---

## 4. Storage
Stores:
- **Glycogen**
- **Vitamins**: A, D, E, K, B12
- **Minerals**: iron (ferritin), copper
- **Blood** (acts as reservoir)

---

## 5. Immune Function
- **Kupffer cells** destroy bacteria, toxins, and old erythrocytes  
- Helps maintain clean blood from the digestive system

## Clinical Relevance
- **Cirrhosis** – scarring from chronic liver damage (alcohol, hepatitis)
- **Hepatitis** – inflammation (viral or toxic)
- **Jaundice** – buildup of bilirubin → yellow skin/eyes
- **Fatty liver disease** – fat accumulation (alcoholic or non-alcoholic)
