**The [[Pharynx]]** is a muscular tube connecting the [[Mouth]] and nasal passages to the [[Oesophagus]] and [[Larynx]].

## Structure
- Divided into the nasopharynx, oropharynx, and laryngopharynx.  
- Lined with stratified squamous epithelium to protect against abrasion from swallowed food.  
- Surrounded by skeletal muscles that contract during swallowing.  

## Function
- Directs food from the [[Mouth]] to the [[Oesophagus]].  
- Prevents food from entering the airway during swallowing via the epiglottis.  
- Initiates the swallowing reflex (deglutition), a coordinated muscular movement.
