**The oesophagus** is a muscular tube that transports food from the [[Pharynx]] to the [[Stomach]].

## Structure
- Inner mucosa lined with stratified squamous epithelium to resist abrasion.  
- Muscular layer consists of an upper third of [[Skeletal Muscle]], a middle third of mixed muscle, and a lower third of [[Smooth Muscle]].  
- The lower oesophageal sphincter prevents acid reflux from the [[Stomach]].  

## Function
- Moves food from the [[Pharynx]] to the [[Stomach]] using **peristalsis**, which are coordinated waves of muscular contraction.  
- Protects the airway and prevents backflow of [[Stomach]] contents.
