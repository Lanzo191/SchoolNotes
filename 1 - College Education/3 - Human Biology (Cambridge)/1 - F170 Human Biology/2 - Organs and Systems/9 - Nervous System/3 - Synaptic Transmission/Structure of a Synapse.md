**Definition**
A **Synapse** is the junction between two neurons (or a neuron and an effector) where the electrical impulse is transmitted chemically.

## Key Structures

### 1. Pre-synaptic Knob
- The swollen end of the axon of the transmitting neuron.
- Contains many **[[Mitochondria]]** (to provide [[ATP]] for neurotransmitter synthesis and vesicle movement).
- Contains **Smooth Endoplasmic Reticulum** (to synthesise neurotransmitters).
- Contains **Vesicles** filled with neurotransmitter (e.g., Acetylcholine).
- Membrane contains **Voltage-Gated [[Calcium]] Channels**.

### 2. Synaptic Cleft
- The small physical gap (20-30 nm wide) between the two neurons.
- Enzymes (like **Acetylcholinesterase**) are found here to break down the neurotransmitter.

### 3. Post-synaptic Membrane
- The membrane of the receiving neuron (dendrite).
- Contains **Receptor [[Proteins]]** (Ligand-Gated [[Sodium]] Channels) complementary to the neurotransmitter.
