A **reflex** is a rapid, automatic, and involuntary response to a stimulus. It is designed to protect the body from harm.

## The Reflex Arc (Pathway)
The reflex arc is the nerve pathway that bypasses the brain for speed, involving the spinal cord instead.

1. **Stimulus**: A change in the environment (e.g., touching a hot plate).
2. **Receptor**: Detects the stimulus (e.g., thermoreceptors in the skin).
3. **Sensory Neuron**: Transmits the electrical impulse to the Central Nervous System (CNS).
4. **Relay Neuron**: Located in the grey matter of the spinal cord; it connects the sensory and motor neurons.
5. **Motor Neuron**: Transmits the impulse from the CNS to an effector.
6. **Effector**: A muscle or gland that carries out the response (e.g., biceps muscle contracting).
7. **Response**: The action taken (e.g., pulling the hand away).

---

## Key Characteristics
- **Rapid**: Only involves a few synapses to minimize delay.
- **Involuntary**: You don't have to think about it (the brain only finds out *after* the action has happened).
- **Protective**: Prevents tissue damage.
