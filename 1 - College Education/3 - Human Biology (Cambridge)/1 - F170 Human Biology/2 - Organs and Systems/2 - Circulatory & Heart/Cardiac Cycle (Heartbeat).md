#Biology #BiologyF170 #CirculatorySystem 

### Diastole -  

The heart muscle is **relaxed**.  
- Blood flows into the atria from the **vena cava** and **pulmonary [[Veins]]**.  
- Pressure in atria is higher than in ventricles → **atrioventricular (AV) valves open**.  
- Ventricles fill passively with blood.  
- Semilunar valves (pulmonary and aortic) remain closed.  

---

### Atrial Systole -  

The atria **contract**, forcing the last bit of blood into the [[Heart structure|ventricles.]]  
- Atrial contraction increases atrial pressure.  
- Ventricles now hold maximum blood volume just before contraction.  
- AV valves remain open.  

---

### Ventricular Systole -  

The ventricles **contract** strongly.  
- Pressure rises sharply in ventricles.  
- **AV valves close** to prevent backflow into atria → makes the **“lub”** sound.  
- When ventricular pressure exceeds pressure in pulmonary artery and aorta, the **semilunar valves open**.  
- Blood is ejected into the pulmonary artery (right ventricle) and aorta (left ventricle).  
- After ejection, semilunar valves snap shut → **“dub”** sound.

### SAN (Sinoatrial Node) -  

Located in the wall of the **right atrium**.  
- Known as the heart’s **natural pacemaker**.  
- Generates an electrical impulse that spreads across the atria, causing **atrial systole**.  

---

### AVN (Atrioventricular Node) -  

Located between the atria and ventricles.  
- Delays the impulse from the SAN slightly.  
- This ensures atria finish contracting before ventricles contract.  
- Then passes impulse to the bundle of His.  

---

### Bundle of His -  

A bundle of specialised conducting fibres in the interventricular septum.  
- Carries the electrical impulse from the AVN down towards the apex of the heart.  
- Splits into left and right branches for each ventricle.  

---

### Purkinje Fibres -  

Fine muscle fibres branching from the bundle of His into the ventricular walls.  
- Spread the impulse rapidly throughout ventricles.  
- Ensures **coordinated ventricular contraction** from the base upwards, forcing blood into arteries efficiently.  

---

### Pacemaker -  

Refers to the **SAN** (sinoatrial node), which initiates the heartbeat.  
- The SAN sets the rhythm of the heart by generating regular impulses.  
- If the SAN fails, an **artificial pacemaker** may be fitted to restore normal rhythm.
