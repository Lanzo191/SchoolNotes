#Biology #CirculatorySystem #Heart 

### Overview
- The human circulatory system is a **double circulatory system**, meaning blood passes through the heart **twice** for every full body circuit.  
- Consists of:  
  - **Pulmonary circulation:** Heart → [[Lungs]] → Heart (oxygenates blood).  
  - **Systemic circulation:** Heart → Body → Heart (delivers oxygen/nutrients and removes waste).  
- Advantages: Maintains **high pressure** and **efficient separation** of oxygenated vs deoxygenated blood.  

---

### Route of Blood in the Heart - 

1. **Pulmonary Veins** → carry **oxygenated blood** from the lungs into the **left atrium**.  
2. **Left Atrium** → thin-walled chamber, pumps blood through the [[Bicuspid (Mitral) Valve]] into the left ventricle.  
3. **Left Ventricle** → thick muscular wall generates high pressure to pump oxygenated blood through the [[Aortic Valve]].  
4. **Aorta** → largest artery, distributes oxygenated blood to all body tissues (systemic circulation).  
5. **Body** → oxygen and nutrients delivered, carbon dioxide and wastes collected. Blood becomes **deoxygenated**.  
6. **Vena Cava (Superior & Inferior)** → return deoxygenated blood to the right atrium.  
   - Superior vena cava → from head, neck, arms.  
   - Inferior vena cava → from lower body.  
7. **Right Atrium** → thin-walled chamber, pumps blood through the [[Tricuspid Valve]] into the right ventricle.  
8. **Right Ventricle** → pumps deoxygenated blood at **lower pressure** through the [[Pulmonary Valve]] into pulmonary arteries.  
9. **Pulmonary Arteries** → only arteries carrying **deoxygenated blood**, transport blood to the lungs.  
10. **Lungs** → gas exchange occurs in alveoli: O₂ enters blood, CO₂ leaves. Blood becomes oxygenated and returns via pulmonary veins.  

---

### Major Blood Vessels - 

- **Arteries:**  
  - Aorta → systemic circulation.  
  - Pulmonary arteries → deoxygenated blood to lungs.  
  - Coronary arteries → supply oxygen to myocardium.  
- **Veins:**  
  - Pulmonary veins → oxygenated blood to heart.  
  - Vena cava (superior/inferior) → deoxygenated blood to heart.  
  - Coronary veins → drain myocardium.  

---

### Structure of Blood Vessels - 

- **Arteries:**  
  - Thick muscular walls, elastic tissue → withstand high pressure.  
  - Narrow lumen.  
  - Outside Layer of collagen to prevent bursting 
- **Veins:**  
  - Thin walls, large lumen, valves prevent back flow.  
  - Lower pressure.  
- **Capillaries:**  
  - Single cell thick wall (endothelium).  
  - Large surface area for exchange.  
  - Short diffusion distance 

---

### Pressure Differences - 

- **Left ventricle** → much thicker wall than right ventricle → pumps at higher pressure into systemic circulation.  
- **Right ventricle** → thinner wall, pumps at lower pressure to lungs (to avoid damaging delicate alveoli).  

---

### Key Adaptations - 

- **Double circulation** keeps oxygenated and deoxygenated blood separate.  
- High-pressure systemic circulation ensures rapid oxygen delivery.  
- Low-pressure pulmonary circulation protects [[Lungs]].
