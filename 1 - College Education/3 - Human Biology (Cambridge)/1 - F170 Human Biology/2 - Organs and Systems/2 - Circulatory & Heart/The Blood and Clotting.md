Blood is a specialized fluid [[Connective Tissue]] that acts as the body's primary transport system.

## 1. Composition of Blood
- **Plasma (~55%)**: The liquid part of the blood (mostly water). Transports nutrients, hormones, CO2, urea, and heat.
- **Erythrocytes (Red Blood Cells)**: Biconcave discs with no [[Nucleus]]; packed with **Haemoglobin** to transport oxygen.
- **Leucocytes (White Blood Cells)**: Involved in the **[[Immunity]]**. Includes phagocytes and lymphocytes.
- **Platelets (Thrombocytes)**: Cell fragments involved in **blood clotting**.

---

## 2. The Blood Clotting Cascade
Clotting prevents excessive blood loss and the entry of pathogens.
1. **Platelet Plug**: Platelets stick to the damaged blood vessel wall.
2. **Thromboplastin**: Damaged tissue and platelets release the enzyme **Thromboplastin**.
3. **Prothrombin to Thrombin**: Thromboplastin (with Calcium ions and Vitamin K) converts the inactive protein **Prothrombin** into the active enzyme **Thrombin**.
4. **Fibrinogen to Fibrin**: Thrombin converts the soluble plasma protein **Fibrinogen** into insoluble **Fibrin** fibers.
5. **Clot Formation**: Fibrin forms a mesh that traps red blood cells, forming a clot.

---

## 3. Blood Types (ABO System)
- **Antigens**: Proteins on the surface of red blood cells (A, B).
- **Antibodies**: Proteins in the plasma (Anti-A, Anti-B).
- **Universal Donor**: Type O (no antigens).
- **Universal Recipient**: Type AB (no antibodies).
