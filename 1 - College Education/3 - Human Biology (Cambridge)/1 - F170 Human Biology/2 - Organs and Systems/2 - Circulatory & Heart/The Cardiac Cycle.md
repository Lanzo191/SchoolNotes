The **Cardiac Cycle** is the sequence of events that occurs in one complete heartbeat. It is driven by pressure changes and the opening/closing of valves to ensure blood flows in one direction.

## The Three Stages of the Cardiac Cycle

### 1. Atrial Systole (Contraction)
- **What happens**: The muscular walls of the atria contract.
- **Pressure**: Atrial pressure becomes higher than ventricular pressure.
- **Valves**: The **Atrioventricular (AV) valves** (tricuspid and bicuspid) are pushed open.
- **Blood Flow**: Blood is forced from the atria into the ventricles.
- **Ventricles**: They are relaxed (in diastole) and filling with blood.

### 2. Ventricular Systole (Contraction)
- **What happens**: After a short delay, the thick muscular walls of the ventricles contract from the bottom up.
- **Pressure**: Ventricular pressure rises rapidly, becoming higher than both atrial and arterial pressure.
- **Valves**: 
    - The high pressure forces the **AV valves shut** (preventing backflow to atria). This creates the "lub" sound.
    - The **Semi-lunar (SL) valves** are forced open.
- **Blood Flow**: Blood is forced out of the heart into the **Pulmonary Artery** and **Aorta**.

### 3. Diastole (Relaxation)
- **What happens**: Both the atria and ventricles relax.
- **Pressure**: Pressure in the heart falls.
- **Valves**: 
    - Pressure in the [[Arteries]] is now higher than in the ventricles, forcing the **SL valves shut** (preventing backflow into the heart). This creates the "dub" sound.
    - As pressure drops further, the AV valves begin to open again.
- **Blood Flow**: Blood flows passively from the [[Veins]] (Vena Cava/Pulmonary Vein) into the atria and begins to trickle into the ventricles.

---

## The "Lub-Dub" Sound
- **Lub**: Closing of the **AV valves** (Start of Ventricular Systole).
- **Dub**: Closing of the **Semi-lunar valves** (Start of Diastole).

## Pressure-Volume Graphs
In the exam, you may be shown a graph of pressure changes. 
- When the **ventricular pressure line crosses above the atrial line**, the AV valves close.
- When the **ventricular pressure line crosses above the aortic line**, the SL valves open.
