#CirculatorySystem #BiologyF170 #Biology 
### Location -  

Found between the **left ventricle** and the **aorta**.  

---

### Structure -  

A **semilunar valve** made of three cusps.  
No chordae tendineae.  

---

### Function -  

Prevents **backflow of oxygenated blood** from aorta → left ventricle after contraction.  
Opens when pressure in left ventricle exceeds aortic pressure.  

---

### Clinical/Exam Relevance -  

Aortic stenosis → narrowing causes reduced cardiac output and left ventricular hypertrophy.  
Aortic regurgitation → leakage back into ventricle, decreasing efficiency.  
Closure contributes to the **“dub”** heart sound.
