#CirculatorySystem #BiologyF170 #Biology 
### Location -  

Found between the **right ventricle** and the **pulmonary artery**.  

---

### Structure -  

A **semilunar valve** formed of three half-moon shaped cusps.  
Does not have chordae tendineae.  

---

### Function -  

Prevents **backflow of deoxygenated blood** from pulmonary artery → right ventricle after contraction.  
Opens when pressure in right ventricle exceeds pulmonary artery pressure.  

---

### Clinical/Exam Relevance -  

Pulmonary stenosis → narrowing of the valve, increasing strain on the right ventricle.  
Closure contributes to the **“dub”** heart sound.
