
#CirculatorySystem #BiologyF170 #Biology 
### Location -  

Found between the **left atrium** and **left ventricle**.  

---

### Structure -  

Made of **two cusps (flaps)**.  
Anchored by **chordae tendineae** attached to papillary muscles in the left ventricle.  

---

### Function -  

Prevents **backflow of blood** from left ventricle → left atrium during ventricular contraction.  
Ensures **oxygenated blood flows forward** into the aorta.  

---

### Clinical/Exam Relevance -  

Mitral valve prolapse → valve bulges into atrium, sometimes causing regurgitation.  
Malfunction may reduce systemic cardiac output.  
Closure contributes to the **“lub”** heart sound.
