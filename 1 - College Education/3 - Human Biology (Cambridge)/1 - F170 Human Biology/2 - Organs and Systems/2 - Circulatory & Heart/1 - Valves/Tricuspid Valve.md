#CirculatorySystem #BiologyF170 #Biology 
## Location - 

- Found between the **right atrium** and **right ventricle**.  


## Structure - 

- Made of **three cusps (flaps)** of endocardium reinforced with [[Connective Tissue]].  
- Attached to **chordae tendineae** (“heart strings”), which anchor the cusps to papillary muscles in the ventricular wall.  


## Function - 

- Prevents **backflow of blood** from right ventricle → right atrium during ventricular contraction (systole).  
- Ensures **one-way flow** of deoxygenated blood towards the pulmonary artery.  


### Clinical/Exam Relevance- 

- Tricuspid valve regurgitation → allows blood to leak back into atrium, reducing cardiac efficiency.  
- Sound of closure contributes to the **“lub”** in the **lub-dub** heartbeat sound.
