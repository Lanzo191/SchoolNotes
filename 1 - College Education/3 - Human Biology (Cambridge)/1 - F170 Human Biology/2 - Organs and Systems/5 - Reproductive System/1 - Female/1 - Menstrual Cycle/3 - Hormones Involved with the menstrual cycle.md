## Hormonal Control of the Menstrual Cycle  

### Key Hormones  
- **FSH** stimulates follicle development.  
- **LH** triggers [[Ovulation]].  
- **Oestrogen** rebuilds the endometrium.  
- **Progesterone** maintains the endometrium.

### Cycle Stages  
- **Days 1–5**: Menstruation.  
- **Days 6–13**: Endometrium repairs and thickens.  
- **Day 14**: [[Ovulation]].  
- **Days 15–28**: Progesterone maintains [[Uterus]] lining.

### Feedback  
- Low oestrogen inhibits FSH.  
- High oestrogen causes positive feedback leading to an LH surge.  
- Progesterone inhibits FSH and LH.
