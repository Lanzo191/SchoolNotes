Ovulation occurs around **day 14** of a typical cycle.

## Events
- **LH surge** triggered by peak oestrogen levels.  
- Dominant follicle ruptures and releases **secondary oocyte**.  
- Ovum enters the **fallopian tube** for potential [[Fertilisation]].

## Purpose
- Release a mature egg for [[Fertilisation]].  
- Marks the transition between follicular and luteal phases.
