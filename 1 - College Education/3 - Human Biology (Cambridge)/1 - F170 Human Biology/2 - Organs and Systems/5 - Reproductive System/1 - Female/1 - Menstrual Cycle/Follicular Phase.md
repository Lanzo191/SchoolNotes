The follicular phase occurs in the **first half of the menstrual cycle (~days 1–14)** and involves preparation of the follicle and endometrium.

## Events
- **FSH** from anterior pituitary stimulates follicle growth.  
- Follicles produce **oestrogen**, which:
  - Stimulates proliferation of endometrial lining.  
  - Inhibits FSH via negative feedback as levels rise.  
- **Dominant follicle** selected; others degenerate (atresia).

## Purpose
- Develop a mature ovum ready for [[Ovulation]].  
- Prepare the uterine lining for potential implantation.
