The luteal phase occurs in the **second half of the menstrual cycle (~days 15–28)**.

## Events
- Ruptured follicle becomes the **corpus luteum**.  
- **Progesterone** (and some oestrogen) secreted to:
  - Maintain endometrial lining.  
  - Inhibit FSH and LH via negative feedback.  
- If [[Fertilisation]] does not occur:
  - Corpus luteum degenerates.  
  - Progesterone and oestrogen drop → menstruation occurs.

## Purpose
- Maintain a suitable environment for implantation and early pregnancy.
