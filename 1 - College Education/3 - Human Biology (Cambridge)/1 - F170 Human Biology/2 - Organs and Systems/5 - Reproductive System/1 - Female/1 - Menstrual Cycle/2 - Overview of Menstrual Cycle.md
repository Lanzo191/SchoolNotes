#Biology #HumanBiology #EndocrineSystem #OCR #MenstrualCycle

The menstrual cycle is a roughly 28-day cycle of changes in the female reproductive system that prepares the body for potential pregnancy.

## Key Features
- Controlled by **hormones** from the [[Hypothalamus|Hypothalamus (Psychology)]], [[Pituitary Gland]], and [[Ovaries]].  
- Two main cycles: **Ovarian cycle** ([[Ovaries]]) and **Uterine cycle** (endometrium).  
- Phases: [[Follicular Phase]], [[Ovulation]], [[Luteal Phase]], Menstruation (if no [[Fertilisation]] occurs).

## Purpose
- Develop a mature ovum for [[Fertilisation]].  
- Prepare the endometrium for implantation.  
- Regulate reproductive hormone levels for fertility.
