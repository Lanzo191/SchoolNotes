## Follicle-Stimulating Hormone (FSH)
- Secreted by anterior pituitary.  
- Stimulates **follicle growth** in [[Ovaries]].  
- Triggers oestrogen production from follicles.

## Luteinising Hormone (LH)
- Secreted by anterior pituitary.  
- Stimulates **[[Ovulation]]** and formation of **corpus luteum**.  
- Surges mid-cycle in response to high oestrogen.

## Oestrogen
- Produced by growing follicles.  
- Stimulates **endometrial proliferation**.  
- Negative feedback on FSH initially, positive feedback triggers LH surge.  
- Supports secondary sexual characteristics.

## Progesterone
- Produced by corpus luteum.  
- Maintains **endometrial lining** for implantation.  
- Inhibits FSH and LH to prevent further [[Ovulation]].

## Inhibin
- Produced by granulosa cells of follicles.  
- Selectively inhibits **FSH** secretion from anterior pituitary.
