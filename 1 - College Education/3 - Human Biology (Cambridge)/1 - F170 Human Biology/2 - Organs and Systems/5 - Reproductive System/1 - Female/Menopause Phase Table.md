#OCR #Component3 #Biopsychology #Endocrine #ReproductiveSystem #[[Menopause]]

## Comparison Table: Perimenopause vs Menopause vs Postmenopause

| Feature                     | **[[Perimenopause]]**                                                  | **[[Menopause]]**                                                         | **[[Postmenopause]]**                                                             |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Definition**              | Transitional phase before [[Menopause]]; cycles become irregular       | The point where a woman has gone **12 months without a period**       | Life stage *after* [[Menopause]] is complete                                      |
| **Typical Age**             | 40–50                                                              | ~51 (average)                                                         | 51+                                                                           |
| **Ovarian Function**        | Fluctuating, declining                                             | Stops completely                                                      | Permanently inactive                                                          |
| **Cycles**                  | Irregular (shorter/longer/heavier/lighter)                         | None - complete cessation                                             | None                                                                          |
| **Oestrogen Levels**        | Very unstable: sometimes high, sometimes low                       | Sharp drop to consistently low                                        | Remain chronically low                                                        |
| **Progesterone**            | Falls due to fewer ovulations                                      | Very low                                                              | Very low                                                                      |
| **FSH Levels**              | Rising, but fluctuate                                              | High                                                                  | High and stable                                                               |
| **Key Symptoms**            | Hot flushes, mood swings, anxiety, irregular periods, sleep issues | Intense vasomotor symptoms, vaginal dryness, low libido, mood changes | Symptoms may stabilise; dryness continues; higher chronic health risks        |
| **Cognitive Effects**       | Brain fog, memory lapses, poor concentration                       | Cognitive slowing, emotional sensitivity                              | Processing speed may stay reduced; memory stabilises                          |
| **Long-Term Health Risks**  | Mild bone loss begins                                              | Significant bone loss, cardiovascular vulnerability increases         | Highest risk for **osteoporosis**, **CVD**, and urogenital atrophy            |
| **Diagnostic Criteria**     | Clinical symptoms + irregular periods                              | 12 months without menstruation                                        | N/A (already diagnosed)                                                       |
| **Hormone Pattern Summary** | Hormones fluctuate unpredictably                                   | Oestrogen drops sharply                                               | Oestrogen stays permanently low                                               |
| **Treatment Options**       | Lifestyle changes, low-dose HRT, progesterone support              | HRT (combined unless hysterectomy), non-hormonal symptom relief       | HRT (if benefits outweigh risks), local oestrogen, bone-protection medication |
| **Notable Features**        | Most chaotic hormone stage                                         | When [[Menopause]] *officially* occurs                                    | Highest long-term health considerations                                       |

## Quick Summary (Indented Overview)

- **[[Perimenopause]]** → Hormones fluctuate. Symptoms begin. Periods irregular.  
- **[[Menopause]]** → Periods stop permanently. Oestrogen collapses. Symptoms peak.  
- **[[Postmenopause]]** → Hormones stay low forever. Long-term health risks increase.
