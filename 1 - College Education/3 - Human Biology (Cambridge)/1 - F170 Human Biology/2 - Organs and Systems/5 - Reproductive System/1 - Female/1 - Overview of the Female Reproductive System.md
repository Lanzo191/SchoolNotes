## Female Reproductive System  
The **female reproductive system** is adapted to produce ova and support [[Fertilisation]] and pregnancy.

### Structure  
- **[[Ovaries]]** contain follicles at different stages of development.  
- **Oviducts (fallopian tubes)** transport ova and are the site of [[Fertilisation]].  
- **[[Uterus]]** has a thick muscular wall and vascular lining.  
- **[[Cervix]]** controls entry to the [[Uterus]].  
- **[[Vagina]]** receives sperm and acts as the birth canal.

### Function  
- Production of ova through [[Oogenesis]].  
- Secretion of oestrogen and progesterone.  
- Support of implantation and foetal development.

### Adaptations  
- Thickened endometrium for implantation.  
- Ciliated oviduct lining to move ova.  
- Strong uterine muscles for childbirth.
