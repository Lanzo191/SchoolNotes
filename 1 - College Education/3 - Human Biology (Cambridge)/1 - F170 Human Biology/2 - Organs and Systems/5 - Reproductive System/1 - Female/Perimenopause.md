## Overview
- Perimenopause is the **transition period before [[Menopause]]** when ovarian function begins to decline.
- Typically starts **in the 40s** (but can begin earlier).
- Characterised by **irregular menstrual cycles** and fluctuating hormone levels.
- Can last **4–10 years**.

## Hormonal Changes
- **Oestrogen fluctuates** wildly (can be high OR low).
- **Progesterone consistently decreases** due to fewer ovulations.
- **FSH rises** as the pituitary attempts to stimulate unresponsive [[Ovaries]].
- Hormone imbalance causes unpredictable symptoms.

## Key Symptoms
- Irregular periods (shorter, longer, heavier, lighter)
- Hot flushes & night sweats  
- Mood swings, irritability
- Anxiety and low mood
- Sleep disturbances  
- Brain fog / poor concentration  
- Breast tenderness  
- Decreased libido  
- Vaginal dryness  
- Weight gain (especially central)

## Biological Mechanisms
- Oestrogen affects:  
  - [[Thermoregulation]] (→ hot flushes)  
  - Serotonin (→ mood changes)  
  - Vaginal tissue elasticity (→ dryness)  
  - Bone density regulation  
- Reduced progesterone → worsened sleep and anxiety (calming effect removed).

## Psychological & Cognitive Effects
- Emotional reactivity increases  
- Memory lapses & reduced working memory  
- Stress tolerance decreases  
- Increased risk of depressive episodes (especially with prior history)

## Diagnostic Notes
- No single blood test needed → diagnosis based on **changing cycles + symptoms**.
- FSH may be elevated but fluctuates too much for reliable diagnosis.

## Treatment Overview
- Lifestyle: sleep hygiene, exercise, diet changes  
- Non-hormonal: SSRIs/SNRIs, clonidine  
- Hormonal: low-dose combined HRT or progesterone (cycle regulation)
