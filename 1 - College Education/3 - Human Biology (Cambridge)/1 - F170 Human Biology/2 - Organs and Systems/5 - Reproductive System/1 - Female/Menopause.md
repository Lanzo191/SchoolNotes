## Overview
- Menopause = the point when a woman has had **12 consecutive months without menstruation**.
- Average age: **51**.
- Marks the **end of natural fertility**.

## Hormonal Status
- **Oestrogen levels fall sharply**.
- **Progesterone remains low** due to lack of [[Ovulation]].
- **FSH and LH remain chronically high** due to lack of negative feedback.
- [[Ovaries]] stop releasing eggs entirely.

## Key Symptoms
- Hot flushes and night sweats (vasomotor symptoms)
- Vaginal dryness and painful intercourse
- Loss of libido
- Mood instability / irritability
- Persistent sleep disturbances
- Joint aches, muscle stiffness
- Skin thinning, dry hair
- Increased abdominal fat

## Physiological Changes
- Reduced oestrogen →  
  - **Decreased bone density** → risk of osteoporosis  
  - **Skin collagen loss**  
  - **Reduced cardiovascular protection** → higher CVD risk  
  - **Thinner vaginal epithelium**  
  - **Slower metabolism**  

## Mental Health Effects
- Increased vulnerability to:  
  - Anxiety  
  - Low mood  
  - Reduced stress resilience  
  - Cognitive slowing  

## Diagnosis
- Clinical diagnosis: **12 months without a period**.
- FSH testing unnecessary for women over 45 unless diagnostic uncertainty.

## Treatment Overview
- **HRT** (oestrogen + progesterone unless hysterectomy)
- Non-hormonal treatments for vasomotor symptoms
- Lifestyle: weight-bearing exercise, diet high in [[Calcium]]/vitamin D
