## Overview
- Postmenopause = the life stage **after [[Menopause]] has occurred**.
- Symptoms may continue but generally become more stable.
- Hormone levels remain low permanently.

## Hormonal Profile
- **Very low oestrogen**  
- **Very low progesterone**  
- **High FSH and LH** with no cyclic variation  
- [[Ovaries]] no longer release eggs or produce significant hormones

## Long-Term Health Effects
### Bone Health
- Low oestrogen → accelerated bone loss  
- Highest risk of **osteoporosis** and fractures  
- Common sites: hip, spine, wrist

### Cardiovascular System
- Loss of oestrogen's protective effects →  
  - Increased risk of coronary heart disease  
  - Higher LDL cholesterol, lower HDL  
  - Greater arterial stiffness  

### Urogenital System
- Urogenital atrophy:  
  - Vaginal dryness  
  - Painful intercourse  
  - Higher UTI risk  
  - Reduced pelvic floor strength  

### Cognitive & Mental Health
- Memory issues may stabilise but processing speed remains slower  
- Sleep disturbances may persist  
- Mood changes often improve but can remain if untreated

## Symptoms
- Hot flushes may continue (for months or years)  
- Vaginal dryness often gets worse without treatment  
- Reduced muscle tone & strength  
- Weight gain + redistribution of fat to the abdomen  

## Treatment and Management
- **HRT** still recommended for some women if benefits > risks  
- Topical oestrogen for vaginal symptoms  
- Lifestyle: weight-bearing exercise, cardiovascular fitness  
- Bone protection: [[Calcium]], vitamin D, bisphosphonates if required
