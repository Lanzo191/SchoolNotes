## Gametogenesis  

### Spermatogenesis  
- Occurs in the seminiferous tubules.  
- Diploid cells undergo [[Meiosis]] to form four haploid sperm.  
- Continuous from puberty.

### Oogenesis  
- Occurs in the [[Ovaries]].  
- Produces one haploid ovum and polar bodies.  
- Begins before birth and continues cyclically.

### Comparison  
- Spermatogenesis is continuous; [[Oogenesis]] is cyclical.  
- Sperm are small and motile; ova are large and nutrient-rich.
