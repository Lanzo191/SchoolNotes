The epididymis is an elongated tubular genital organ attached to the posterior side of each one of the two male reproductive glands, the testicles. It is a single, narrow, tightly coiled tube in adult humans, 6 to 7 centimetres in length; uncoiled the tube would be approximately 6 m long. It connects the testicle to the [[Vas Deferens]] in the male reproductive system. The epididymis serves as an interconnection between the multiple efferent ducts at the rear of a testicle, and the [[Vas Deferens]]. Its primary function is the storage, maturation and transport of [[Sperm Cells]]
