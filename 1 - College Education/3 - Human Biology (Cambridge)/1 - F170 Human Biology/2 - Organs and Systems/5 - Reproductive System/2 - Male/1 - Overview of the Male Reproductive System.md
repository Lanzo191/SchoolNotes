The **male reproductive system** is adapted to produce, store, and deliver large numbers of sperm continuously from puberty.

### Structure  
- **[[Testes]]** are located in the scrotum to maintain a temperature lower than body temperature.  
- **Seminiferous tubules** are the site of sperm production.  
- **Leydig cells** secrete testosterone.  
- **[[Epididymis]]** stores and matures sperm.  
- **[[Vas Deferens]]** transports sperm during ejaculation.  
- **Accessory glands** include seminal vesicles and the [[Prostate Gland]].

### Function  
- Production of sperm via [[Meiosis]].  
- Secretion of testosterone.  
- Transport and ejaculation of sperm.

### Adaptations  
- External [[Testes]] for efficient [[Meiosis]].  
- High rate of cell division.  
- Sperm contain a flagellum and many [[Mitochondria]] for movement.
