### Diabetes type 1 

- Type 1 is an autoimmune disease where the [[Pancreas]] doesn't produce [[Insulin]]

### Diabetes type 2 

- Type 2 is a metabolic disorder where the body becomes resistant to [[Insulin]] or doesn't produce enough 

### Combined Symptoms 

- Common symptoms of both type 1 and type 2 diabetes include increased thirst, frequent urination, extreme hunger, fatigue, blurred vision, and slow-healing sores. Type 1 diabetes symptoms often develop quickly, while type 2 diabetes symptoms can take years to appear and may be mild or unnoticed.
