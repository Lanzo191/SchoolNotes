### Hypothermia  
Hypothermia occurs when **core temperature drops below 35°C**. It reduces the rate of metabolic reactions and slows nerve conduction, affecting coordination and consciousness.

### Causes  
- Cold weather exposure  
- Cold water immersion  
- Inadequate insulation  
- Alcohol consumption (causes [[Vasodilation]] → heat loss)

### Physiological Consequences  
- Reduced enzyme activity  
- Shivering becomes uncontrollable then ceases as energy depletes  
- Heart rate and breathing slow  
- Risk of cardiac arrest  
- Confusion and impaired judgement  

### Body Responses  
- Maximum [[Vasoconstriction]]  
- Shivering  
- Increased [[Adrenaline]] and thyroxine to raise metabolic heat  
- Behavioural responses (seeking warmth)

---

### Hyperthermia  
Hyperthermia occurs when **body temperature rises above 38–40°C**, overwhelming the body’s ability to lose heat.

### Causes  
- Hot environment  
- Exercise without adequate hydration  
- Fever due to infection  
- Drugs that impair sweating (e.g. antihistamines)

### Physiological Consequences  
- Enzyme denaturation  
- Dehydration from sweating  
- Salt imbalance → muscle cramps  
- Heat exhaustion or heat stroke  
- Organ failure if untreated

### Body Responses  
- Maximal [[Vasodilation]]  
- Heavy sweating  
- Reduced activity to decrease heat production
