#Biology #[[Thermoregulation]] #Vasodilation

### Vasodilation  
Vasodilation is the **widening of arterioles** near the skin, allowing more warm blood to flow into surface capillary beds. This increases heat loss to the environment to prevent overheating.

### Mechanism  
- [[Hypothalamus]] detects increased blood temperature.  
- Sympathetic impulses to arteriole muscles decrease.  
- [[Smooth Muscle]] relaxes, causing dilation.  
- More blood flows near the surface → more heat lost.  

### Heat Loss Processes Enhanced  
- **Radiation:** heat radiates from warm blood to cooler air.  
- **Conduction/Convection:** more warm air around skin is replaced by cooler air.  
- **Evaporation:** sweating increases efficiency of cooling.

### Effects  
- Skin becomes red and warm  
- Sweat production increases  
- Core temperature begins to fall toward normal

### Importance  
Prevents hyperthermia and protects enzymes from heat denaturation, which would disrupt metabolic reactions.
