## Adrenaline 
- **Type:** Amino acid-derived (amine)  
- **Source:** Adrenal medulla  
- **Target Cells/Organs:** Heart, [[Lungs]], [[Liver|Liver]], blood vessels, [[Skeletal Muscle]]  
- **Function:**  
  - Increases heart rate and cardiac output.  
  - Dilates [[Bronchioles]] for improved oxygen intake.  
  - Stimulates glycogen breakdown in [[Liver|Liver]] and [[Skeletal Muscle]] → increases blood [[Glucose]].  
  - Constricts blood vessels in skin and gut, redirects blood to muscles.  
- **Purpose:** Rapid stress/fight-or-flight response.
