## Antidiuretic Hormone (ADH)
- **Type:** Peptide hormone  
- **Source:** Posterior pituitary (produced in [[Hypothalamus]]
- **Target Cells/Organs:** [[Kidney]] collecting ducts, blood vessels  
- **Function:**  
  - Increases water reabsorption via aquaporin insertion in kidneys.  
  - Causes [[Vasoconstriction]] → increases blood pressure.  
- **Purpose:** Maintains fluid balance and blood pressure.
