## Calcitonin
- **Type:** Peptide hormone  
- **Source:** [[Thyroid Gland]] (parafollicular C cells)  
- **Target Cells/Organs:** [[Osteoclasts]] in bones, kidneys  
- **Function:**  
  - Inhibits osteoclast activity → reduces bone resorption.  
  - Enhances [[Calcium]] excretion in kidneys.  
- **Purpose:** Lowers blood [[Calcium]] levels.
