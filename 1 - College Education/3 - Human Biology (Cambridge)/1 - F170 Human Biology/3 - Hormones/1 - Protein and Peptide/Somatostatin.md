## Somatostatin 
- **Type:** Peptide hormone  
- **Source:** Delta cells of [[Pancreas]], [[Hypothalamus|Hypothalamus (Psychology)]]  
- **Target Cells/Organs:** Anterior pituitary, [[Pancreas]], gastrointestinal tract  
- **Function:**  
  - Inhibits secretion of **growth hormone (GH)** and **thyroid-stimulating hormone (TSH)**.  
  - Reduces [[Insulin]] and glucagon release.  
  - Slows GI motility and nutrient absorption.  
- **Purpose:** Regulates and balances other hormone secretions.
