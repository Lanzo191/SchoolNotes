## Insulin
- **Type:** Peptide hormone  
- **Source:** Beta cells of [[Pancreas]]  
- **Target Cells/Organs:** [[Liver|Liver]], muscle, adipose tissue  
- **Function:**  
  - Promotes [[Glucose]] uptake into cells.  
  - Stimulates glycogen, lipid, and [[Protein Synthesis]].  
- **Purpose:** Lowers blood [[Glucose]] and stores energy.
