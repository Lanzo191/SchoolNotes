## Thyroxine (T4)
- **Type:** Amino acid-derived (iodinated tyrosine)  

- **Target Cells/Organs:** Most body cells, especially [[Liver|Liver]], heart, and [[Nervous Tissue]]  

- **Function:**  
  - Increases basal metabolic rate (BMR) → energy production.  
  - Stimulates [[Protein Synthesis]] and growth.  
  - Supports normal **development of nervous system**.  

- **Purpose:** Regulates metabolism, growth, and development.

* **Structure:** A derivative of tyrosine, consisting of two iodinated phenolic rings linked by an ether bond.

* **Synthesis:** Produced by the [[Thyroid Gland]] through the process of thyroid hormone synthesis, which involves the conversion of tyrosine to diiodotyrosine (DIT) and then to T4.

* **Conversion:** T4 is converted to triiodothyronine (T3) in peripheral tissues, with varying degrees of efficiency depending on individual factors such as age, nutrition, and health status.
