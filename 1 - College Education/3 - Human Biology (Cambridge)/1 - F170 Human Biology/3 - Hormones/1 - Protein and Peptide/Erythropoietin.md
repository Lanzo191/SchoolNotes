## Erythropoietin 
- **Type:** Glycoprotein (peptide)  
- **Source:** Kidneys (peritubular fibroblasts)  
- **Target Cells/Organs:** Bone marrow (erythroid progenitor cells)  
- **Function:**  
  - Stimulates red blood cell production.  
  - Responds to low oxygen levels (hypoxia).  
- **Purpose:** Maintains oxygen-carrying capacity of blood.
