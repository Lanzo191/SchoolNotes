## Why use a tissue culture 
- Drug testing without actual patients 
- Understanding diseases 
- Reduces animal harm 
- Genetic Research 
- Vaccine Development 

## Limitations - 
- No immune system
- No blood Supply 
- Hard to mimic complex organ interactions 
- Can be Expensive and needs Expertise
