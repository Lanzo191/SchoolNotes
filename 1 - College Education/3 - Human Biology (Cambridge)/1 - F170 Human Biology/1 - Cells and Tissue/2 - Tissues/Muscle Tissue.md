Muscle tissue is specialised for **contraction**, producing **movement**, **posture**, and **heat**.

### Structure  
- Long, excitable cells containing **actin** and **myosin**  
- Rich blood supply  
- Highly metabolically active

### Types  
1. **[[Skeletal Muscle]]**  
   - Striated, voluntary  
   - Attached to bones  
   - Multinucleated  
   - Allows movement and posture

2. **[[Cardiac Muscle]]**  
   - Striated, involuntary  
   - Found only in the heart  
   - Contains **intercalated discs** for coordinated contraction  

3. **[[Smooth Muscle]]**  
   - Non-striated, involuntary  
   - Found in walls of organs (intestine, bladder, blood vessels)  
   - Controls movement of substances through organs  

### Functions  
- Movement of the skeleton  
- Pumping of the heart  
- Control of organ diameter and movement of contents
