Epithelial tissue covers **body surfaces**, lines **organs and cavities**, and forms **glands**. It is tightly packed and forms protective barriers.

### Structure  
- Closely packed cells with **minimal extracellular matrix**  
- Attached to a **basement membrane**  
- Avascular (no blood vessels)  
- High rate of cell division

### Types (by shape)  
- **Squamous** – thin and flat  
- **Cuboidal** – cube-shaped  
- **Columnar** – tall and rectangular  

### Types (by layers)  
- **Simple** – one layer  
- **Stratified** – multiple layers  
- **Pseudostratified** – appears layered but isn’t  
- **Transitional** – stretches (e.g., bladder)

### Functions  
- Protection (skin)  
- Absorption (intestine)  
- Secretion (glands)  
- Filtration (kidneys)  
- Gas exchange ([[Lungs]])
