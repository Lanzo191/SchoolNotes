This glossary provides a high-density reference for the technical terminology used across the "Genetics and Molecular Biology" folders. It is designed to ensure accuracy in OCR exam definitions.

## A - C

**Allele**: 
- One of two or more alternative forms of a gene that arise by mutation and are found at the same place on a chromosome.

**Anticodon**: 
- A sequence of three [[Nucleotides]] in a [[tRNA]] molecule that corresponds to a complementary [[Codon]] in [[mRNA]] during [[Translation]].

**Centromere**: 
- The specialized [[DNA]] sequence of a chromosome that links a pair of sister chromatids and acts as the attachment site for spindle fibers.

**Chromatin**: 
- The material of which the [[Chromosomes]] of organisms are composed, consisting of protein, [[RNA]], and [[DNA]]. (See: **Euchromatin** vs **Heterochromatin**).

**[[Codon]]**: 
- A sequence of three [[Nucleotides]] which together form a unit of [[Genetic Code]] in a [[DNA]] or [[RNA]] molecule.

## D - G

**Degenerate Code**: 
- A property of the [[Genetic Code]] where multiple different [[Codon]] can code for the same single amino acid (e.g., 6 different [[Codon]] for Leucine).

**Exon**: 
- A segment of a [[DNA]] or [[RNA]] molecule containing information coding for a protein or peptide sequence.

**Genotype**: 
- The genetic constitution of an individual organism; the specific alleles present at a locus.

**Genome**: 
- The complete set of genetic material ([[DNA]]) present in an organism or virus.

## H - M

**Histone**: 
- Any of a group of basic [[Proteins]] found in chromatin that act as "spools" around which [[DNA]] winds to create nucleosomes.

**Intron**: 
- A non-coding segment of a [[DNA]] or [[RNA]] molecule which is removed during [[RNA]] processing (splicing).

**Locus**: 
- The specific physical location or position of a gene or other [[DNA]] sequence on a chromosome.

**Methylation**: 
- The addition of a methyl group (-CH3) to [[DNA]] (usually Cytosine), typically acting to repress gene [[Transcription]] (Epigenetics).

## N - R

**Nucleosome**: 
- The fundamental subunit of chromatin; consists of a section of [[DNA]] wrapped around a core of eight histone [[Proteins]].

**Phenotype**: 
- The set of observable characteristics of an individual resulting from the interaction of its genotype with the environment.

**Purine**: 
- A nitrogenous base with a double-ring structure: **Adenine (A)** and **Guanine (G)**.

**Pyrimidine**: 
- A nitrogenous base with a single-ring structure: **Thymine (T)**, **Cytosine (C)**, and **Uracil (U)**.

## S - Z

**[[Transcription]]**: 
- The process of creating an [[RNA]] copy ([[mRNA]]) from a [[DNA]] template.

**[[Translation]]**: 
- The process by which a ribosome decodes [[mRNA]] to produce a specific amino acid chain (polypeptide).

**Zygote**: 
- A diploid cell resulting from the fusion of two haploid gametes (sperm and egg).
