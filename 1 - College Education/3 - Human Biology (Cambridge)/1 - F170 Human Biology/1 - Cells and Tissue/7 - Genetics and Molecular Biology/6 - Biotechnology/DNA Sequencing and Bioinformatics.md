**[[DNA]] Sequencing** is the process of determining the precise order of [[Nucleotides]] within a [[DNA]] molecule, while **Bioinformatics** is the use of software to analyze that biological data.

## Structure: Sanger Sequencing (The Foundation)
- Uses **dideoxynucleotides (ddNTPs)** which act as "chain terminators." 
- When a ddNTP is incorporated, [[DNA]] synthesis stops.
- By using four different fluorescently labeled ddNTPs, the sequence can be read by a laser.

## Function
- **Genome Mapping**: Understanding the entire genetic blueprint of an organism.
- **Phylogenetics**: Comparing sequences between species to determine evolutionary relationships (e.g., verifying the "crabification" of different lineages).

## Clinical Relevance
- **Personalized Medicine**: Sequencing a patient's genome to determine which cancer drugs will be most effective for their specific mutations.

## Summary
Sequencing turns biological matter into digital data, allowing us to read the book of life one "letter" at a time.
