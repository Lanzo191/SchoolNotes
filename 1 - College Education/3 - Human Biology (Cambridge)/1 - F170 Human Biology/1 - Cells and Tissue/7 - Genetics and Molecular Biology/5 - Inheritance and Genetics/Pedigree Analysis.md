**Pedigree Analysis** is the study of an inherited trait in a group of related individuals to determine the pattern of inheritance and predict the risk of recurrence in future generations.

## Structure: Common Symbols
- **Squares**: Males.
- **Circles**: Females.
- **Shaded**: Affected individuals.
- **Half-shaded**: Known carriers (in some diagrams).
- **Horizontal Line**: Mating.

### Patterns to Recognize
- **Autosomal Dominant**: Every affected person has an affected parent; doesn't skip generations.
- **Autosomal Recessive**: Can skip generations; affected individuals may have unaffected (carrier) parents.
- **X-linked Recessive**: Primarily affects males; never passed from father to son.

## Function
- Used by genetic counselors to calculate the probability of a child inheriting a genetic disease.

## Clinical Relevance
- **Family History (Hx)**: Essential for diagnosing rare genetic syndromes and assessing cancer risk.

## Summary
Pedigrees are visual tools that map the journey of a gene through a family tree, revealing the underlying mode of inheritance.
