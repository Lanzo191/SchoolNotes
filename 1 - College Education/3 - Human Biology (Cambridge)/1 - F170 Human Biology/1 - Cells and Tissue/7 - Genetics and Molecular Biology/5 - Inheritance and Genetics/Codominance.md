**Codominance** is a genetic scenario where **both alleles** of a gene are fully expressed in the phenotype. It's like a partnership where both partners speak at the same time, rather than one dominating the other or both compromising.

---

## 1. The Simple Analogy (Paint)
To understand Codominance, compare it to mixing paint:

*   **Complete Dominance (The Bully):** Red Paint + White Paint = **RED** (Red covers the white completely).
*   **Incomplete Dominance (The Blender):** Red Paint + White Paint = **PINK** (They mix to make a new intermediate color).
*   **Codominance (The Co-Stars):** Red Paint + White Paint = **Red & White Stripes/Spots**. (You see *distinct* Red patches and *distinct* White patches).

> **Key Rule:** In Codominance, you see **BOTH** traits distinctly. No blending.

## 2. Detailed Examples

### A. Human Blood Types (The Classic Example)
This is the most important biological example.
*   **Alleles:**
    *   $I^A$ (Codes for Antigen A)
    *   $I^B$ (Codes for Antigen B)
    *   $i$ (Codes for NO antigen - Recessive)

*   **The Codominant Scenario:**
    *   If you inherit $I^A$ from Mom and $I^B$ from Dad, your genotype is $I^A I^B$.
    *   Your cells don't pick one. They don't mix them.
    *   **Result:** Your Red Blood Cells have **BOTH** Antigen A **AND** Antigen B sticking out of their surface.
    *   **Phenotype:** Blood Group AB.

### B. Roan Cows (The Visual Example)
*   **Parents:** A Red Cow ($C^R C^R$) mates with a White Cow ($C^W C^W$).
*   **Offspring ($C^R C^W$):** They are **Roan**.
*   **What is Roan?** It looks pinkish from a distance, but if you look close up, the cow has **individual Red hairs** and **individual White hairs** side-by-side.
    *   It is **NOT** a pink hair.
    *   It is **Red + White** acting together.

### C. Sickle Cell Trait (The Molecular Example)
*   **HbA** = Normal Haemoglobin.
*   **HbS** = Sickle Haemoglobin.
*   **Heterozygote ($HbA HbS$):**
    *   They have **Sickle Cell Trait**.
    *   Their blood contains **both** normal round red blood cells **and** some sickle-shaped cells (especially under low oxygen).
    *   They are resistant to Malaria (an evolutionary advantage!).

## 3. Comparison: The "Big Three"

| Feature | Complete Dominance | Incomplete Dominance | Codominance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Heterozygote Look** | Looks exactly like the Dominant parent. | Looks like a blend/mix of both parents. | Shows distinct traits of **BOTH** parents. |
| **Flower Example** | Red x White = **Red** | Red x White = **Pink** | Red x White = **Red & White Spots** |
| **Notation** | $A$ (Big) and $a$ (Small) | $C^R$ and $C^W$ (Both capital) | $I^A$ and $I^B$ (Both capital) |

## 5. Common Misconception: "Is an allele always Dominant?"
**No.** Dominance is a **relationship** between two alleles, not a fixed personality trait of the gene.

**Look at Blood Type A ($I^A$):**
1.  **vs Type O ($i$):** $I^A$ acts **Dominant** (It bullies $i$). ($I^A i$ = Type A).
2.  **vs Type B ($I^B$):** $I^A$ acts **Codominant** (It shares with $I^B$). ($I^A I^B$ = Type AB).

So, you can't just say "$I^A$ is a dominant allele." You have to ask: "**Dominant to what?**"
- In Codominance, we specifically have **two alleles that are equally strong** when paired together.

## 6. Flashcard Q&A

### Q) If a flower shows Codominance, and you cross a Blue parent with a Yellow parent, what does the offspring look like?
**Model Answer:**
The offspring would be spotted or striped with **both Blue and Yellow** patches. (If it were Green, that would be *Incomplete* Dominance).

### Q) Why is the notation for Codominance written with superscripts (e.g., $C^W C^R$)?
**Model Answer:**
Because neither allele is recessive, we cannot use a lowercase letter (like 'r'). We use a capital letter to represent the Gene (e.g., C for Color) and a superscript capital letter to represent the specific Allele (R for Red, W for White) to show they are equals.

### Q) A man with Type A blood (heterozygous) marries a woman with Type B blood (heterozygous). What is the chance of a Type AB child?
**Model Answer:**
*   Man: $I^A i$
*   Woman: $I^B i$
*   Punnett Square results:
    *   $I^A I^B$ (Type AB) - 25%
    *   $I^A i$ (Type A) - 25%
    *   $I^B i$ (Type B) - 25%
    *   $ii$ (Type O) - 25%
*   **Answer:** 25% chance.
