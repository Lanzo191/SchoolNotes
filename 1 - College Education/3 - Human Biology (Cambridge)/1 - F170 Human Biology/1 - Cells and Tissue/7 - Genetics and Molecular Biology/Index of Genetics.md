This **Map of Content (MOC)** is your central command center for Molecular Biology. It bridges the gap between the storage of genetic information and the physical expression of traits.

---

## 1 - DNA Structure and Function
> **Key Concepts:** The stability of the double helix and the high-fidelity nature of replication are the foundations of heredity. Understanding how DNA is packaged into chromatin explains how 2 meters of code fits into a microscopic nucleus.

- [[DNA]] – Chemical composition and anti-parallel orientation.
- [[DNA Replication]] – The semi-conservative mechanism and enzymatic control.
- [[Chromatin and [[Chromosomes]] – Histones, nucleosomes, and levels of compaction.
- [[DNA Repair Mechanism]] – How the cell prevents the accumulation of damage.

##  2 - Gene Expression
> **Key Concepts:** This folder tracks the "Central Dogma." It details how the cell "reads" the DNA master copy and creates mobile RNA versions to transport information to the cytoplasm.

- [[Transcription]] – RNA Polymerase and the creation of pre-mRNA.
- [[RNA]] – How introns are removed and exons are joined.
- [[Transcription]] – The ribosome-led process of building polypeptides.
- [[Gene Regulation]] – How cells switch genes 'on' or 'off' via transcription factors.
- [[mRNA]] – The transient genetic template.
- [[tRNA]] – The molecular adapter for amino acids.
- [[rRNA]] – The structural and catalytic core of the ribosome.

##  3 - Protein Synthesis
> **Key Concepts:** Translation is only the beginning. This section focuses on the "grammar" of the genetic code and how raw chains of amino acids are folded and modified into functional 3D machines.

- [[Protein Synthesis]] – A unified look at the flow of info.
- [[Genetic Code]] – Redundancy, universality, and triplet logic.
- [[Post-Translational Modifications]] – Phosphorylation, glycosylation, and folding.

##  4 - Mutations and Genetic Variation
> **Key Concepts:** Variation is the engine of evolution. Here we explore how "mistakes" in the code lead to diversity-from single-base changes to the curious phenomenon of animals repeatedly evolving into crabs.

- [[Types of Genetic Mutation]] – Point mutations vs. large-scale frameshifts.
- [[Genetic Variation]] – Shuffling the deck through meiosis and crossing over.
- [[Mutation Effects]] – The spectrum from deleterious to beneficial traits.
- [[Islandification]] – Why species change size on islands.
- [[Evolutionary Pressures and Adaptation]] – Natural selection in action.
- [[Carcinisation (Crabification)]] – The efficiency of the crab body plan.

##  5 - Inheritance and Genetics
> **Key Concepts:** This is the mathematical side of biology. By using Mendel’s laws and pedigree charts, we can predict the probability of traits appearing in future generations.

- [[Mendelian Genetics]] – Dominance, segregation, and independent assortment.
- [[Sex Linkage]] – Why some traits are linked to the X and Y chromosomes.
- [[Pedigree Analysis]] – Mapping family trees to identify inheritance patterns.

##  6 - Biotechnology
> **Key Concepts:** The "How-To" of modern science. This folder covers the tools humans use to amplify, read, and manipulate the genetic code for medicine and forensics.

- [[PCR and DNA Amplification]] – The thermal cycler and Taq polymerase.
- [[DNA Sequencing and Bioinformatics]] – Sanger sequencing and digital analysis.
