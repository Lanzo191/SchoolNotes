## Overview
**Prokaryotic cells** are single-celled organisms, such as **bacteria** and archaea, that lack a **nucleus** and other membrane-bound organelles. They are significantly smaller and simpler than eukaryotic cells, yet they are highly successful and diverse.

---

## General Features
- **Size:** Much smaller than eukaryotic cells (typically **0.1 to 5 µm**).
- **Nucleus:** Absent; the genetic material is free-floating in the cytoplasm.
- **DNA:** A single circular chromosome, not associated with **histone** proteins.
- **Ribosomes:** Smaller than eukaryotic ribosomes (**70S** vs. 80S).
- **Cell Wall:** Almost always present; composed of **murein (peptidoglycan)**.
- **Organelles:** No mitochondria, ER, or Golgi apparatus.

---

## Prokaryotic Structures and Functions

| Structure | Description | Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Plasma Membrane** | Phospholipid bilayer (no sterols). | Controls the entry and exit of substances. |
| **Cell Wall** | Made of **murein (peptidoglycan)**. | Provides structural support; prevents osmotic lysis. |
| **Capsule (Slime Layer)** | Thick polysaccharide layer outside the wall. | Protects against desiccation and phagocytosis; aids adhesion. |
| **Cytoplasm** | Jelly-like substance containing enzymes. | Site of metabolic reactions. |
| **DNA (Nucleoid)** | Single circular molecule. | Carries the genetic blueprint of the cell. |
| **Plasmids** | Small extra loops of **DNA**. | Carry extra genes, such as **antibiotic resistance**. |
| **Ribosomes (70S)** | Smaller protein-making factories. | Site of **protein synthesis**. |
| **Flagella** | Rotating protein filaments. | Provide **motility** (movement). |
| **Pili (Fimbriae)** | Hair-like appendages. | Used for **attachment** and **conjugation**. |

---

## Comparison: Prokaryotes vs. Eukaryotes
*For a more detailed comparison, see [[Prokaryote vs Eukaryote]].*

- **Genetic Material:** Prokaryotes have circular DNA; eukaryotes have linear DNA in a nucleus.
- **Reproduction:** Prokaryotes use **binary fission**; eukaryotes use **mitosis** and **meiosis**.
- **Metabolism:** Prokaryotes lack mitochondria and use the plasma membrane for energy production.
