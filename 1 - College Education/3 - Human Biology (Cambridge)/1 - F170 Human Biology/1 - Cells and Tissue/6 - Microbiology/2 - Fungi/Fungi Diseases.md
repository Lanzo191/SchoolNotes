**Aspergillosis:** A respiratory infection caused by inhaling Aspergillus spores, often in immunocompromised individuals. - Aspergillus - include cough (which may bring up blood), shortness of breath, fever, and chest pain. People with weakened immune systems or existing lung conditions are at higher risk for more severe symptoms.
Treatment for aspergillosis varies based on the type of infection. Options may include anti-fungal medications, corticosteroids, and in some cases, surgery to remove fungal masses.
