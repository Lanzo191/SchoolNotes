## Overview
Bacteria can be classified using several criteria, including their physical **shape**, their **Gram staining** characteristics, and their **oxygen requirements**.

---

## Classification by Shape (Morphology)
Bacterial shapes are determined by the **murein (peptidoglycan)** cell wall and can be used for initial identification.

| Shape | Description | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Coccus (pl. Cocci)** | Spherical or round cells. | *Staphylococcus aureus* |
| **Bacillus (pl. Bacilli)** | Rod-shaped cells. | *Escherichia coli* |
| **Vibrio** | Curved or comma-shaped cells. | *Vibrio cholerae* |
| **Spirillum (pl. Spirilla)** | Rigid, spiral-shaped cells. | *Spirillum minus* |
| **Spirochaete** | Flexible, corkscrew-shaped cells. | *Treponema pallidum* |

---

## Classification by Arrangement (Grouping)
How bacteria remain attached after **binary fission** determines their arrangement:

- **Diplo-:** Arranged in pairs (e.g., *Diplococci*).
- **Strepto-:** Arranged in chains (e.g., *Streptococci*).
- **Staphylo-:** Arranged in grape-like clusters (e.g., *Staphylococci*).
- **Tetrads:** Arranged in groups of four.
- **Sarcina:** Arranged in cube-like packets of eight.

---

## Other Classification Criteria

### Gram Staining
- **Gram-positive:** Retains purple dye due to a thick murein layer.
- **Gram-negative:** Loses purple dye and takes up red counterstain due to a thin murein layer and outer lipid membrane.
- *See [[Gram Staining]] for more detail.*

### Oxygen Requirements
- **Obligate Aerobes:** Require oxygen.
- **Obligate Anaerobes:** Oxygen is toxic.
- **Facultative Anaerobes:** Can live with or without oxygen.
- *See [[Anaerobic and Aerobic Bacteria]] for more detail.*
