## Overview
Control of microorganisms is essential in medical and laboratory settings to prevent the spread of **pathogens** and contamination of cultures. This is achieved through three main levels of decontamination.

---

## Levels of Decontamination

| Method | Definition | Goal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sterilisation** | The complete destruction of all living organisms, including **bacterial spores**. | Total absence of viable microbes. |
| **Disinfection** | The reduction of the number of pathogenic organisms on **inanimate objects** (non-living). | Safe level for human contact. |
| **Antisepsis** | The reduction of pathogens on **living tissues** (e.g., skin). | Prevent infection of wounds or surgical sites. |

---

## Physical Methods of Sterilisation

### Heat
- **Dry Heat (Oven):** Used for glass and metal at 160°C for 2 hours.
- **Moist Heat (Autoclave):** The most effective method. Uses steam under high pressure to reach **121°C** for 15 minutes. This destroys even the most heat-resistant **spores**.
- **Incineration (Flaming):** Used for inoculating loops during **aseptic technique**.

### Radiation
- **UV Light:** Causes DNA damage (thymine dimers). Used for sterilising laboratory surfaces.
- **Ionising Radiation (Gamma Rays):** High-energy rays that penetrate deep into materials. Used for sterilising pre-packaged medical equipment (e.g., syringes, catheters).

### Filtration
- Using filters with tiny pores (e.g., **HEPA filters**) to physically remove microbes from gases or heat-sensitive liquids.

---

## Chemical Methods (Disinfectants and Antiseptics)

| Chemical | Use | Mechanism |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Alcohols (e.g., Ethanol)** | Antiseptic (hand rub) and disinfectant. | Denatures proteins and dissolves lipid membranes. |
| **Chlorine (Bleach)** | Strong disinfectant for surfaces. | Oxidises biological molecules. |
| **Iodine (Betadine)** | Common antiseptic for skin preparation. | Inhibits protein synthesis and disrupts cell membranes. |
| **Phenolics** | Disinfectant for drains and surfaces. | Disrupts cell walls and membranes. |
