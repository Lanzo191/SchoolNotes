## Overview
**Binary fission** is the primary method of **asexual reproduction** used by **prokaryotic cells** (bacteria and archaea). It results in the production of two genetically identical daughter cells from a single parent cell.

---

## Steps of Binary Fission

1. **Replication of DNA:** The single circular chromosome and any **plasmids** are replicated. Each copy of the DNA attaches to a different part of the plasma membrane.
2. **Cell Elongation:** The cell grows in size, and the two DNA molecules are pulled to opposite ends (poles) of the cell.
3. **Formation of a Septum:** The plasma membrane and the **murein (peptidoglycan)** cell wall begin to grow inwards, forming a division wall called a **septum**.
4. **Division:** The septum is completed, and the cytoplasm is divided equally (cytokinesis). The parent cell splits into two identical **daughter cells**.

---

## Generation Time and Exponential Growth
The time it takes for a bacterial population to double in size is called the **generation time**.
- Under optimal conditions (plenty of nutrients, correct temperature, etc.), some bacteria like *E. coli* can divide every **20 minutes**.
- This leads to **exponential growth**, where the number of cells increases rapidly: 1 → 2 → 4 → 8 → 16 → 32, and so on.

### The Growth Curve Phases
When grown in a closed environment, bacteria follow a specific population growth curve:
1. **Lag Phase:** Slow growth as cells adjust to their new environment.
2. **Log (Exponential) Phase:** Rapid division under ideal conditions.
3. **Stationary Phase:** Rate of death equals the rate of new cell production due to nutrient depletion and toxin buildup.
4. **Death (Decline) Phase:** The population crashes as resources are exhausted and the environment becomes toxic.

---

## Horizontal Gene Transfer (Genetic Variation)
Since binary fission produces identical clones, bacteria use other methods to gain genetic variation:
- **Transformation:** Taking up DNA fragments from the environment.
- **Transduction:** DNA transfer via a **virus** (bacteriophage).
- **Conjugation:** Transfer of **plasmids** between two bacteria through a **pilus**.
