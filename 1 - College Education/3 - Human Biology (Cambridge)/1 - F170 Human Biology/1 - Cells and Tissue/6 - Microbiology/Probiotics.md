## Overview
**Probiotics** are live microorganisms (usually bacteria or yeasts) that provide health benefits to the host when consumed in adequate amounts. They are often called "good" or "friendly" bacteria because they help restore and maintain a healthy balance in the **microbiome**.

---

## Common Types of Probiotics

| Type | Examples | Use |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Lactic Acid Bacteria** | *Lactobacillus acidophilus*, *Bifidobacterium bifidum*. | Maintaining gut health and vaginal health. |
| **Spore-forming Bacteria** | *Bacillus coagulans*. | Survives extreme environments (like stomach acid). |
| **Yeast** | *Saccharomyces boulardii*. | Effective against antibiotic-associated diarrhoea. |

---

## Mechanism of Action
Probiotics work through several mechanisms to improve health:
- **Colonisation Resistance:** They compete with **pathogens** for nutrients and binding sites on the **mucous membrane**.
- **Antimicrobial Production:** Some produce substances like lactic acid or bacteriocins that inhibit the growth of harmful bacteria.
- **Immune Modulation:** They interact with the host's immune system to regulate inflammation and strengthen the gut barrier.
- **Enzymatic Activity:** Some help digest certain nutrients, such as lactose.

---

## Benefits of Probiotics
1. **Digestive Health:** Can help manage symptoms of **IBS (Irritable Bowel Syndrome)** and prevent **diarrhoea** caused by antibiotics or travel.
2. **Restoring Balance:** Helps restore the microbiome after a course of **antibiotics** that may have caused **dysbiosis**.
3. **Immune Support:** May reduce the risk and duration of certain infections.
4. **Specific Conditions:** Some strains are used to treat **Bacterial Vaginosis (BV)** or **Candidiasis (Thrush)**.

---

## Key Considerations
- **Strain Specificity:** Not all probiotics are the same. A specific **strain** (e.g., *Lactobacillus rhamnosus GG*) may be effective for one condition but not another.
- **Viability:** To be effective, the bacteria must be **live** when they reach the large intestine.
- **Dosage:** Measured in **CFU (Colony Forming Units)**. Dosages often need to be in the billions to be effective.
