nterphase is a crucial part of the cell cycle where the cell prepares for division. It consists of three distinct phases: G1, S, and G2.

### Phases of Interphase

|Phase|Description|
|---|---|
|**G1 Phase (First Gap)**|The cell grows and synthesizes [[Proteins]] and organelles. It accumulates the necessary building blocks for [[DNA]] replication.|
|**S Phase (Synthesis)**|[[DNA]] replication occurs, resulting in the formation of sister chromatids. Each chromosome is duplicated, ensuring that both daughter cells will receive identical genetic material.|
|**G2 Phase (Second Gap)**|The cell continues to grow and prepares for [[Mitosis]]. It synthesizes [[Proteins]] required for chromosome manipulation and duplicates some organelles.|

### Importance of Interphase

- **Growth**: Cells increase in size and mass.
- **[[DNA]] Replication**: Ensures genetic material is accurately copied for cell division.
- **Preparation for [[Mitosis]]**: The cell organizes its components for the upcoming division.

Interphase is essential for the proper functioning and replication of cells, allowing multicellular organisms to grow, repair tissues, and maintain homeostasis.
