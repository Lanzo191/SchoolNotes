In **[[Meiosis]] Metaphase II**, individual [[Chromosomes]] align at the equator of the two haploid cells.

## Key Events
1. **Alignment**: Individual [[Chromosomes]] (consisting of two sister chromatids) line up along the **metaphase plate** (equator).
2. **Spindle Attachment**: Spindle fibers from opposite poles attach to the **centromere** of each chromosome.
3. **Variation**: Due to crossing over in Prophase I, the sister chromatids are no longer identical. Their random orientation in this stage contributes to further **[[Genetic Variation]]**.
