**[[Meiosis]] Prophase I** is the first and longest stage of [[Meiosis]] I. It is distinct from [[Mitosis]] Prophase because it involves the pairing of homologous [[Chromosomes]] and genetic recombination.

## Key Events
1. **Chromatin Condensation**: [[Chromosomes]] become visible as they shorten and thicken.
2. **Synapsis**: **Homologous [[Chromosomes]]** pair up to form **bivalents** (or tetrads).
3. **Crossing Over**: Non-sister chromatids wrap around each other at points called **chiasmata**. They exchange sections of [[DNA]], resulting in new combinations of alleles (**recombinant chromatids**).
4. **Nuclear Envelope Breakdown**: The nuclear membrane fragments and the nucleolus disappears.
5. **Spindle Formation**: Centrioles move to opposite poles and begin producing spindle fibers.

---

## Significance
- **Genetic Variation**: Crossing over is the first source of genetic variation in meiosis, ensuring that daughter cells are genetically unique.
