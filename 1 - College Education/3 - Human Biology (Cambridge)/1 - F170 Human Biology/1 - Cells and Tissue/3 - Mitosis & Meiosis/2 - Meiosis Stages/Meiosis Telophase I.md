**[[Meiosis]] Telophase I** marks the end of the first division. It is usually followed immediately by **[[Cytokinesis]]**.

## Key Events
1. **Arrival at Poles**: The haploid sets of [[Chromosomes]] reach opposite poles.
2. **Nuclear Reform**: In some species, the nuclear envelope reforms and [[Chromosomes]] decondense (in others, cells go straight into [[Meiosis]] II).
3. **[[Cytokinesis]]**: The cytoplasm divides, resulting in **two haploid daughter cells**.

---

## Result
Two haploid (n) cells, each containing one chromosome from each homologous pair. Because of crossing over and independent assortment, these two cells are **genetically non-identical**.
