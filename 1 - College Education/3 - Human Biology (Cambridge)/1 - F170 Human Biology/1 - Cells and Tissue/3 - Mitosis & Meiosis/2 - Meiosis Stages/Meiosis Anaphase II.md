In **[[Meiosis]] Anaphase II**, the sister chromatids are finally separated.

## Key Events
1. **Centromere Division**: The **centromeres divide**, allowing the two sister chromatids to separate.
2. **Chromatid Migration**: Spindle fibers shorten, pulling the **sister chromatids** (now called individual [[Chromosomes]]) to opposite poles.

---

## Significance
- This stage is very similar to Anaphase in [[Mitosis]], but it occurs in haploid cells.
