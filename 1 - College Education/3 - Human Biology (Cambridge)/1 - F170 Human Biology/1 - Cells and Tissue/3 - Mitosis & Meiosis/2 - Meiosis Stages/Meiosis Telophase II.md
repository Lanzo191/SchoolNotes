**[[Meiosis]] Telophase II** is the final stage of [[Meiosis]], leading to the formation of four distinct gametes.

## Key Events
1. **Arrival at Poles**: [[Chromosomes]] reach the poles and begin to decondense into chromatin.
2. **Nuclear Reform**: Nuclear envelopes reform around the four sets of haploid daughter nuclei.
3. **[[Cytokinesis]]**: The cytoplasm of both cells divides.

---

## Final Result
- **Four haploid daughter cells** (gametes).
- Each cell is **genetically unique** due to crossing over and independent assortment.
- In males, these become four sperm cells; in females, one becomes a functional egg (ovum) and the others become polar bodies.
