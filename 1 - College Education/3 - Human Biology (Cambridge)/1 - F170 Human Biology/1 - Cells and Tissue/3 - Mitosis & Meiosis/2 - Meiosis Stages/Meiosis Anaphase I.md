In **[[Meiosis]] Anaphase I**, the homologous [[Chromosomes]] are separated and pulled to opposite poles.

## Key Events
1. **Separation of Bivalents**: Spindle fibers shorten, pulling the **homologous [[Chromosomes]]** apart.
2. **Chiasmata Separate**: The connections between non-sister chromatids break.
3. **Sister Chromatids Stay Together**: Unlike in [[Mitosis]], the **centromeres do not divide** yet. The sister chromatids remain attached and move as a single unit to the poles.

---

## Result
Each pole now has a **haploid** set of chromosomes (n), but each chromosome still consists of two sister chromatids.
