**[[Meiosis]] Prophase II** is the beginning of the second division. It is much simpler and faster than Prophase I.

## Key Events
1. **[[Chromosomes]] Condense**: If [[Chromosomes]] decondensed in Telophase I, they thicken and become visible again.
2. **Nuclear Envelope Breakdown**: The nuclear membrane (if reformed) fragments again.
3. **Spindle Formation**: A new spindle apparatus forms at right angles to the old spindle from [[Meiosis]] I.
4. **No Crossing Over**: There is no pairing of homologous [[Chromosomes]] and **no crossing over** in this stage.
