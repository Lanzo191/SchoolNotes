[[Mitosis]] is a type of cell division that occurs in eukaryotic cells (all somatic (non-reproductive) cells in the body, such as skin cells, blood cells, and muscle cells). It results in the formation of two identical daughter (Diploid) cells from a single parent cell. used for growth, tissue repair, and asexual reproduction

- Prior to everything there is the active portion of the cell cycle which is called [[Interphase]]

- The first stage is **[[ Mitosis Prophase]]**: [[DNA]] condenses into visible [[Chromosomes]] and the nuclear envelope breaks down.
  
- The second stage is [[Mitosis]] Prometaphase]]: which is where the nuclear envelope breaks down, and [[Chromosomes]] become attached to spindle fibres via kinetochores  this is followed by **[[Mitosis]] Metaphase]]**: Individual [[Chromosomes]] line up at the equator; spindles attach to centromeres.
  
- The third stage is **[[Mitosis Anaphase]]**: Centromeres divide and sister chromatids are pulled to opposite poles.
  
- The fourth stage is **[[Mitosis Telophase]]: Nuclear membranes reform around two identical sets of [[DNA]]; [[Cytokinesis]] occurs.

- The final process (Not a stage) is [[Cytokinesis]]: cytoplasm of a single cell divides to form two daughter cells
