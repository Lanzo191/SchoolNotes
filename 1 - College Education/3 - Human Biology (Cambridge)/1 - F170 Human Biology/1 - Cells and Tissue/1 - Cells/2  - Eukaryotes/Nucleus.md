#Biology #CellBiology #EukaryoticCells #Organelle  

The **nucleus** is the control centre of the eukaryotic cell, containing nearly all of the cell’s genetic material. It regulates gene expression, cell division, and ribosome synthesis. The nucleus is one of the most essential organelles within eukaryotic cells, maintaining the integrity of [[DNA]] and controlling cellular activity through [[Transcription]].

## Structure
- **Nuclear Envelope**: A double membrane composed of an inner and outer membrane that encloses the nucleus and separates it from the cytoplasm. The outer membrane is continuous with the rough endoplasmic reticulum and may have [[Ribosomes]] attached. It contains numerous nuclear pores which control the exchange of substances such as [[mRNA]], [[Nucleotides]], ions, and regulatory [[Proteins]] between the nucleus and cytoplasm. 

- **Nucleoplasm (Nuclear Sap)**: The semi-fluid medium filling the nucleus, rich in ions, enzymes, [[Nucleotides]], and chromatin. It provides a medium for diffusion and supports the structures within the nucleus.  

- **Chromatin**: A complex of [[DNA]] and histone [[Proteins]] that makes up the cell’s [[Chromosomes]]. Exists as euchromatin (loosely packed, transcriptionally active) and heterochromatin (densely packed, inactive). During [[Mitosis]], chromatin condenses to form distinct [[Chromosomes]].  

- **Nucleolus**: A dense, spherical region responsible for synthesising ribosomal [[RNA]] ([[rRNA]]) and assembling ribosomal subunits from [[rRNA]] and [[Proteins]]. These subunits are later exported through the nuclear pores into the cytoplasm.

## Function
- **Genetic Information Storage**: Houses [[DNA]] that carries all hereditary instructions necessary for cell survival, growth, and reproduction.  

**[[Transcription]] and Gene Expression**: 
- Acts as the site of [[Transcription]], where [[DNA]] is transcribed into [[mRNA]] which exits via nuclear pores to be translated by [[Ribosomes]].  

 **Ribosome Assembly**: 
 - The nucleolus manufactures and assembles [[rRNA]] into ribosomal subunits that later combine into complete [[Ribosomes]] in the cytoplasm.  
 
 **Regulation of Cell Activities**: 
 - Directs cellular metabolism by regulating which [[Genes]] are expressed and when.  
 
 **Replication**: 
 - Before cell division, [[DNA]] within the nucleus is replicated to ensure accurate genetic inheritance.
