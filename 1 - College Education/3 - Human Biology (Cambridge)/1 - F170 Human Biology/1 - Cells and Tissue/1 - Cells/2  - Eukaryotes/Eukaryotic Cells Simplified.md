Prokaryotes are simple, unicellular organisms such as **bacteria** and **archaea**. They lack a [[Nucleus]] and [[Cell Membranes|Membrane Bound]] organelles, but still carry out all essential life processes.  
## 1. Nucleus
- **Structure**: Surrounded by a double [[Cell Membranes]] (nuclear envelope) with pores; contains chromatin ([[DNA]] + [[Proteins]]) and a nucleolus.  
- **Function**: Stores genetic material ([[DNA]]); controls cell activities (gene expression); nucleolus makes ribosomal [[RNA]] and assembles [[Ribosomes]].  

## 2. Mitochondria
- **Structure**: Double membrane; inner membrane folded into cristae; matrix contains enzymes, [[DNA]], and [[Ribosomes]].  
- **Function**: Site of aerobic respiration → [[ATP]] production.  


## 4. Rough Endoplasmic Reticulum (RER)
- **Structure**: Flattened sacs of membrane with [[Ribosomes]] attached.  
- **Function**: [[Protein Synthesis]] (on [[Ribosomes]]) and modification; transport of [[Proteins]] to Golgi.  

## 5. Smooth Endoplasmic Reticulum (SER)
- **Structure**: Network of membrane tubules, no [[Ribosomes]].  
- **Function**: Lipid and steroid synthesis; detoxification of harmful substances.  

## 6. Golgi Apparatus
- **Structure**: Stack of flattened membrane sacs (cisternae).  
- **Function**: Modifies [[Proteins]] (e.g., adds [[Carbohydrates]] → glycoproteins), packages into vesicles for secretion or use inside the cell.  

## 7. Ribosomes
- **Structure**: Small, no membrane; made of [[rRNA]] + [[Proteins]]; 80S in eukaryotes, 70S in prokaryotes and [[Mitochondria]]/chloroplasts.  
- **Function**: [[Protein Synthesis]].  

## 8. Lysosomes
- **Structure**: Spherical vesicles containing digestive enzymes (hydrolases).  
- **Function**: Breakdown of waste, old organelles, pathogens (via phagocytosis).  

## 9. Plasma Membrane
- **Structure**: Phospholipid bilayer with [[Proteins]], cholesterol, glycoproteins, glycolipids (fluid mosaic model).  
- **Function**: Selectively permeable barrier; regulates entry/exit of substances; involved in [[Cell Signalling]].  

## 10. Cytoskeleton
- **Structure**: Network of microfilaments (actin), microtubules (tubulin), and intermediate filaments.  
- **Function**: Maintains cell shape, provides mechanical support, transport of organelles, enables movement (e.g., cilia, flagella).  

## 11. Centrioles 
- **Structure**: Cylindrical structures made of microtubules, usually in pairs (centrosome).  
- **Function**: Organise spindle fibres in cell division.  

## 12. Cell Wall (plants, fungi, prokaryotes)
- **Structure**: Plants = cellulose, fungi = chitin, bacteria = peptidoglycan.  
- **Function**: Provides structural support and prevents osmotic lysis.  

## 13. Vacuole (large permanent in plants)
- **Structure**: Fluid-filled sac surrounded by tonoplast.  
- **Function**: Stores water, ions, and substances; maintains turgor pressure in plant cells.  

## 14. Plasmodesmata (plants only)
- **Structure**: Pores in the cell wall lined with plasma membrane, containing cytoplasmic strands.  
- **Function**: Allows transport and communication between plant cells.  

## 15. Cilia and Flagella
- **Structure**: Extensions of plasma membrane with 9+2 arrangement of microtubules.  
- **Function**: Movement of cells (e.g., sperm) or movement of substances (e.g., mucus in [[Trachea]]).

## Differences Between Prokaryotic and Eukaryotic Cells

| Feature      | Prokaryotes                 | Eukaryotes                                         |
| ------------ | --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Size         | [[Microscopy\|0.1–5 µm]]    | [[Microscopy\|10–100 µm]]                          |
| [[DNA]]          | Circular, free in cytoplasm | Linear, inside [[Nucleus]]                             |
| Histones     | Absent                      | Present                                            |
| Organelles   | None membrane-bound         | Many membrane-bound                                |
| [[Ribosomes]]    | 70S                         | 80S                                                |
| Cell wall    | Murein                      | Cellulose (plants), chitin (fungi), none (animals) |
| Reproduction | Binary fission              | [[Mitosis]]/[[Meiosis]]                                    |
| Examples     | Bacteria, archaea           | Animals, plants, fungi, protists                   |
