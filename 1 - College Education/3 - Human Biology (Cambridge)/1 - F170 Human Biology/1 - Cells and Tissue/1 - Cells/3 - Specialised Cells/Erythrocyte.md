**Erythrocytes (red blood cells)** transport gases in the blood.

## Structure
- Biconcave disc shape increases surface area for gas exchange.  
- Lack [[Nucleus]] and organelles to maximise haemoglobin content.  
- Packed with haemoglobin for oxygen and carbon dioxide transport.  

## Functions
- Transport oxygen from [[Lungs]] to tissues.  
- Transport some carbon dioxide back to [[Lungs]].  
- Help maintain blood pH via buffering.
