**Lymphocytes** are white blood cells involved in adaptive [[Immunity]].

## Structure
- Large round [[Nucleus]] with minimal cytoplasm.  

## Functions
- **B cells:** produce antibodies.  
- **T helper cells:** coordinate immune response.  
- **Cytotoxic T cells:** destroy infected or cancerous cells.  
- Provide long-term immunological memory.
