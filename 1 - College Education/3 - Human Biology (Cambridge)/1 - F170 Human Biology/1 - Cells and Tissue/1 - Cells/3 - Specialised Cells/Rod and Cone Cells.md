**Photoreceptor cells** detect light and colour in the retina.

## Structure
- Outer segments contain photopigments (rhodopsin in rods, iodopsins in cones).  
- Synaptic terminals connect to bipolar cells.  

## Functions
- **Rods:** detect low light for black/white vision.  
- **Cones:** detect colour and provide high visual acuity.
