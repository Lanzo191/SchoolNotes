**Ciliated [[Epithelial cells]]** line airways and move mucus.

## Structure
- Lined with motile cilia.  
- Interspersed goblet cells secrete mucus.  

## Functions
- Move mucus containing dust or pathogens out of the [[Lungs]].  
- Protect the respiratory tract from infection.
