**Egg cells (ova)** are large female gametes adapted to support early embryo development.

## Structure
- Large cytoplasm containing nutrients for the zygote.  
- Protective **zona pellucida** glycoprotein layer.  
- Cortical granules to prevent polyspermy after [[Fertilisation]].  

## Functions
- Serve as the female gamete.  
- Provide nutrients for early embryo development.  
- Prevent multiple sperm from fertilising the egg.
