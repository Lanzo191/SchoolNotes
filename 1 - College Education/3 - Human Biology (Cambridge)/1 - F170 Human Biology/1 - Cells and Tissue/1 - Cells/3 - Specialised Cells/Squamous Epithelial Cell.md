**Squamous [[Epithelial cells]]** are thin, flat cells designed for diffusion.

## Structure
- Single-layered, flat cells (e.g., [[Alveoli]], [[Capillaries]]).  

## Functions
- Provide short diffusion pathways.  
- Allow rapid gas exchange.  
- Reduce friction in vessels (endothelium).
