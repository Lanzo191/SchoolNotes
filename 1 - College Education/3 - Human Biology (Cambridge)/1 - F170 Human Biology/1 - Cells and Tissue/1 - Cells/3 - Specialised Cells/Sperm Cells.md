**Sperm cells** are specialised for [[Fertilisation]] and delivering paternal [[DNA]] to the egg.

## Structure
- Flagellum for motility, allowing the cell to swim toward the ovum.  
- Midpiece packed with [[Mitochondria]] to provide [[ATP]] for movement.  
- Acrosome containing hydrolytic enzymes to penetrate the egg's zona pellucida.  
- Haploid [[Nucleus]] carrying paternal genetic material.  

## Functions
- Fertilise the egg by delivering [[DNA]].  
- Penetrate egg membranes to allow fusion.  
- Activate egg metabolism after [[Fertilisation]].
