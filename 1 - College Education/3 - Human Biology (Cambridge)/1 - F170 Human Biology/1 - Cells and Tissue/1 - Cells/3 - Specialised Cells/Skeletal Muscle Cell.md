**Skeletal (striated) muscle cells** are long, multinucleate fibres specialised for voluntary movement.

## Structure
- Long multinucleate fibres.  
- Striated with actin and myosin.  
- Many [[Mitochondria]] for [[ATP]] production.  

## Functions
- Voluntary contraction for movement.  
- Maintain posture and joint stability.  
- Generate heat during shivering.
