**Monocytes** are large white blood cells that differentiate into macrophages.

## Structure
- Largest WBC with [[Kidney]]-shaped [[Nucleus]].  
- Differentiate into macrophages once in tissues.  

## Functions
- Phagocytose pathogens and cellular debris.  
- Present antigens to lymphocytes.  
- Secrete cytokines to activate other immune cells.
