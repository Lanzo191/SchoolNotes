**Eosinophils** defend against parasitic infections and modulate allergic responses.

## Structure
- Bilobed [[Nucleus]].  
- Cytoplasmic granules containing toxic [[Proteins]].  

## Functions
- Attack parasitic worms.  
- Participate in allergic reactions and inflammation modulation.
