**Neurones** are [[Specialised cells]] for rapid electrical communication.

## Structure
- Dendrites receive signals.  
- Long axon transmits impulses.  
- Myelin sheath (in many neurones) and nodes of Ranvier speed conduction.  

## Functions
- Transmit electrical impulses throughout the body.  
- Integrate sensory, motor, and cognitive functions.  
- Enable rapid communication between distant tissues.
