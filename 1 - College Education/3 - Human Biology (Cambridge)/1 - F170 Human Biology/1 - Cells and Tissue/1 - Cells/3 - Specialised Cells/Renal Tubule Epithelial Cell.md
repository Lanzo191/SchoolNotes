**Renal tubule [[Epithelial cells]]** are specialised for selective reabsorption and secretion.

## Structure
- [[Microvilli]] (brush border) provide large surface area.  
- Many [[Mitochondria]] support [[Active Transport]].  

## Functions
- Reabsorb water, [[Glucose]], and ions.  
- Secrete waste products into filtrate.  
- Maintain acid–base and electrolyte balance.
