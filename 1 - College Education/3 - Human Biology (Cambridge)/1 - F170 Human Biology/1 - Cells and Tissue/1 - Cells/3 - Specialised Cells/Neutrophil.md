**Neutrophils** are phagocytic white blood cells part of innate [[Immunity]].

## Structure
- Multi-lobed [[Nucleus]].  
- Many [[Lysosomes]] containing hydrolytic enzymes.  

## Functions
- Engulf and digest bacteria and fungi.  
- Release cytokines to recruit additional immune cells.  
- Act as the first line of defence against infection.
