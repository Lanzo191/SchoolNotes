**Gastric pit [[Epithelial cells]]** line the [[Stomach]] and secrete substances essential for digestion.

## Structure
- Line [[Stomach]] mucosa, including specialised secretory cell types.  

## Functions
- **Mucous cells:** secrete mucus to protect lining.  
- **Chief cells:** secrete pepsinogen.  
- **Parietal cells:** secrete hydrochloric acid and intrinsic factor for digestion and B12 absorption.
