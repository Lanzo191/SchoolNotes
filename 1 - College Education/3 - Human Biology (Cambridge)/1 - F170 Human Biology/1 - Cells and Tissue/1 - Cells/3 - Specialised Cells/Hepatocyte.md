**Hepatocytes** are [[Liver]] cells specialised for metabolism and detoxification.

## Structure
- Polygonal shape with numerous [[Mitochondria]].  
- Extensive smooth endoplasmic reticulum.  
- Glycogen granules for energy storage.  

## Functions
- Metabolise [[Carbohydrates]], [[Proteins]], and lipids.  
- Detoxify harmful substances.  
- Produce bile.  
- Store glycogen, vitamins, and minerals.
