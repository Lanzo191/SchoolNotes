Antimicrobials 
antibiotics prevent bacteria from building cell walls, causing them to burst
antifungals target ergosterol (similar to cholesterol) causing leaky plasma membrane 
antivirals target your cell receptors so viruses cant bind to them 
antiprotozoals prevent protozoans from replicating which prevent them from reproducing


Examples
Tetracyclines such as Doxycycline, Minocycline.  
Common Uses: Acne, Lyme disease, and malaria prevention. 
Side Effects: Photosensitivity (easy sunburn), and they can cause permanent tooth discolouration if given to children under 8 or during pregnancy.
Minocycline works by stopping bacteria from making proteins they need to grow, which prevents their growth and can kill them
