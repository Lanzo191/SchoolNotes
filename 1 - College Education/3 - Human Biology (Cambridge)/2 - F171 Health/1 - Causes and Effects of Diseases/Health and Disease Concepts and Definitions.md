## Core Concepts of Health and Wellbeing
- **Health (WHO Definition)**: A state of complete physical, mental, and social well-being and not merely the absence of disease or infirmity.
- **Wellbeing**: A holistic state of being comfortable, healthy, or happy, encompassing subjective life satisfaction, physical vitality, and psychological stability.

## Dimensions of Health
- **Physical Health**: Optimal biological functioning of the body and its physiological systems, maintaining bodily integrity, adequate energy, and resilience to disease.
- **Mental Health**: A state of emotional and psychological well-being in which an individual can utilize cognitive abilities, cope with normal life stresses, and function within society.
- **Social Health**: The dimension of well-being derived from interpersonal relationships, community belonging, and positive social interaction.

## Clinical and Pathological Terminology

| Term | Operational Definition | Clinical Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Physiology** | The scientific study of normal biological functions and mechanical processes within living organisms and organ systems. | Cardiac muscle conduction, nephron filtration mechanisms. |
| **Disease** | An objective pathological impairment of bodily structure or function caused by infection, genetics, or environmental factors. | Atherosclerosis, Type 1 diabetes, Crohn's disease. |
| **Medical Disorder** | An abnormal functional state or condition affecting psychological or physiological processes without a uniform single cause. | Generalised anxiety disorder, cardiac arrhythmia. |
| **Medical Sign** | An objective, observable, and clinically measurable indication of medical pathology identified by a clinician. | Elevated blood pressure, localized edema, pulmonary crackles. |
| **Medical Symptom** | A subjective experience of illness reported directly by the patient that cannot be objectively quantified by examination. | Nausea, vertigo, fatigue, neuropathic pain. |
| **Medical Syndrome** | A recognized cluster of signs and symptoms that consistently co-occur to characterize a specific medical condition. | Metabolic syndrome, Cushing's syndrome. |
| **Medical Condition** | A broad umbrella term describing any acute or chronic disease, illness, disorder, or injury requiring clinical evaluation. | Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD). |

## Additional Clinical Considerations
- **Polypharmacy**: The concurrent administration of multiple prescription drugs (typically five or more) to a single patient, presenting elevated risks of adverse drug-drug interactions, toxicity, and compliance difficulties.
- **Hydatid Disease (Cystic Echinococcosis)**: A zoonotic parasitic disease caused by infection with larval stages of *Echinococcus granulosus* tapeworms, resulting in slow-growing, fluid-filled hydatid cysts primarily in the liver and lungs.
