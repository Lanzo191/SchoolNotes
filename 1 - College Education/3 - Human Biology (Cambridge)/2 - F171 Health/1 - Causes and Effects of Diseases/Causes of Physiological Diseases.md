## Pathological Causes of Physiological Diseases

| Body System | Physiological Disease | Aetiological Factors | Pathophysiological Mechanism |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nervous** | Motor Neurone Disease (MND) | Genetic mutations, oxidative stress, cellular aging | Progressive degeneration and apoptosis of upper and lower motor neurons, terminating voluntary muscular control. |
| **Nervous** | Parkinson's Disease | Genetic mutations, environmental toxins, aging | Degeneration of dopaminergic neurons in the substantia nigra, impairing motor regulation by the basal ganglia. |
| **Circulatory** | Abdominal Aortic Aneurysm | Atherosclerosis, hypertension, smoking, collagen defects | Elastin and collagen breakdown in the arterial tunica media, causing localized dilation and potential catastrophic rupture. |
| **Circulatory** | Hypertension | Chronic high dietary sodium, arterial stiffness, obesity | Sustained systemic vascular resistance forces excessive left ventricular workload and damages endothelial linings. |
| **Respiratory** | Asthma | Bronchial hyperresponsiveness, allergen exposure | IgE-mediated chronic airway inflammation, smooth muscle bronchospasm, and hypersecretion of thick mucus. |
| **Respiratory** | COPD (Emphysema / Bronchitis) | Long-term tobacco smoke, environmental particulate exposure | Protease-antiprotease imbalance destroys alveolar septa (emphysema) alongside chronic bronchial goblet cell hyperplasia. |
| **Digestive** | Crohn’s Disease | Autoimmune dysregulation, genetic susceptibility, microbiota | Transmural inflammatory response affecting any portion of the gastrointestinal tract, causing ulceration and strictures. |
| **Digestive** | Hiatus Hernia | Weakening of diaphragmatic phrenoesophageal ligament | Intra-abdominal pressure forces protrusion of the upper gastric fundus through the esophageal hiatus into the thoracic cavity. |
| **Urinary** | Nephritis (Glomerulonephritis) | Autoimmune complex deposition, streptococcal infection | Immune-complex mediated inflammation of renal glomeruli impairs selective filtration, causing hematuria and proteinuria. |
| **Urinary** | Polycystic Kidney Disease (PKD) | Autosomal dominant or recessive genetic mutations (PKD1/2) | Disrupted epithelial cell signaling triggers development of multiple fluid-filled cysts, compressing functional renal parenchyma. |
| **Musculoskeletal** | Multiple Sclerosis (MS) | Autoimmune attack, genetic risk, environmental factors | Auto-reactive T-cells target myelin basic protein, causing demyelination, plaque formation, and saltatory conduction failure. |
| **Musculoskeletal** | Rheumatoid Arthritis | Autoimmune response, anti-CCP antibodies, genetics | Persistent autoimmune synovitis with pannus tissue formation, resulting in cartilage destruction and bony erosion. |
| **Cancer** | Hodgkin's Lymphoma | Genetic aberrations, Epstein-Barr virus (EBV) | Malignant transformation of germinal centre B-cells into characteristic multi-nucleated Reed-Sternberg cells. |
| **Cancer** | Malignant Melanoma | Ultraviolet (UV) radiation, CDKN2A genetic mutations | UV-induced DNA thymine dimer formation in melanocytes triggers unchecked cellular proliferation and metastatic capacity. |
| **Nutritional Deficiency** | Anaemia (Iron / B12 / Folate) | Inadequate dietary intake, chronic gastrointestinal blood loss | Deficient iron impairs haem synthesis, or B12/folate deficiency halts erythrocyte nuclear maturation (macrocytic anaemia). |
| **Nutritional Deficiency** | Rickets / Osteomalacia | Inadequate sunlight exposure, dietary Vitamin D deficiency | Impaired calcitriol synthesis prevents intestinal calcium absorption, leading to defective bone matrix mineralization. |
| **Genetic** | Cystic Fibrosis | Recessive mutations in the CFTR gene (e.g. delta-F508) | Defective chloride channels impair epithelial water transport, creating thick secretions that obstruct pulmonary and pancreatic ducts. |
| **Genetic** | Sickle Cell Anaemia | Point mutation (Glu6Val) in the HBB beta-globin gene | Polymerization of abnormal HbS molecules under low oxygen tension deforms erythrocytes into rigid sickled shapes, causing vaso-occlusion. |
