
---

##  Definition
- Nutrients are **chemical substances** obtained from food that are used by the body for **energy, growth, repair, and regulation**.  
- Divided into:  
  - [[Carbohydrates]] 
  - [[Fats (Lipids)]] 
  - [[Proteins]]  
  - [[Sodium]]
  - [[Potassium]]
  - [[Calcium]]
  - [[Sugars]]

---

## Distinction
- **Macronutrients:** Needed in large amounts → carbohydrates, proteins, lipids, water.  
- **Micronutrients:** Needed in small amounts → vitamins and minerals.  

---

## Purposes
- Provide **energy** (e.g. [[Glucose]], fatty acids).  
- Provide **building blocks** for growth and repair (e.g. [[Amino Acids]]).  
- Regulate **metabolic processes** (e.g. vitamins as coenzymes, minerals as cofactors).
