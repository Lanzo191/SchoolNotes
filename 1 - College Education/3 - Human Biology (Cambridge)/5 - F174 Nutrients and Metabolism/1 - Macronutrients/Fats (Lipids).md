##  Overview
- Lipids are organic compounds made of **carbon (C), hydrogen (H), and oxygen (O)**, but with much less oxygen than [[Carbohydrates]].  
- Insoluble in water but soluble in organic solvents.  
- Main types:  
  - **Triglycerides** (fats and oils) → glycerol + 3 fatty acids.  
  - **Phospholipids** → glycerol + 2 fatty acids + phosphate group.  
  - **Steroids** (e.g. cholesterol, steroid hormones).  

---

##  Roles in the Body
- **Energy source & storage:**  
  - 1 g lipid = 37 kJ (more than double carbohydrates).  
  - Stored in adipose tissue for long-term energy.  
- **Insulation & protection:**  
  - Thermal insulation (blubber, adipose under skin).  
  - Protects organs (e.g. kidneys).  
- **Structural role:**  
  - Phospholipids → form cell membranes (phospholipid bilayer).  
  - Cholesterol → maintains membrane fluidity and stability.  
- **Hormones:**  
  - Steroid hormones (oestrogen, testosterone, cortisol).  
- **Other functions:**  
  - Myelin sheath around axons (insulation of nerve impulses).  
  - Source of fat-soluble vitamins (A, D, E, K).  

---

##  Clinical/Exam Links
- High saturated fat intake → increased LDL cholesterol → risk of atherosclerosis, heart disease.  
- Essential fatty acids (omega-3, omega-6) must come from diet.  
- Lipid digestion: emulsified by bile salts, digested by lipase → glycerol + fatty acids.
