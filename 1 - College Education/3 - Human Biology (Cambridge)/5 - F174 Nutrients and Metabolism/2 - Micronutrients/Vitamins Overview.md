**Vitamins** are organic compounds required in tiny amounts for essential metabolic reactions. They are not an energy source but are vital for health.

## Classification

### 1. Fat-Soluble Vitamins (A, D, E, K)
*   **Absorption:** Absorbed with dietary fats; require bile.
*   **Storage:** Stored in the [[Liver]] and adipose (fat) tissue.
*   **Risk:** Can build up to toxic levels (Hypervitaminosis) if consumed in excess.
*   **Deficiency:** Takes longer to develop as body has stores.

### 2. Water-Soluble Vitamins (B-Complex, C)
*   **Absorption:** Absorbed directly into the blood.
*   **Storage:** Not stored (except B12); excess is excreted in urine.
*   **Risk:** Low toxicity (hard to overdose).
*   **Deficiency:** Develops quickly; regular intake is required.

## Vitamin D Activation
Vitamin D is unique as it behaves like a hormone.
1.  **Skin:** UV light converts 7-dehydrocholesterol -> Cholecalciferol (Vit D3).
2.  **[[Liver]]:** Converts D3 -> 25-hydroxyvitamin D.
3.  **[[Kidney]]:** Converts 25-hydroxyvitamin D -> **Calcitriol** (Active form).
    *   *Function:* Increases uptake of [[Calcium]] from the gut.
