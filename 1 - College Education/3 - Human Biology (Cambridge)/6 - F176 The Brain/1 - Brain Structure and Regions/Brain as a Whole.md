## Overview of the Human Brain
- The brain serves as the central command centre of the human nervous system, integrating sensory input, coordinating motor output, and enabling consciousness, cognition, and emotion.
- Weighs approximately 1.3 to 1.4 kg in an adult human and contains an estimated 86 billion neurons interconnected by over 100 trillion synapses.
- Consists of the **Cerebrum**, the **Diencephalon**, the **Cerebellum**, and the **Brainstem**.

## Major Anatomical Divisions

| Division | Primary Structural Components | Core Physiological & Behavioural Functions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cerebrum** | Cerebral cortex (two hemispheres, four lobes) and deep basal nuclei | Conscious thought, voluntary motor initiation, perception, reasoning, language, and executive control. |
| **Diencephalon** | [[Thalamus]], [[Hypothalamus]], Epithalamus | Sensory gating, endocrine control, homeostatic regulation, and circadian rhythms. |
| **Limbic System** | [[Hippocampus]], [[Amygdala]], Cingulate Gyrus, Fornix | Emotional processing, threat appraisal, long-term memory encoding, and motivation. |
| **Cerebellum** | Cerebellar cortex, deep cerebellar nuclei, vermis | Fine motor coordination, balance, motor learning, and predictive error correction. |
| **Brainstem** | Midbrain, Pons, Medulla Oblongata | Autonomic cardiorespiratory control, cranial nerve nuclei, sleep-wake cycles, and ascending sensory pathways. |

## Cerebral Lobes Summary

| Lobe | Major Functional Cortices | Primary Functions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **[[Frontal Lobe]]** | [[Prefrontal Cortex]], [[Motor Cortex]], [[Broca’s Area]] | Planning, executive function, motor control, emotional regulation, and expressive speech. |
| **[[Parietal Lobe]]** | [[Somatosensory Cortex]], Posterior Parietal Cortex | Somatosensory processing, tactile perception, proprioception, and spatial manipulation. |
| **[[Temporal Lobe]]** | Primary Auditory Cortex, [[Wernicke’s Area]], [[Hippocampus]] | Auditory perception, speech comprehension, complex memory consolidation, and face recognition. |
| **[[Occipital Lobe]]** | Primary Visual Cortex (V1), Visual Association Cortices | Visual sensory processing (colour, shape, orientation, motion, and retinotopic mapping). |

## Protection and Homeostasis
- **Meninges**: Three protective connective tissue membranes (Dura mater, Arachnoid mater, Pia mater).
- **Cerebrospinal Fluid (CSF)**: Produced by the choroid plexus; cushions the brain against trauma and removes metabolic waste.
- **Blood-Brain Barrier (BBB)**: Specialized capillary endothelial cells with tight junctions and astrocyte foot processes regulating solute entry.

## Clinical Relevance
- **Stroke (Ischaemic / Haemorrhagic)**: Disruption of cerebral vascular supply causing focal neuronal death and neurological deficits.
- **Traumatic Brain Injury (TBI)**: Physical shear or concussive trauma causing diffuse axonal injury and cognitive impairment.
- **Neurodegeneration**: Pathological protein misfolding and neuronal death (e.g. Alzheimer's disease, Parkinson's disease).
