### Location
- Deep in the centre of the brain
- Connects the **left** and **right hemispheres**
- Located above the **[[Thalamus]]** and under the cortex

### Structure
- Largest **white matter** structure in the brain
- A thick band of nerve fibres (commissural fibres)
- Consists of approximately 200 million axons

### Functions
- Facilitates **inter-hemispheric communication**
- Allows information to be transferred between the left and right sides of the brain
- Coordinates motor, sensory, and cognitive performances between the hemispheres
- Enables the visual field from one eye to be processed by the opposite hemisphere

### Clinical Links
- **Split-Brain Patients:** Individuals who have had the corpus callosum severed (corpus callosotomy) to treat severe epilepsy.
- **Sperry’s Research:** Showed that without the corpus callosum, the two hemispheres function independently (e.g., objects seen in the left visual field cannot be named because the language centre is usually in the left hemisphere).
- **Agenesis of the Corpus Callosum:** A congenital disorder where the structure is partially or completely missing.
