#Education #NervousSystem #OCR #Psychology

## Location -
- Deep in the centre of the brain, atop the **[[Brainstem]]**.
- Consists of two symmetrical halves.

## Functions -
- **Sensory Relay Station:** Acts as a gateway, receiving all sensory input (except smell) and sending it to the appropriate part of the cortex for processing.
- **Filtering:** Filters out irrelevant sensory information to prevent the brain from being overwhelmed.
- **Consciousness:** Plays a key role in regulating alertness, sleep, and wakefulness.

## Clinical Links -
- **Damage:** Can lead to sensory loss, hallucinations, or even a permanent coma.
- **Schizophrenia:** Some theories suggest schizophrenia is linked to a malfunctioning thalamus that fails to filter out background noise or irrelevant thoughts.
