#Education #NervousSystem #OCR #Psychology

### Location
- Base of the brain, connecting spinal cord to cerebrum and [[Cerebellum]]  
- Composed of: midbrain, pons, medulla oblongata  

### Functions
- Controls autonomic functions: heart rate, breathing, blood pressure  
- Reflex centres: swallowing, vomiting, coughing, sneezing  
- Conduit for ascending sensory and descending motor pathways  
- Coordinates basic survival behaviours  

### Clinical Links
- Damage → life-threatening autonomic dysfunction  
- Stroke or trauma → motor/sensory deficits, cranial nerve impairments
