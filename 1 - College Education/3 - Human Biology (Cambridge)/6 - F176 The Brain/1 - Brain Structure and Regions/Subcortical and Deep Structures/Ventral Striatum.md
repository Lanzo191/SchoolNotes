#Education #NervousSystem #OCR #Psychology

## Location -
- Part of the **[[Basal Ganglia]]**.
- Located deep within the cerebral hemispheres.
- Key component: **[[Nucleus]] Accumbens**.

## Function -
- **Reward System:** The "Hot" system in the brain.
- **Motivation:** Drives behaviour towards rewards (food, sex, money, social approval).
- **Addiction:** Heavily involved in the [[Dopamine]] reward pathway.

## Link to Core Studies -
*   **[[Casey et al. (2011)]]:**
    *   This region was **more active** in "Low Delay" participants (those who couldn't wait for the marshmallow/cookie).
    *   It responds to "alluring cues" (happy faces).
    *   Linked to inability to delay gratification.
