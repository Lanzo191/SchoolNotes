## Location -
- Located at the back of the brain (posterior), tucked underneath the [[Occipital Lobe]].
- Means "The Little Brain."

## Structure -
- Like the cerebrum, it has two hemispheres and a highly folded outer layer.
- **Purkinje Cells:** Large neurons that provide the primary output from the cerebellum.

## Functions -
- **Coordination:** Ensures that voluntary movements (initiated by the [[Motor Cortex]]) are smooth and balanced.
- **Precision:** Controls the timing and accuracy of movements (e.g., threading a needle).
- **Motor Learning:** Vital for learning new physical skills through repetition (e.g., playing an instrument).
- **Balance & Posture:** Maintains the body's equilibrium and vertical orientation.

## Clinical Links -
- **Ataxia:** Damage results in a lack of coordination, slurred speech, and an unsteady, "drunken" gait.
- **Alcohol:** One of the first areas affected by alcohol consumption, which explains why intoxicated people struggle with balance and fine motor tasks.
