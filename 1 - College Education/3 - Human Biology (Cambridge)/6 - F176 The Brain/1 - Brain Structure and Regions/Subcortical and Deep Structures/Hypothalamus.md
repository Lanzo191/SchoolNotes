#Education #NervousSystem #OCR #Psychology

## Location -
- Located below the **[[Thalamus]]** and above the **[[Pituitary Gland]]**.
- Forms the floor of the third ventricle.

## Functions -
- **Homeostasis:** The "Master Regulator" of the body's internal environment.
    - **Temperature Control:** Acts as a thermostat (triggers sweating/shivering).
    - **Hunger & Thirst:** Monitors nutrient and hydration levels in the blood.
    - **Sleep-Wake Cycle:** Controls circadian rhythms.
- **Endocrine Control:** Controls the **[[Pituitary Gland]]**, linking the nervous system to the hormone system.
- **Stress Response:** Triggers the HPA Axis and the Sympathetic Nervous System ("Fight or Flight").
- **Emotion:** Works with the Limbic System to coordinate physiological responses to emotion (e.g., increased heart rate when afraid).

## Clinical Links -
- **Hypothalamic Dysfunction:** Can lead to disorders in growth, body temperature, or severe weight issues (Prader-Willi Syndrome).
- **Stress Disorders:** Chronic activation is linked to anxiety and cardiovascular issues.
