#Education #NervousSystem #OCR #Psychology

## Location -
- At the front of the brain, behind the forehead.
- The largest of the four lobes in the human brain.

## Functions -
- **Motor Control:** Contains the **[[Motor Cortex]]**, which initiates voluntary movement.
- **Speech:** Contains **[[Broca’s Area]]** (usually in the left hemisphere), which handles speech production.
- **Executive Functions:** The "CEO" of the brain. Responsible for planning, reasoning, decision-making, and problem-solving.
- **Impulse Control:** Inhibits inappropriate social behaviours and manages risk-taking.
- **Personality:** Strongly associated with an individual's unique personality and emotional regulation.

## Clinical Links -
- **Frontal Lobe Syndrome:** Damage (via trauma or tumor) can cause radical personality changes, impulsivity, and poor social judgement.
- **ADHD:** Often associated with low activity in the frontal regions, leading to difficulty with focus and inhibition.
- *Case Study:* **Phineas Gage**, whose personality changed entirely after an iron rod went through his frontal lobe.
