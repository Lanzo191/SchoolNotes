#Education #NervousSystem #OCR #Psychology

## Location -
- Located in the **[[Parietal Lobe]]**.
- Positioned on the **postcentral gyrus**, immediately behind the [[Motor Cortex]].

## Functions -
- **Sensory Processing:** Receives and processes all sensory input from the body (skin, muscles, and joints).
- **Senses:** Handles sensations of **Touch, Temperature, Pressure, and Pain**.
- **Somatotopic Mapping:** Different parts of the cortex represent different body parts. This is known as the **Sensory Homunculus**.
    - Areas with high sensitivity (like lips and fingertips) have a much larger area of the cortex dedicated to them.

## Clinical Links -
- **Damage:** Can result in numbness, loss of sensation, or the inability to identify objects by touch (**Astereognosis**).
- **Phantom Limb Pain:** Can occur when the somatosensory cortex continues to receive or generate signals from a limb that has been amputated.
