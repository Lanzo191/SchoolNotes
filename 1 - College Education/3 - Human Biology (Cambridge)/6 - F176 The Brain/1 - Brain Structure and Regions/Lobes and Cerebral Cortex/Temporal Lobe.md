## Location -
- Located on the sides of the brain, roughly behind the ears and below the lateral fissure.

## Functions -
- **Auditory Processing:** The primary area for receiving and interpreting sounds.
- **Language Comprehension:** Contains **[[Wernicke’s Area]]** (usually left hemisphere), which allows us to understand spoken and written words.
- **Memory:** Deep within the temporal lobe lies the **[[Hippocampus]]**, crucial for forming new long-term memories.
- **Emotional Processing:** Houses the **[[Amygdala]]**, the centre for fear and threat detection.
- **Visual Recognition:** Involved in identifying objects and recognizing faces (specifically the Fusiform Face Area).

## Clinical Links -
- **Wernicke’s Aphasia:** Damage results in fluent but nonsensical speech and poor comprehension.
- **Amnesia:** Damage to the hippocampal regions within the lobe leads to profound memory loss.
- **Temporal Lobe Epilepsy:** Can cause intense emotional disturbances and sensory hallucinations.
