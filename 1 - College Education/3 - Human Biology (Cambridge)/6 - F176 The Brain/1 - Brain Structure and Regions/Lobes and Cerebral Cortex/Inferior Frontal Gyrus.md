#Education #NervousSystem #OCR #Psychology

## Location -
- Located in the **[[Frontal Lobe]]**.
- Specifically in the prefrontal area.

## Function -
- **Cognitive Control:** The "Cool" system.
- **Inhibition:** Stops impulsive responses (inhibitory control).
- **Language:** Contains **[[Broca’s Area]]** (involved in speech production).

## Link to Core Studies -
*   **[[Casey et al. (2011)]]:**
    *   This region was **less active** in "Low Delay" participants when they tried to resist the alluring cue.
    *   It is responsible for suppressing the urge to press the button (or eat the marshmallow).
