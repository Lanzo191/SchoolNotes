#Education #NervousSystem #OCR #Psychology

## Location -
- Located in the **Left [[Frontal Lobe]]** (in the posterior [[Inferior Frontal Gyrus]]).
- Language is **lateralised** to the left hemisphere for approx. 95% of right-handers.

## Functions -
- **Speech Production:** Coordinates the motor movements required to speak.
- **Syntactic Processing:** Involved in the grammatical structure of sentences.
- **Motor Planning:** Planning how the [[Mouth]] and tongue should move to articulate sounds.

## Clinical Links -
- **Broca’s Aphasia:** Caused by damage to this area (often via stroke).
- **Symptoms:** 
    - Slow, effortful, and "broken" speech.
    - Difficulty forming sentences.
    - **Comprehension remains intact** (they understand what is said to them, but can't reply easily).
- *Case Study:* [[Patient Tan]] (Louis Leborgne).
