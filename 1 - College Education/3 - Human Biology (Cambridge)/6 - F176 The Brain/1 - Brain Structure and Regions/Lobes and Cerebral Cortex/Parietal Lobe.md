#Education #NervousSystem #OCR #Psychology

## Location -
- Located in the upper back portion of the brain, behind the [[Frontal Lobe]] and above the [[Temporal Lobe]].

## Functions -
- **Sensory Processing:** Contains the **[[Somatosensory Cortex]]**, which processes touch, pressure, pain, and temperature from the entire body.
- **Spatial Awareness:** The "where" system. Essential for knowing where your body parts are in relation to each other and your environment.
- **Visual-Spatial Processing:** Helps with navigating through space and localising objects.
- **Mathematics & Logic:** Involved in some aspects of numerical processing and logical reasoning.

## Clinical Links -
- **Agnosia:** Damage can result in the inability to recognise or name common objects by touch.
- **Hemispatial Neglect:** Damage (usually to the right parietal lobe) can cause a person to completely ignore one half of their world (e.g., they might only eat the food on the right side of their plate).
