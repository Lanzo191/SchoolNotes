#Education #NervousSystem #OCR #Psychology

## Location -
- Located in the **Left [[Temporal Lobe]]** (posterior region).
- Part of the language loop, connected to Broca's Area via the arcuate fasciculus.

## Functions -
- **Language Comprehension:** The processing and understanding of spoken and written language.
- **Semantic Processing:** Mapping sounds to their correct meanings.
- **Meaningful Speech:** Ensuring that the words chosen for speech make sense in context.

## Clinical Links -
- **Wernicke’s Aphasia:** Caused by damage to this area.
- **Symptoms:**
    - **Fluent** but nonsensical speech (often called "word salad").
    - Use of made-up words (neologisms).
    - **Poor comprehension:** The patient cannot understand what is being said to them and is often unaware that their own speech doesn't make sense.
