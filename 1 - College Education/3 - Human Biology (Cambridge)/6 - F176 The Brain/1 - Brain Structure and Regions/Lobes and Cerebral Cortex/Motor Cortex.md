## Location -
- Located at the rear of the **[[Frontal Lobe]]**.
- Positioned on the **precentral gyrus**, immediately in front of the Central Sulcus.

## Functions -
- **Movement Initiation:** The primary area responsible for generating neural impulses that travel down the spinal cord to execute voluntary muscle movements.
- **Fine Motor Control:** Manages complex movements, especially in the hands and face.
- **Somatotopic Mapping:** Different parts of the cortex control different body parts. This is known as the **Motor Homunculus**.
    - Areas that require high precision (like the thumb or tongue) occupy a much larger proportion of the motor cortex.

## Clinical Links -
- **Paralysis:** Damage to one side of the motor cortex results in loss of movement or weakness on the **opposite side** of the body (contralateral control).
- **Epilepsy:** Motor seizures can occur if there is abnormal electrical activity in this region.
