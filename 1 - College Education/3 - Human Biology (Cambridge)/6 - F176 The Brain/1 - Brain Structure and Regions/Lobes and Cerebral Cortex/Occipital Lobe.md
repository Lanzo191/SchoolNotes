## Location -
- Located at the very back of the brain (posterior).
- The smallest of the four lobes.

## Functions -
- **Primary Visual Processing:** Contains the **Visual Cortex**, which receives electrical impulses from the eyes via the optic nerves.
- **Interpretation:** Not just seeing, but identifying what you are looking at.
    - **V1 (Primary Visual Cortex):** Detects edges, orientation, and basic movement.
    - **V2-V5:** Process more complex features like **Colour, Shape, and Motion**.
- **Depth Perception:** Allows the brain to estimate how far away objects are.

## Clinical Links -
- **Cortical Blindness:** Damage to the occipital lobe can result in blindness, even if the eyes themselves are perfectly healthy.
- **Prosopagnosia:** Although face recognition happens in the [[Temporal Lobe]], disrupted connections from the occipital lobe can cause "face blindness."
- **Hallucinations:** Overactivity or damage can cause visual hallucinations or distorted perceptions.
