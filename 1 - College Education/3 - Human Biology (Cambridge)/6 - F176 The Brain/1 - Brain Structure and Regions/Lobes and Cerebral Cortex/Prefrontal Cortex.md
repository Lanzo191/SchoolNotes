## Location -
- At the very front of the **[[Frontal Lobe]]**.
- The most recently evolved part of the human brain.

## Functions -
- **Executive Functions:** The "Manager" of the brain.
    - **Planning:** Setting goals and formulating the steps to reach them.
    - **Decision-Making:** Evaluating options and choosing a course of action.
    - **Inhibition:** Stopping inappropriate impulses or reactions (Social Filter).
- **Working Memory:** Holding information in the mind temporarily to work with it.
- **Moral Reasoning:** Determining right from wrong and considering social consequences.
- **Emotional Regulation:** The PFC sends inhibitory signals to the **[[Amygdala]]** to dampen fear or anger responses.

## Key Subregions -
*   **Dorsolateral PFC:** Logic, problem-solving, and working memory.
*   **Ventromedial PFC:** Risk assessment and emotional control.
*   **Orbitofrontal Cortex:** Reward, punishment, and impulse control.

## Clinical Links -
- **Damage:** Results in "Executive Dysfunction"-difficulty planning, poor attention, and a "personality shift" (impulsivity).
- **ADHD:** Associated with underactivity in the PFC, leading to difficulty with focus and inhibition.
- **Schizophrenia:** Structural and functional abnormalities in the PFC are linked to delusions and cognitive deficits.
