**Nature:** Behaviour is caused by inherited (often biological) factors
**Nurture:** Behaviour is caused by environmental factors e.g. socialisation experiences **Determinism:** Behaviour is predetermined by factors outside of our control e.g. biology or environment 
**Freewill:** Behaviour is a result of our own free choice; we choose how to behave and are responsible for our own actions 
**Reductionism:** Behaviour is explained in terms of specific components e.g. a gene or stimulus-response 
**Holism:** Considering multiple and interacting factors when explaining behaviour
**Situational:** Behaviour is caused by the situation or environment that we are in e.g. obeying an authority figure 
**Individual:** Behaviour is caused by factors that are specific to the individual e.g. their personality or biology 
**Usefulness:** The extent to which an area has practical applications / research that can be generalised to real life or target population. Ethics: Whether research in an area adheres to ethical principles. 
**Social sensitivity:** Whether research in an area can have consequences for groups in society 
**Science:** Whether research in an area uses scientific, objective methods and quantitative data
