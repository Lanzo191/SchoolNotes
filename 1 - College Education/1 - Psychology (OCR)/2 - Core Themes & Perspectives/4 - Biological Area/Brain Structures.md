## Essential Brain Structures for OCR Psychology
- **Cerebral Lobes**:
  - [[Frontal Lobe]] (Executive control, decision making)
  - [[Parietal Lobe]] (Sensory processing, spatial perception)
  - [[Temporal Lobe]] (Auditory processing, memory)
  - [[Occipital Lobe]] (Visual processing)
- **Cortical Functional Areas**:
  - [[Prefrontal Cortex]] (Impulse control, delay of gratification - Casey et al.)
  - [[Motor Cortex]] (Voluntary movement control)
  - [[Somatosensory Cortex]] (Touch and sensory feedback)
  - [[Broca’s Area]] (Speech production - Patient Tan)
  - [[Wernicke’s Area]] (Language comprehension)
  - [[Inferior Frontal Gyrus]] (Cognitive control - Casey et al.)
- **Subcortical & Deep Structures**:
  - [[Corpus Callosum]] (Hemispheric communication - Sperry)
  - [[Hippocampus]] (Spatial memory, plasticity - Maguire et al.)
  - [[Amygdala]] (Fear response, emotional conditioning)
  - [[Ventral Striatum]] (Reward sensitivity - Casey et al.)
  - [[Hypothalamus]] (Homeostasis, stress response)
  - [[Thalamus]] (Sensory relay)
  - [[Basal Ganglia]] (Motor habits and routines)
  - [[Cerebellum]] (Coordination and balance)
  - [[Brainstem]] (Autonomic vital functions)
