## Brain Plasticity in Psychology
- **Definition & Biological Mechanisms**: See [[Brain Plasticity]] in Unit F176.

## Relevance to OCR Psychology Core Studies
- **Maguire et al. (2000)**: Proved structural neuroplasticity in adult humans. Licensed London taxi drivers displayed significantly greater grey matter volume in the posterior hippocampus compared to control subjects, directly correlated with navigational experience ("The Knowledge").
- **Blakemore and Cooper (1970)**: Proved experiential and developmental plasticity in early life. Kittens visually restricted to exclusively vertical or horizontal striped environments during the critical period failed to develop cortical neurons responding to the perpendicular orientation.

## Key Mechanisms
- **Synaptic Pruning**: Elimination of redundant or unused neural connections ("use it or lose it") to enhance network efficiency.
- **Axonal Sprouting**: Growth of new axon collateral terminals to form novel functional connections following trauma or specialized learning.
