## Overview -
**Replicability** is the ability of a study to be repeated exactly by another researcher. If a study is replicable and the results are consistent, it is considered **reliable**.

---

## Why is it Scientific? -
*   **Verification:** It allows the scientific community to check if a researcher's findings were a one-off "fluke" or a genuine effect.
*   **Trustworthiness:** Highly replicable research builds a body of credible evidence.
*   **Objectivity:** If anyone can repeat the study and get the same result, it suggests the finding is an objective truth, not just the researcher's opinion.

## How to Ensure Replicability -
1.  **Full Method Disclosure:** The researcher must publish exactly what they did, including materials and timings.
2.  **Operationalisation:** Defining all variables clearly so they can be measured in the same way by others.
3.  **Standardisation:** Using a set procedure that doesn't change between trials.
