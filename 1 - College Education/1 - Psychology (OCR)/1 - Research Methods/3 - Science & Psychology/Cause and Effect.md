## Overview -
The primary goal of the experimental method is to establish **Cause and Effect**. This means proving that the [[Independent Variable]] (IV) was the **sole reason** for the change in the [[Dependent Variable]] (DV).

## How to Establish Cause and Effect -

1.  **Manipulation of IV:** The researcher must deliberately change one factor.
2.  **Measurement of DV:** The outcome must be recorded accurately.
3.  **Control of [[Extraneous Variables]]:** All other factors (e.g., room temperature, time of day) must be kept constant.
4.  **Random Allocation:** Participants should be assigned to groups by chance to avoid bias.

---

## Threats to Cause and Effect -
*   **Confounding Variables:** If an outside factor (like participant intelligence) changes alongside the IV, you cannot be sure what caused the result.
*   **Correlational Data:** Correlations show a *link* but **cannot** show cause and effect (e.g., just because heat and ice cream sales both go up, doesn't mean heat *causes* people to like ice cream).
