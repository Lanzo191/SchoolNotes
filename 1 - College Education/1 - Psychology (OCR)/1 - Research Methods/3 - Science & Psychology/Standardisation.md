## Overview -
**Standardisation** is the process of keeping every aspect of the research procedure the **same for every participant**. It acts as a "recipe" for the study.

---

## Key Components of Standardisation -

*   **Standardised Instructions:** Reading from a script so that no participant gets more help or information than another.
*   **Standardised Environment:** Keeping the room temperature, noise levels, and lighting identical for all groups.
*   **Standardised Materials:** Using the same word lists, questionnaires, or equipment.
*   **Standardised Timings:** Giving everyone exactly 5 minutes for a task, for example.

## Importance -
1.  **Increases Internal Validity:** By keeping everything the same, the researcher ensures that only the IV is affecting the DV.
2.  **Reduces Extraneous Variables:** It eliminates "noise" from the environment or researcher behaviour.
3.  **Improves Replicability:** Makes it easy for other researchers to repeat the study in the future.
