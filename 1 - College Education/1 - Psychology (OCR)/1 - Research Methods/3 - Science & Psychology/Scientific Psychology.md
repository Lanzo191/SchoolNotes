# Scientific vs Unscientific Psychology

## Scientific Psychology -  
- Uses **controlled experiments**  
- Hypotheses are **testable and falsifiable**  
- Data is **objective and quantifiable**  
- Results are **replicable**  
- Based on **systematic observation**  
- Uses **statistical analysis** to draw conclusions  
- Operationalised variables allow **precise measurement**  

## Unscientific Psychology -  
- Relies on **personal opinions or anecdotal evidence**  
- Hypotheses are **not testable or falsifiable**  
- Data is **subjective or purely descriptive**  
- Results **cannot be replicated**  
- [[Observations]] are **unsystematic**  
- Lacks **control of [[Extraneous Variables]]**  
- Conclusions are based on **intuition or speculation**
