## Overview -
These terms refer to the level of **bias** in research. Science strives for objectivity to ensure that findings are a true reflection of reality, not the researcher's opinion.

---

## 1. Objectivity -
**Definition:** Being uninfluenced by personal feelings, expectations, or bias. 
*   **Scientific Ideal:** All researchers should be able to look at the data and come to the same conclusion.
*   **Methods:** Lab experiments, structured observations, and quantitative data (numbers) are more objective.

## 2. Subjectivity -
**Definition:** Based on or influenced by personal tastes, opinions, or emotions.
*   **Human Element:** Some argue that because psychology studies humans, it can never be 100% objective.
*   **Methods:** Unstructured interviews, case studies, and qualitative data (words) are more subjective.

---

## Comparison -

| Feature | Objectivity | Subjectivity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Goal** | To find "Universal Truths." | To find "Individual Meaning." |
| **Data** | Numbers (Quantitative). | Words (Qualitative). |
| **Bias** | Minimised. | Acknowledged or ignored. |
| **Reliability** | High (easy to repeat). | Low (unique to the person). |
