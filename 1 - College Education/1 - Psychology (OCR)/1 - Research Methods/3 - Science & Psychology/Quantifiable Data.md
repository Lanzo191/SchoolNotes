## Overview -
**Quantifiable Data** is information that is recorded in numerical form. It answers the questions "how much," "how many," or "how long."

## Why is it Scientific? -
1.  **Objectivity:** Numbers are less open to individual interpretation than words.
2.  **Statistical Analysis:** Allows for the use of [[Inferential Statistics]] to find the **[[P-Value]]**.
3.  **[[Replicability]]:** It is easier for another researcher to use the same numerical scale than to interpret a narrative description in the same way.
4.  **Statistical Power:** Using more precise numerical data (Interval/Ratio) makes statistical tests more sensitive to finding real effects.

---

## Examples -
*   **Scores:** Total marks on a test (0-100).
*   **Time:** Latency in seconds (e.g., reaction time).
*   **Physical:** Body mass (kg), brain volume (cm³).
*   **Ratings:** Score on a 1-10 Likert scale.

## Comparison -
Quantifiable data is the opposite of **descriptive** or **qualitative** data. While qualitative data provides "depth," quantifiable data provides "precision."
