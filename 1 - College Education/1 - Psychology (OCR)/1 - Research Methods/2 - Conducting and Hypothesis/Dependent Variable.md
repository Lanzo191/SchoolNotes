Dependent Variable (DV) -  
The variable that is **measured** to see whether it has changed as a result of manipulating the IV.

### Strengths -  
- Produces **[[Quantifiable Data]]**, allowing statistical tests.  
- Can be objective (e.g., reaction time) or subjective (e.g., questionnaire score).  
- Directly indicates behavioural changes.

### Weaknesses -  
- Vulnerable to **[[Extraneous Variables]]** affecting accuracy.  
- Measurement tools may be unreliable or biased.  
- Poor [[Operationalisation]] leads to inconsistent results.

### Examples -  
- Time (ms) taken to press a button after a stimulus.  
- Score on a memory recall test out of 20.  
- Anxiety rating on a validated scale.

### Starter Questions -  
- What is the DV in a study on caffeine and alertness?  
- How can poor measurement affect the DV?
