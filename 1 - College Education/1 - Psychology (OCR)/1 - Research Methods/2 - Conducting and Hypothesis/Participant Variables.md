Participant Variables -  
Individual differences that may affect behaviour in a study.

### Examples -  
- Age  
- Gender  
- Motivation  
- Intelligence  
- Personality traits  

### Strengths -  
- Can be reduced using matched pairs or random allocation.  
- Awareness improves study design.

### Weaknesses -  
- Hard to fully control.  
- Can create systematic bias between conditions.

### Starter Questions -  
- Give an example of a participant variable.  
- How can participant variables be controlled?
