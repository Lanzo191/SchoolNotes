#Psychology  #research-methods  #experiment-designs  #quasi-experiments  

## Description  
The IV is **not manipulated by the researcher** - it is naturally occurring.  
Participants fall into groups based on features like gender, age, disorder, or trauma exposure.

## Key Features  
- Used when manipulation would be unethical or impossible  
- Can still measure differences between groups  

## Strengths  
- Allows study of variables that cannot be manipulated  
- High ecological [[Validity]] if real-life situations are used  

## Weaknesses  
- No random allocation → poorer internal [[Validity]]  
- Hard to establish [[Cause and Effect]]  
- [[Confounding Variables]] often unavoidable  

## Example  
Comparing memory ability between people with depression and people without.
