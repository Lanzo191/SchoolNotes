#Psychology  #research-methods  #experiment-designs  #field-experiments  

## Description  
Conducted in a **real-life environment**, but the researcher still manipulates the IV.

## Key Features  
- Real-world behaviour  
- Some variables still controlled  

## Strengths  
- High ecological [[Validity]]  
- Reduced [[Demand Characteristics]]  

## Weaknesses  
- Less control over [[Extraneous Variables]]  
- [[Ethics]] issues (consent, privacy)  
- Harder to replicate  

## Example  
Measuring helping behaviour in a shopping centre by having a confederate drop items.
