#Psychology  #research-methods  #experiment-designs  #natural-experiments  

## Description  
A naturally occurring event creates the IV; the researcher only records the outcome.  
E.g., natural disaster, policy change, lack of sleep due to exam week.

## Key Features  
- IV occurs without researcher involvement  
- Often takes place in real-world settings  

## Strengths  
- High ecological [[Validity]]  
- Allows study of unethical or impossible situations  

## Weaknesses  
- No control over variables  
- Hard to replicate  
- Low internal [[Validity]]  

## Example  
Investigating stress levels before vs after a school introduces compulsory exams.
