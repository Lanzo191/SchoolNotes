## Definition -
**Continuous data** is information that can take **any value within a range**. It is **measured** rather than counted. It can be represented with decimals and fractions, allowing for infinite precision depending on the tool used.

## Strengths -
*   **High Precision:** Captures very subtle differences between participants.
*   **Statistical Power:** Typically associated with **Interval** and **Ratio** [[Levels of Data]], allowing for more powerful statistical tests.

## Weaknesses -
*   **Collection Difficulty:** Often requires precise tools (e.g., stopwatches, scales, thermometers).
*   **Complexity:** Can be more difficult to categorise and present simply.

## Examples -
*   **Time:** 5.23 seconds.
*   **Height/Weight:** 175.5 cm or 70.2 kg.
*   **Temperature:** 36.6°C.
*   **Age:** If measured in precise days/months rather than just years.
