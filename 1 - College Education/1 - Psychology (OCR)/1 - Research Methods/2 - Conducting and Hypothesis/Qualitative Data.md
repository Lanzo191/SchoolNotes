## Definition -
**Qualitative data** is **descriptive, word-based information** that provides insight into the qualities, characteristics, or experiences of participants. It focuses on the "how" and "why" behind behaviour.

## Strengths -
*   **Richness and Detail:** Provides a "thick description" of human behaviour, capturing complexity.
*   **Participant Perspective:** Allows participants to explain their feelings and thoughts in their own words.
*   **High [[Validity]]:** Often provides a more accurate reflection of real life because it isn't restricted by fixed response options.

## Weaknesses -
*   **Subjectivity:** The analysis depends on the researcher's interpretation, which could be biased.
*   **Hard to Analyse:** It is difficult to compare results statistically or identify clear trends across large groups.
*   **Time Consuming:** Collecting and transcribing [[Interviews]] or observational notes takes a significant amount of time.

## Examples -
*   **[[Interviews]]:** Transcripts of what a person said during a therapy session.
*   **Open Questions:** Written answers where people can write as much as they want.
*   **[[Observations]]:** Narrative descriptions of a child's play behaviour.
