Situational Variables -  
Environmental factors that may influence participant behaviour.

### Examples -  
- Temperature  
- Lighting  
- Time of day  
- Instructions given  
- Noise level  

### Strengths -  
- Easily controlled in laboratory settings.  
- [[Standardisation]] reduces problems.

### Weaknesses -  
- Harder to control in [[Field Experiments]].  
- Can interact withs [[Participant Variables]].

### Starter Questions -  
- What situational variables might affect memory recall?  
- How can [[Standardisation]] help?
