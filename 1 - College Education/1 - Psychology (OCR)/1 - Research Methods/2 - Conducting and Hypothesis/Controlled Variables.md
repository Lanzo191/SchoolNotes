Controlled Variables (CV) -  
Factors kept **constant** to ensure they do not influence the DV. Essential for maintaining **internal [[Validity]]**.

### Strengths -  
- Reduces risk of [[Confounding Variables]].  
- Ensures differences between conditions are due to the IV.  
- Makes replication easier.

### Weaknesses -  
- Harder to control in **field** experiments.  
- Over-controlling can make experiments feel unrealistic or artificial.  
- Some variables cannot be controlled (participant mood, motivation).

### Examples -  
- Same instructions given to all participants.  
- Same testing environment (lighting, noise, room).  
- Same duration of exposure to a task.
