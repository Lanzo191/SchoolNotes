# Sampling Techniques - 

### Self Sampling - 

Participants select themselves by responding to an advert etc volunteering themselves to take part 

##### Strengths - 
1. Informed consent
2. Easy
3. Cheap 
4. Diverse 

##### Weakness - 
1. Bias 
2. Ethnocentric 
3. No control over sample

--- 
### Snowball Sampling - 

Researcher identify potential participants.these participants can then contact other people they know for further participation 

##### Strengths - 
1. Can start with a small sample
2. Inexpensive 
3. Less work for researcher 
4. Good for Niche groups 

##### Weaknesses - 
1. Not good for generalisations 
2. Reliance on social network 

---
### Opportunity Sampling - 

Using whoever is willing at the time. 

##### Strengths - 
1. Quick and easy 
2. Cheap
3. High Ecological value

##### Weakness - 
1. Positivity Bias / Sampling bias 
2. No external validity 
3. Ethnocentric 

---
### Random Sampling - 

Using a method where everyone in the target pop has a chance of being selected. 

##### Strengths - 
1. Easy to form sample groups
2. Equal Chance of selection
3. Easiest Method 
4. No Selection bias 

##### Weaknesses - 
1. Can make social bases (gender)
2. Expensive 
3. Slow process

---

One weakness is the sampling method itself, there is a major bias around location/sampling.
