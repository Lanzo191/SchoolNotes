## Definition -
**Ratio data** is the highest and most precise level of measurement. It has all the features of [[Interval Data]] (ordered, equal intervals) but also has a **true zero point**. This means a value of zero represents the complete absence of the characteristic.

## Strengths -
*   **Maximum Analysis:** Supports all mathematical and statistical operations.
*   **Meaningful Comparisons:** You can make "ratio" statements (e.g., "Participant A was twice as fast as Participant B").
*   **Highest Power:** Suitable for the most powerful **Parametric Tests**.

## Weaknesses -
*   **Complexity:** Often requires physical measurement tools rather than simple [[Questionnaires]].
*   **Rarity in Psychology:** It is harder to find psychological constructs that have a true zero (e.g., you can't have "zero intelligence").

## Examples -
*   **Time:** 0 seconds is the absence of time. 10 seconds is twice as long as 5 seconds.
*   **Weight:** 0kg is the absence of weight.
*   **Distance/Height:** Measured in cm or metres.
*   **Reaction Time:** A common ratio measure in cognitive psychology.
