#Psychology  #research-methods  #experiment-designs  #laboratory-experiments  

## Description  
Conducted in a **controlled**, artificial setting with manipulation of the IV.

## Key Features  
- Standardised procedures  
- High experimental control  

## Strengths  
- High internal [[Validity]]  
- Easy to replicate  
- [[Extraneous Variables]] controlled  

## Weaknesses  
- Low ecological [[Validity]]  
- Participant awareness → [[Demand Characteristics]]  
- Artificial behaviour  

## Example  
Memory test conducted in a controlled university research lab.
