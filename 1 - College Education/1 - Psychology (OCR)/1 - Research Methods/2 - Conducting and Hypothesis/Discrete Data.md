## Definition -
**Discrete data** is information that can only take **specific, separate values**. It involves things that are **counted**, not measured. You cannot have "half" a value in discrete data.

## Strengths -
*   **Clarity:** Very straightforward to record and count.
*   **Analysis:** Ideal for frequency tables and bar charts.

## Weaknesses -
*   **Limited Sensitivity:** Cannot represent the nuances or fractions of a behaviour.
*   **Reduced Detail:** Often over-simplifies complex traits.

## Examples -
*   **People:** Number of participants in a study.
*   **Choices:** Number of "Yes" responses.
*   **Frequencies:** Number of times a child pushes a button.
*   **Test Scores:** Total correct answers (if no half-marks are given).
