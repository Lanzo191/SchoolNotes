### Opportunity Sampling
Opportunity sampling involves selecting participants who are **available and willing** at the time the study is conducted. It is often used in classroom or lab settings.

#### Strengths
- It is **quick and easy** to gather participants, saving time.  
- **Cheap**, because minimal resources are needed for recruitment.  
- Can have **high ecological [[Validity]]** because participants are naturally available in real-world settings.

#### Weaknesses
- Can produce **positivity or [[Sampling Bias]]**, as only those present and willing participate.  
- Has **limited external [[Validity]]**, making generalisation to the wider population difficult.  
- Can be **ethnocentric**, especially if the location or population is homogeneous.

#### Explanation
This method is suitable for **practical studies with time constraints**, but care must be taken when drawing conclusions about the broader population.
