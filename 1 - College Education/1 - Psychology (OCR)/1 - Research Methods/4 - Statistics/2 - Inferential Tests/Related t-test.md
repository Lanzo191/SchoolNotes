The **Related t-test** is a **parametric** test for a **difference** with **related groups** and **[[Interval Data]]**.

## When to Use
1. **Goal**: Difference.
2. **Design**: Related (Repeated/Matched).
3. **Data**: Interval.

---

## Expansive Worked Scenario
**Research Aim**: To see if reaction time (seconds) changes after drinking coffee.
**Design**: Repeated Measures (12 participants).

### Step 1: Observed Value ($t$)
- After calculating the means and variance, the researcher found an **Observed Value of $t = 2.45$**.

### Step 2: Calculate Degrees of Freedom ($df$)
- $df = N - 1 = 12 - 1 = \mathbf{11}$

### Step 3: Compare to Critical Value
- For $df=11$ at $p \leq 0.05$ (two-tailed), the **Critical Value is 2.201**.

### Conclusion
"The observed value of $t=2.45$ is **greater than** the critical value of 2.201. Therefore, the results are **significant** at $p \leq 0.05$. We reject the Null Hypothesis and conclude that drinking coffee significantly changes reaction time."
