The **Unrelated t-test** is a **parametric** test for a **difference** with **independent groups** and **[[Interval Data]]**.

## When to Use
1. **Goal**: Difference.
2. **Design**: Independent (Unrelated).
3. **Data**: Interval.

---

## Expansive Worked Scenario
**Research Aim**: To see if there is a difference in "Test Scores" between Students using a Tablet ($n_1=10$) vs Students using Paper ($n_2=10$).
**Data**: Independent groups ($N=20$).

### Step 1: Observed Value ($t$)
- After calculating the means and variance, the researcher found an **Observed Value of $t = 1.82$**.

### Step 2: Calculate Degrees of Freedom ($df$)
- $df = (n_1 + n_2) - 2 = (10 + 10) - 2 = \mathbf{18}$

### Step 3: Compare to Critical Value
- For $df=18$ at $p \leq 0.05$ (two-tailed), the **Critical Value is 2.101**.

### Conclusion
"The observed value of $t=1.82$ is **less than** the critical value of 2.101. Therefore, the results are **not significant** at $p \leq 0.05$. We accept the Null Hypothesis and conclude there is no significant difference in test scores between tablet and paper users."
