#### 2) How is Milgram's study considered valid (2)

There is a high internal [[Validity]] inside the experiment; in specific there is a high face [[Validity]]. 

Face [[Validity]] is how well the original claim is measured in the experiment, In Milgram's shock experiment he wanted to find how people act in response to authority and if they are going to comply  to an authority figure or not, This was a large question at the time given the holocaust had just taken place. 

Milgram's shock experiment used a controlled observation design which allows the participant to not display [[Demand Characteristics]] and makes it a fairer test. 


##### 3) How obedience was measured in Bocchiario's study into disobedience and whistleblowing 

Bocchiaro's study measured obedience by asking participants to write an article to recruit people into a dangerous experiment, They had 3 options and that was to write the article, Refuse outright or write a letter to an outside source detailing that the experiment they proposed was dangerous . 

If the participant was obedient then they would write the article promoting the hypothetical experiment. 


#### 4) One Strength of Bocchiaro's study (3)

One strength of the study is the fact it took place in a controlled environment, whilst a controlled environment does lower the ecological [[Validity]] it does mean they can control [[Extraneous Variables]] such as social pressure from the other participants.

All the participants where taken to a separate secluded  room to write the advertisement article which minimises social pressure / diffusion of responsibility.  If there were multiple people around there would be a higher likelihood that the participant would have 'blew the whistle'

This high level of control increases the study’s internal [[Validity]], as it ensures that differences in obedience or whistleblowing were caused by the experimental manipulation rather than other social pressures.

#### 6) One weakness of the type of data collected in Piliavin's sutdy (3)

One weakness is that the experiment relies of covert observation in an already busy environment which makes the experiment vulnerable to observer bias. 

The observers had to record multiple different things such as; how long they took to respond race/gender of the helper and if they where in a group or not. Because these judgements were made at the time of it happening it may be subjective or they may have misinterpreted / not noticed behaviours of the helper/other civilians. This reduces internal [[Validity]] as it may not be accurately measuring what was happening
