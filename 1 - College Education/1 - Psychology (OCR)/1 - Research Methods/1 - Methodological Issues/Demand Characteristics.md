## Demand Characteristics
When participants change their behaviour because they **think they know the aim** of the study.

### Types of Demand Characteristics
- **Please-U effect:** participants try to help the researcher.
- **Screw-U effect:** participants try to sabotage results.

## Social Desirability Bias
Occurs when participants give answers they believe are socially acceptable rather than truthful ones.

### Strengths
- Recognising these biases allows researchers to design better-controlled studies and helps design better experiments.
- Awareness can lead to the use of single- or double-blind methods to reduce bias.

### Weaknesses
- Distorts genuine behaviour and data, reducing internal [[Validity]].
- Threatens internal [[Validity]].
- Difficult to detect or measure how much bias has affected responses.
- Common in [[Self-Report Methods]].

### Examples / How to Reduce
- Use deception (ethically) to conceal the true aim.
- Use anonymity to reduce pressure to conform.
- Include filler questions or indirect measures in [[Questionnaires Sociology]].
