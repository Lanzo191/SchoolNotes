#PsychologyComp1 #ResearchMethods

Operationalisation -  
Defining variables in **clear, measurable terms** so they can be tested and replicated.

### Strengths -  
- Ensures clarity and consistency.  
- Allows objective measurement.  
- Makes research replicable.

### Weaknesses -  
- Over-simplification may reduce ecological [[Validity]].  
- Poor operationalisation can distort results.

### Examples -  
- “Aggression” → number of punches thrown at a punch bag.  
- “Memory” → number of words recalled from a list in 60 seconds.  
- “Stress” → score on the perceived stress scale.
