Different research designs affect reliability, [[Validity]], and generalisability.  

### Experimental Designs -  
- [[Laboratory Experiments]] – high control and internal [[Validity]] but low ecological [[Validity]].  
- [[Field Experiments]] – higher realism but less control over [[Extraneous Variables]].  
- [[Natural Experiments]] – ethical and practical but lack random allocation.  

### Correlational Studies -  
- Identify relationships between variables but cannot infer causation.  

### Observational Methods -  
- Allow study of natural behaviour but may suffer from observer bias.  

### Self-Report Methods (Questionnaires/Interviews) -  
- Provide access to subjective data but risk social desirability bias and misunderstanding.  

### Case Studies -  
- Offer detailed [[Qualitative Data]] but lack generalisability.
