The **Medical Model** (or Biological Model) assumes that mental disorders are physical illnesses with biological causes.

## Core Assumptions
1. **Biochemical Explanations**: Disorders are caused by an imbalance of neurotransmitters (e.g., Low **Serotonin** in Depression; High **[[Dopamine]]** in Schizophrenia).
2. **Genetic Explanations**: Mental illness is inherited through [[Genes]] (studied via Twin Studies and Adoption Studies).
3. **Brain Structure**: Abnormalities in specific brain regions cause certain symptoms (e.g., an overactive **[[Amygdala]]** in Anxiety).

---

## Biological Treatments
- **Drug Therapy**: (e.g., SSRIs for Depression, Antipsychotics for Schizophrenia).
- **Electroconvulsive Therapy (ECT)**: Used for severe, treatment-resistant depression.
- **Psychosurgery**: (Rarely used today).

---

## Strengths and Weaknesses

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Scientific**: Based on objective, physical evidence (brain scans, blood tests). | **Reductionist**: Reduces complex human emotions to just brain chemistry. |
| **Effective Treatments**: Drug therapies have helped millions of people lead normal lives. | **Side Effects**: Medications can have severe physical side effects. |
| **Reduced Stigma**: Viewing illness as biological can reduce the "blame" on the patient. | **Passive Patient**: The patient is "treated" by a doctor rather than actively working on their issues. |
