## Maguire et al. (2000) - Structural Changes in the Hippocampus

### 1. Theoretical Background
*   **Brain Plasticity:** The idea that the brain is not a static organ but can change its physical structure and density in response to learning and experience.
*   **Hippocampus:** A brain region in the **limbic system** that is responsible for **spatial memory** and navigation.
*   **The Knowledge:** The extensive training program London taxi drivers undergo, which requires them to memorize over 25,000 streets and thousands of landmarks.

### 2. Aim
To investigate whether structural changes could be detected in the brains of people with extensive experience of spatial navigation, specifically **London taxi drivers**.

### 3. Methodology
*   **Method:** Quasi-experiment.
*   **Design:** **Matched Pairs Design**.
*   **Sample:** 
    *   **Experimental Group:** 16 right-handed male London taxi drivers (mean experience 14.3 years).
    *   **Control Group:** 50 right-handed male non-taxi drivers (matched for age).
*   **Independent Variable (IV):** Whether the participant was a London taxi driver or a non-taxi driver.
*   **Dependent Variable (DV):** The volume and density of grey matter in the **Hippocampus**, measured via **MRI** scans.

### 4. Procedure
1.  Structural **MRI** scans were taken for all participants.
2.  The scans were analyzed using two techniques:
    *   **Voxel-Based Morphometry (VBM):** Measured the density of grey matter across the brain.
    *   **Pixel Counting:** An expert (blind to the participant's identity) counted pixels on slices of the hippocampus to calculate volume.
3.  The results were correlated with the length of time the participants had been driving taxis.

### 5. Results
*   **Structural Difference:** Taxi drivers had significantly **more grey matter volume** in the **Posterior Hippocampus** compared to the control group.
*   **Anterior vs. Posterior:** Control participants had more grey matter in the **Anterior Hippocampus**.
*   **Correlation:** There was a significant **positive correlation** between the amount of time spent as a taxi driver and the volume of the **Posterior Hippocampus**.

### 6. Conclusion
*   The adult human brain has the ability to structurally reorganize itself in response to environmental demands (**Brain Plasticity**).
*   The **Posterior Hippocampus** is specialized for the storage and retrieval of already learned spatial information (the "mental map" of London).

### 7. Detailed Evaluation & Improvements

| Feature | Evaluation & Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Research Method** | **Quasi-Experiment**: Naturally occurring IV (job). High **ecological validity** but no direct manipulation of the IV. |
| **Design** | **Matched Pairs**: The groups were matched for age, gender, and handedness, which controlled for many **participant variables**. |
| **Sample** | **16 Taxi Drivers**: A relatively small sample, and it was entirely **androcentric** (male only), limiting **generalisability** to women. |
| **Sampling Method** | **Opportunity Sampling**: Participants were recruited from a pool of people who had already received MRI scans or were working drivers. |
| **Data Type** | **Quantitative Data**: **MRI** scans provided objective, scientific measurements that are not subject to researcher bias. |
| **Ethics** | **Very Good**: MRI is a non-invasive and safe procedure. Informed consent was obtained from all participants. |
| **Weakness** | **Correlation vs. Causation**: As a quasi-experiment, we cannot be 100% sure that driving *caused* the brain growth (though the correlation suggests it). |
| **Reliability Improvements** | **Standardisation**: All scans were analyzed using the same **VBM** software and pixel-counting methods, making the results highly **replicable**. |
| **Validity Improvements** | **Single-Blind Technique**: The researcher who analyzed the scans did not know if they belonged to a taxi driver or a control, reducing **researcher bias**. |

**Model Answer:** According to Maguire, the **Posterior Hippocampus** is responsible for the **storage and retrieval** of spatial information, as evidenced by its increased volume in experienced taxi drivers who use "The Knowledge."

### Q2) Describe the "Matched Pairs" used in this study (3 marks)
**Model Answer:** The researchers matched the **16 taxi drivers** with a control group of **50 non-taxi drivers** from an MRI database. They were matched for **age**, **gender** (all male), and **handedness** (all right-handed) to ensure that these participant variables did not influence the brain volume results.

---

### Q3) Discuss one strength of using MRI scans to collect data (3 marks)
**Model Answer:** One strength of using **MRI scans** is that they provide **objective and scientific** quantitative data. Unlike behavioral observations, MRI results are not subject to human interpretation or bias, ensuring that the measurements of hippocampal volume are highly accurate and **internally valid**.
