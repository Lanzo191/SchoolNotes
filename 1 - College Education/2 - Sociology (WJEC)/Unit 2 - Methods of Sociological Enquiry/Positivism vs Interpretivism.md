To understand research methods, you must understand the two main theoretical approaches to sociological research: **Positivism** and **Interpretivism**.

---

## 1. Positivism (Scientific Approach)
Positivists believe that sociology should be studied like a **natural science** (like Chemistry or Physics). 
- **View of Society**: Society is an objective reality that exists "out there," independent of individuals. and so sociology is scientific 
- **Goal**: To find **social laws** and cause-and-effect relationships.
- **Preferred Data**: Quantitative data (numbers, statistics).
- **Preferred Methods**: Questionnaires, structured interviews, official statistics.
- **Key Terms**: Reliability, objectivity, maggtv cro-sociology.
- **Social Facts:**  are objective, measurable phenomena that positivists believe shape individual behavior,
- **Theories:** Durkheim's Suicide Study 

## 2. Interpretivism (Humanistic Approach)
Interpretivists argue that humans are different from chemicals or plants because they have **consciousness** and free will.
- **View of Society**: Society is socially constructed through the meanings we give to our actions. and so sociology should be autonomous 
- **Goal**: To achieve **Verstehen** (empathetic understanding):seeing the world through the eyes of the people being studied.
- **Preferred Data**: Qualitative data (words, descriptions).
- **Preferred Methods**: Unstructured interviews, participant observation, personal documents.
- **Key Terms**: Validity, subjectivity, micro-sociology.
