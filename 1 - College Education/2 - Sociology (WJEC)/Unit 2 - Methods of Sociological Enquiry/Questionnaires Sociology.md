**Questionnaires** are a list of pre-set questions used to gather data from a large number of people. They are a favorite method of **Positivists**.

## Types of Questions
- **Closed Questions**: Offer a fixed set of answers (e.g., Yes/No). Produces **[[Quantitative Data]]**.
- **Open Questions**: Allow participants to answer in their own words. Produces **[[Qualitative Data]]**

---

## PET Evaluation

### 1. Practical
- **Pros**: Cheap, fast, and easy to distribute to thousands of people (especially online). No need for trained interviewers.
- **Cons**: Low response rates are common. You cannot check if the right person actually filled it in.

### 2. Ethical
- **Pros**: Generally very ethical; easy to provide anonymity and withdrawal is as simple as not returning the form.
- **Cons**: Sensitive topics might cause distress without a researcher there to offer support.

### 3. Theoretical
- **Pros (Reliability)**: Highly reliable because everyone answers the same questions; easy to replicate and identify trends.
- **Cons (Validity)**: Participants may lie or misunderstand questions. **Interpretivists** argue they lack "depth" and don't provide a true picture of meaning.

---

## Key Concepts
- **Operationalisation**: Turning an abstract idea (like "happiness") into a measurable question.
- **Social Desirability**: When people give the "right" answer to look good rather than the true one.
