## Overview
The concept of [**Moral Panics** was developed by **[[Interactionism|Interactionist]]** sociologists to explain how society (and specifically the media) reacts to, and often exaggerates, the behavior of youth subcultures.

---

## Key Theorist: Stanley Cohen (1972) : "Folk Devils and Moral Panics"

### Context
Cohen studied the media reaction to clashes between two youth subcultures, the **Mods and Rockers**, at English seaside resorts in the 1960s.

### The Process of a Moral Panic
1.  **Inventory:** The media reports an event (e.g., a minor scuffle) using **sensationalist language** ("Battle", "Terror", "Riots").
2.  **Symbolisation:** The media identifies symbols (clothes, hair, bikes) to clearly identify the group.
3.  **Folk Devils:** The group is demonised and portrayed as a threat to societal values. They become **"Folk Devils"**.
4.  **Deviancy Amplification Spiral:**
    *   The police and courts come under pressure to act tough.
    *   More arrests are made.
    *   This confirms the media's prediction that the group is dangerous.
    *   More young people are attracted to the group *because* of the notoriety.
    *   The problem gets worse *because* of the reaction to it.

### Key Terms
*   **Deviancy Amplification:** The process where attempts to control deviance actually produce more of it.
*   **Folk Devil:** A person or group portrayed by the media as an outsider and a threat to society.
*   **Moral Entrepreneur:** People (editors, politicians, police chiefs) who lead the "crusade" against the deviant group.

## Evaluation

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Explains Media Power**: Shows how the media doesn't just report news but "constructs" it. | **Outdated?**: In the age of social media, it is harder for one single newspaper to start a national panic. |
| **Highlights Labelling**: Links clearly to **[[Interactionism|Labelling Theory]]** and the power of definitions. | **Ignores Real Harm**: Critics (like **Left Realists**) argue that calling something a "panic" implies the problem isn't real, when things like knife crime cause actual harm. |

### Q) Explain the concept of a "Deviancy Amplification Spiral". (10 marks)

**Model Answer:**
The **Deviancy Amplification Spiral**, a concept developed by **Stanley Cohen**, describes how the attempt to control deviant behaviour can actually lead to an increase in that behaviour.

The process begins when the media identifies a group as a **Folk Devil** and uses sensationalist reporting to create a **Moral Panic**. This puts pressure on "Moral Entrepreneurs" (like politicians and the police) to take action. As a result, the police increase their surveillance and arrests of the group. This increase in recorded crime then "proves" the media was right all along, leading to even more negative reporting. Furthermore, the notoriety can attract more young people to the subculture as a form of rebellion. Ultimately, the social reaction creates more deviance than there was originally, showing that deviance is **socially constructed**.
