#Education #Media #Sociology #WJEC

## Overview -
The emergence of distinct youth cultures in the UK was driven by two key factors: the **economic boom** of the 1950s and the role of the **media** in creating a new consumer market.

---

## 1. Economic Factors -

### The 1950s Boom -
*   Post-WWII Britain saw a period of relative prosperity and full employment.
*   Young people now had **disposable income** (wages) because they were no longer required to give all their money to their parents.
*   **Mark Abrams Report (1959):** A key market research report that identified the "Teenage Consumer." It found that young people’s spending power had doubled since 1938, leading to the creation of products specifically for them (fashion, records, coffee bars).

## 2. Media Factors -

### Representation of Youth -
*   The media was the first to identify "The Teenager" as a distinct social group.
*   **Stan Cohen:** Argues that the media often **exaggerates** youth culture, portraying them as a threat to society (**Moral Panics**).
*   **Folk Devils:** The media creates "Folk Devils" (e.g., Mods and Rockers) which leads to a **Deviancy Amplification Spiral**.
