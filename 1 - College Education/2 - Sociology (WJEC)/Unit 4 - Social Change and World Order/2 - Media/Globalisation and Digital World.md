**Globalisation** is the increasing interconnectedness of societies across the world. The development of **digital communication** (social media, the internet) has accelerated this process.

## 1. Key Concepts
- **Global Village (McLuhan)**: The idea that time and distance have "shrunk" because of digital technology, making the world feel like one small community.
- **Network Society (Castells)**: Power now lies in networks (e.g., financial markets, social media platforms) rather than just geographic countries.
- **Cultural Homogenisation**: The fear that Western (specifically American) culture is taking over and destroying local cultures.
- **Cultural Hybridity**: When global and local cultures mix to create something new (e.g., "Glocal" food or music).

## 2. Impact on Identity
- **Choice**: We can now form identities based on global interests (e.g., online gaming, fandoms) rather than just our local neighborhood.
- **The "Digital Divide"**: Inequality still exists; those without access to fast internet (the "digital underclass") are marginalized.
- **Virtual Communities**: People find support and belonging in online groups that transcend physical boundaries.
