Historically, ethnic minorities have been under-represented or portrayed through a narrow range of negative stereotypes.

## Patterns of Representation (Van Dijk)
Van Dijk (1991) analysed news reporting and identified several common stereotypes for ethnic minorities:

1.  **As Criminals:** Black people are disproportionately associated with mugging, drugs, and gang violence (e.g., Hall's study on "Policing the Crisis").
2.  **As a Threat:** Immigrants and asylum seekers are often described using "flood" or "swarm" metaphors, presenting them as a threat to jobs and culture.
3.  **As Unimportant:** Minorities are rarely the "heroes" or focus of the story unless they are criminals or victims.
4.  **As Abnormal:** Cultural practices (e.g., arranged marriages, hijabs) are framed as "problems" or "weird" compared to white norms.

## Three Phases of Representation (Alvarez)
1.  **The Slave Figure:** Historical portrayal of black people as subservient or mocked (e.g., *Tom and Jerry*'s Mammy Two Shoes).
2.  ** The Native:** Portrayal of non-white people as "savage" or "primitive" (e.g., in colonial literature).
3.  **The Entertainer:** Minorities are accepted in sports and music, but still excluded from positions of serious power or expertise.

## Tokenism
*   **Definition:** Including a small number of minority characters just to appear diverse, without giving them meaningful roles or storylines.
*   **Effect:** It creates an illusion of equality while maintaining white dominance.

## Islamophobia
Since 9/11, Muslims have been heavily stereotyped as **"The Enemy Within"** or terrorists.
*   **Poole (2000):** Found that even before 9/11, Islam was mostly reported in negative contexts (fundamentalism, repression of women).

---

## Changes in Representation (Evaluation)
*   **Hybrid Identities:** Newer media reflects complex, hybrid identities rather than simple stereotypes (e.g., *Bend It Like Beckham*).
*   **Black Panther Effect:** The success of films like *Black Panther* proves that minority-led content is commercially viable, challenging the "white hero" norm.
*   **Active Audience:** Minorities are using New Media (social media) to challenge stereotypes directly (e.g., #BlackLivesMatter).
