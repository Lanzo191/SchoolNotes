The media tends to stereotype age groups, often ignoring the middle-aged and focusing negatively on the young and old.

## 1. Representation of Childhood
*   **The "Little Angel":** Children portrayed as innocent, vulnerable, and in need of protection (e.g., in advertising).
*   **The "Little Devil":** Children (especially working-class) portrayed as annoying or evil (e.g., *Bart Simpson*, *Horrid Henry*).
*   **Consumer Target:** Children are increasingly targeted as consumers ("Pester Power").

## 2. Representation of Youth (Teens/20s)
Youth are often subject to negative stereotyping and **Moral Panics**.
*   **Cohen:** Folk Devils and Moral Panics. Youth subcultures (Mods/Rockers) are exaggerated as a threat to social order.
*   **Hebdige:** Youth are portrayed as "trouble" or "lazy".
*   **Key Stereotypes:**
    1.  **The Hooligan/Yob:** Violent, hoodie-wearing troublemaker.
    2.  **The Layabout:** Lazy, glued to phones, lacking motivation.

## 3. Representation of the Elderly
The elderly are largely **under-represented** (Symbolic Annihilation), especially older women (gendered ageism).

*   **Newman:** Upper/Middle-class elderly men are often portrayed as "wise experts" (news anchors, judges).
*   **Negative Stereotypes:**
    1.  **Grumpy:** Difficult and complaining (e.g., *Victor Meldrew*).
    2.  **Burden:** A drain on the NHS and economy ("Grey Tsunami").
    3.  **Infantilised:** Treated like children, befuddled, or incompetent.
*   **"The Grey Pound":** More recent positive representations (e.g., *The Best Exotic Marigold Hotel*) reflect the growing economic power of active retirees.
