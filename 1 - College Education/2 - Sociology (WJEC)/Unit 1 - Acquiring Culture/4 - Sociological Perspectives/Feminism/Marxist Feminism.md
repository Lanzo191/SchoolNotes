## Overview
**Marxist [[Feminism]]** argues that women’s oppression is not primarily caused by men (as Radical Feminists believe) or by laws (as Liberal Feminists believe), but by the system of **[[Capitalism]]**. They see gender inequality as a tool used by the ruling class to maintain their power and wealth.

## Key Ideas
*   **Reserve Army of [[Labour]]:** Women are used as a "reserve" work force:brought into the economy during booms (e.g., WWII) and pushed back into the home during slumps. This keeps wages low for everyone.
*   **Reproduction of [[Labour]] Power:** Women provide unpaid domestic work (childcare, cooking, cleaning), which ensures that the next generation of workers is raised at no cost to the **Capitalist** class.
*   **Absorption of Anger:** **Fran Ansley** argues that women act as "takers of shit":absorbing the frustration and anger of their husbands who are exploited at work. This prevents the men from revolting against the system.

## Key Thinkers
*   **Fran Ansley:** Famous for describing women as the "takers of shit" within the nuclear family.
*   **Margaret Benston:** Argued that capitalism benefits from women's unpaid domestic [[Labour]].

## Evaluation
### Strengths
*   Links gender inequality to the wider economic structure of society (**[[Marxism]]**).
*   Explains the specific economic disadvantages women face (the "double burden" of work and home).

### Weaknesses
*   **Economic Determinism:** Critics argue it reduces all gender issues to money/class and ignores that men in non-capitalist societies also oppress women.
*   **Outdated:** Many women now have their own careers and are no longer just a "reserve army" or "housewives."

### Q) Discuss the Marxist Feminist view that women serve the interests of Capitalism. (10 marks)

**Model Answer:**
Marxist Feminists argue that the exploitation of women is essential for the survival of **[[Capitalism]]**. They believe the nuclear family serves the ruling class by providing "free" services that keep the economy running.

One key way women serve capitalism is as a **Reserve Army of [[Labour]]**. This means women can be hired when the economy is growing to keep wages low, and then fired or encouraged back into domestic roles when there is a recession. This flexibility benefits the employers, not the women.

Another function is the **reproduction of labour power**. By performing unpaid domestic chores and childcare, women ensure that current workers are fed and rested, and that future workers are raised. As **Margaret Benston** notes, if the government had to pay for these services, it would cost billions. Finally, **Fran Ansley** argues that women provide emotional support that absorbs the anger of exploited male workers, acting as **"takers of shit"** and preventing social revolution.

---

**Revision Tip:** When discussing Marxist Feminism, always contrast them with **Traditional [[Marxism]]**. While Traditional Marxists focus on the *factory*, Marxist Feminists focus on the *home* as a place of production.
