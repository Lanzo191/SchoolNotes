## Overview
**Neo-[[Marxism]]** is an updated version of Marxist theory. While traditional **[[Marxism]]** focuses almost entirely on the **Economic Base**, Neo-Marxists argue that **Culture** and **Ideology** are equally important in maintaining ruling-class power. They seek to explain why the **Proletariat** revolution predicted by Marx has not yet occurred.

---

## Key Concepts

### 1. Hegemony (Antonio Gramsci)
Traditional Marxists believe the rich rule through force (e.g., the police). **Antonio Gramsci** argued they rule through **Hegemony**:the use of ideas, values, and culture to persuade the working class that the capitalist system is "natural" and "common sense."
*   This is achieved through the **Superstructure** (Media, Education, Religion).

### 2. Relative Autonomy
The idea that institutions like the state and media have some freedom (**autonomy**) from the economic base. They don't *always* do exactly what the rich want, but they usually support the status quo in the long run to keep society stable.

### 3. Dual Consciousness
The working class often have a **"dual consciousness"** they see the injustices of capitalism through their own lived experience (e.g., low wages), but they are also influenced by ruling-class ideology. This creates a state of confusion that prevents them from revolting.

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Explains Stability**: Provides a clear reason why the working-class revolution hasn't happened in the West. | **Underestimates Economics**: Traditional Marxists argue that focusing on "ideas" ignores that money and ownership are the real sources of power. |

### Q) Explain the difference between Traditional Marxism and Neo-Marxism. (10 marks)

**Model Answer:**
While both theories are **conflict theories** that aim to overthrow **[[Capitalism]]**, they differ in their focus. **Traditional Marxism** is economically deterministic, meaning it believes the **Economic Base** (money and production) controls everything. Marxists like Karl Marx believed that workers would eventually reach a state of class consciousness and revolt.

In contrast, **Neo-[[Marxism]]** focuses on the **Superstructure**. Theorists like **Antonio Gramsci** argue that the ruling class maintains power through **Hegemony**:winning the "hearts and minds" of the workers so they accept their own exploitation. Neo-Marxists also believe in **Dual Consciousness**, where workers are partially aware of their exploitation but are held back by the dominant ideology. Essentially, Traditional Marxism focuses on **force and money**, while Neo-Marxism focuses on **culture and ideas**.
