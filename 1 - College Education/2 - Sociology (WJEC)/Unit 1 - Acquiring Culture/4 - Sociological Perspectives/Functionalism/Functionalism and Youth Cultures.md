### Overview 

- Functionalists argue that a healthy society needs to integrate its members - people need to feel a part of something bigger than themselves, and they need to understand their place in society, society organically finds a way to do that. 

- [[Rites of Passage]] like confirmation in the church used to be our way of helping children integrate into their new adult responsibilities. 

- Talcott Parsons said that youth cultures/subcultures act as a period of transition between dependent childhood and independent adulthood, Youth's in their late teens need to distance themselves from their parents for them to assert independence and that its a natural process in society.  However they need a place of belonging to do this safely and with support so they band together in a separate youth culture or youth subculture

- Eisenstadt agrees that youth cultures act as a place of belonging and built on Parson's argument by saying the youth get integrated into our norms and values of society by first breaking them; They push the boundaries of what is acceptable and then learn through [[Social Control]] where the lines are and through this they learn a value consensus
