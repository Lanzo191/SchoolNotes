## Case Study: Goths and Moral Panics

This case study illustrates how a youth subculture can be demonised by the media, turning them into **Folk Devils**, and the real-world consequences of this labelling.

---

### 1. The "Villains": Columbine and Marilyn Manson (1999)
The first major Moral Panic involving Goths occurred after the **Columbine High School shooting** in the USA.

*   **The Media Narrative:** The media incorrectly linked the shooters to the "Goth" subculture and the music of **Marilyn Manson**.
*   **Folk Devils:** Goths were rebranded from "eccentric teenagers" to dangerous, potential school shooters.
*   **Symbolisation:** Black trench coats, eyeliner, and industrial music became symbols of violence in the public imagination.
*   **Moral Entrepreneurs:** Politicians and religious groups called for bans on Goth music and stricter dress codes in schools.
*   **Result:** A **Moral Panic** ensued where parents were terrified their children were joining a "death cult".

### 2. The Victims: Sophie Lancaster (2007)
In the UK, the long-term effect of "othering" this subculture was seen in the murder of **Sophie Lancaster**.

*   **The Event:** Sophie and her boyfriend were attacked in a park in Lancashire simply because they were dressed as Goths. Sophie died from her injuries.
*   **Sociological Analysis:** The attackers viewed them as "freaks" or "others" worthy of violence. This highlights the danger of **media labelling**-by dehumanising a group as "weird" or "deviant", it can legitimate violence against them.
*   **Legal Change:** The **Sophie Lancaster Foundation** successfully campaigned for police to record attacks on subcultures as **Hate Crimes**, placing "Goth" alongside race, religion, and sexuality as a protected characteristic in some forces.

---

### 3. Application of Stanley Cohen's Theory
The Goth case study fits Cohen's **Moral Panic** model perfectly:

1.  **Inventory:** The media identified Goths as a threat to social values (safety/childhood innocence).
2.  **Exaggeration:** The link between Goth fashion and violence was massively overstated (most Goths are pacifist).
3.  **Prediction:** The media predicted more violence would occur if the subculture wasn't controlled.
4.  **Deviancy Amplification:** By banning the clothing and isolating Goth kids in schools, authority figures alienated them further, pushing them deeper into the subculture for support (Self-Fulfilling Prophecy).
