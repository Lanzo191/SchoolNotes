## Overview
**Angela McRobbie (1978)** coined the term **'Cock Rock'** to describe a specific genre of rock music popular in the 1970s and 80s.

### Characteristics
*   **Aggressive Male Sexuality:** The music and performance style were defined by an aggressive, hyper-masculine expression of sexuality.
*   **Derogatory Lyrics:** Songs often referred to women in objectifying or derogatory ways.
*   **Male Domination:** The scenes were heavily male-dominated, excluding women or relegating them to passive roles (e.g., groupies).

### Sociological Significance
*   **Gender and Youth Culture:** McRobbie used this to explain how gender played a central role in defining youth cultures of that period.
*   **Exclusion of Girls:** It illustrates why girls might have been alienated from public subcultures and retreated to **Bedroom Culture**.
*   **Objectification:** Evidence that some male-dominated youth cultures viewed women primarily as objects.
