The article I used was from the BBC about children's mental health [Located here](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c4gp19n111vo) ( if the link doesnt work its called "Child mental health crisis: Is better resilience the solution?")

The article tries its best to stay from a neutral perspective yet there are is a plethora of quotes that are placed into incorrect context in almost a framing demeanour, the author also frames this as a debate, something that does nothing but fuel right wing views on mental health, they raise the question; "is there really a mental health crisis or are young people simply not resilient enough?" as if it's this wide scale sociological debate that only the greatest minds can dare comment on and yet there is no anecdote from a child who has suffered from bullying , neglectful parenting, abuse, toxicity. They quote "one of the country's leading experts in child and adolescent psychiatry, Prof Andrea Danese, from King’s College London" but lack any actual ground reports from an average child which is what the article is meant to be about. 

There is a clear rise of people suffering from mental health but this isn't due to the loss of resilience it's due to the newly found acceptance over topics once considered taboo topics such as mental health. 

This current generation is being coined the "Snowflake Generation" when the only actual change we've experienced is generosity and basic kindness becoming a core principal in what upholds modern society. There are MANY MANY news sources and even very famous people who believe this generation is weaker than any others simply due to there being more acceptance in this world.
