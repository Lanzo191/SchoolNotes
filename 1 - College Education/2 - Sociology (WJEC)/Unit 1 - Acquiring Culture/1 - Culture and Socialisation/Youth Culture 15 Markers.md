
### Q1: Explain why young people join youth cultures (15 Marks)

- There are numerous different reasons why young people join youth cultures, these can range from ideas of found family and finding friends with shared interests to using it as a way to rebel against societal norms. 
  
  There are a plethora of youth cultures that have been formed as a way to rebel against societal norms for example the punks where formed as an anti-establishment and anti-capitalist group in 1970's in areas such as the United Kingdom and America. There is a large ethos of anti-conformity and as such they have created their own genre of music and styles of clothing with commonly associated with Dr. Martens and predominantly Leather based clothing and DIY accessories. Hebdige's study highlights the large use of bricolage (Recycling items) in these youth cultures as it acts as anti-consumerist and thus anti-capitalist which perfectly fits into the ideologies held by the punk culture. 
  
  Another reason is the sense of belonging and found family. There are numerous people who believe themselves to be social outcasts due to not following the societal norms and they can find "tribes" of people with similar interests and ideologies. For example if someone young was being bullied they could very easily find groups of people with a shared common ground which act as this anchor for the person. 
  
  This explains why there are so many people who belong/want to join specific youth cultures because it allows people to be social, discuss topics and share ideas freely in a comfortable/safe environment. It also allows young people to develop through the means of [[Secondary Socialisation]] and thus healthily shape them into more responsible and educated adults. In specific it can allow people who want to rebel against societal norms to find likeminded individuals and allows for them to have friends.
