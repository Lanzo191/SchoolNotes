## Research A Youth Subculture 

Goth is a music based subculture that came from England in the 1980s. The subculture came from gothic rock which was an evolution of post-punk music, main musicians in this era where "Siouxsie And The Banshees," "The Cure" and "Joy Division"  The subculture also drew from literature/movies and the German Expressionism Cinema Movement some examples are "Mary Shelleys Frankenstein" "The Cabinet Of Dr. Caligari" and "The Crow"

Younger people can be drawn to this subculture as it has a specific blend of music, fashion, and themes that explore the darker aspects of life, such as mortality and existentialism. Additionally, it can overlap with political values such as liberalism / libertarianism
