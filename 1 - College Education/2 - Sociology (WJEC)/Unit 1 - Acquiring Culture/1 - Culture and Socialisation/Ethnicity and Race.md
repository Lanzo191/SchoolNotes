## Differences between Ethnicity and Race

Ethnicity is the social group characterised by shared cultural traits such as language, religion, traditions, and ancestry that distinguish them from other groups it also includes personal sense of identity and clothing (Modood 2015)

The human race is defined as a social construct used to categorise people based on perceived physical traits, such as skin colour and facial features, rather than biological differences

### Definition of Cultural Resistance and Cultural Hybridity 

Cultural Resistance - is the practice of using cultural means, such as art and literature and clothing, to contest and challenge dominant power structures, often creating alternative visions of society. It serves as a way for individuals and communities to express opposition to oppression and injustice

Cultural Hybridity - refers to the blending and merging of different cultural elements, resulting in new cultural expressions. It highlights the dynamic nature of cultures as they interact and evolve, challenging the idea of fixed and pure cultural identities. But it can result in cultural appropriation
