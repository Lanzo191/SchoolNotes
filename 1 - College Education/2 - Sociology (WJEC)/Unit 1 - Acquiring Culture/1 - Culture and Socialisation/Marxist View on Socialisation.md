# Marxist View on Socialisation

## Overview
**Marxism** is a **conflict perspective** that argues socialization serves the interests of the **ruling class (bourgeoisie)** by maintaining the status quo and reproducing social inequality.

## Key Theories
- **Ideological State Apparatus (ISA)**: **Louis Althusser** argued that agencies like education and the family socialize individuals into accepting the values of capitalism as "normal."
- **Hegemony**: **Antonio Gramsci** explained how the ruling class uses socialization to exert moral and intellectual leadership, making their worldview the dominant ideology.
- **Reproducing Inequality**: Socialization ensures the working class (proletariat) remains compliant, preparing them for exploited labor.

## The Role of Agencies
| Agency | Marxist Perspective |
| --- | --- |
| **Family** | Teaches obedience and respect for authority (hierarchy), preparing children for the workplace. |
| **Education** | The **hidden curriculum** teaches time-keeping and conformity, reinforcing capitalist values. |
| **Media** | Distracts the working class from their exploitation and creates **false class consciousness**. |

## Key Terms
- **Base and Superstructure**: The economy (base) shapes social institutions like socialization (superstructure).
- **False Class Consciousness**: When the working class is unaware of their own exploitation due to dominant ideology.
