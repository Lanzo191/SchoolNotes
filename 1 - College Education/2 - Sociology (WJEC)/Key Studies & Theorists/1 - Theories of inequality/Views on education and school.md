
## Functionalism's view 
- Durkheim viewed schools as 'Society in Miniature' 
- Parsons viewed that schools act as a bridge between values learned un the family and those of society 
- Schools provide [[Marxism and Functionalism|Meritocracy]] and Social Solidarity 
- Overall Positive View 

## Feminist Views (Heaton and Lawson)
- Heaton and Lawson (1996) argued that the [[Hidden Curriculum]] taught patriarchal values in schools and that teachers treated presenting men/women differently 

## Marxist View 
- Schools act to reproduce and legitimise ruling class ideology and culture, encouraging the acceptance of social inequality see also [[Bowles & Gintis Schooling in Capitalism]] and [[Willis Learning to Labour]]
