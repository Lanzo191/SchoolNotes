### Ideology vs Values - 

Values are personable and act as a moral compass where as ideology is a set of beliefs, values and attitude that benefits a specific group. E.G a socialist would have a socialist ideology that favours the common people and welfare equality. They might have values tailored towards forgiveness, sharing and basic kindness without gain. 

### What is a sociological Theory - 

A theory around a set of ideas about society which seeks to explain and analyse social changes, concepts help formulate a theory and for any theory to hold weight it requires evidence e.g a theory around reformation of government is a sociological theory. 

Most theories are split into two groups: 
1. **[[Structural Theory]]**
2. **[[Social Action Theory]]**


Structuralism is a theory that we are controlled/nurtured by a society and all aspects of intelligence / cognition are by society. Everything is a web of institution.(Macro Theory)

Social Action theory is the polar opposite where we can directly influence society via human actions and our personality is built via interaction and not society (Micro Theory)

These two can diverge again:
1. Consensus Theory
2. Conflict Theory

Consensus Theory states that a global consensus is reached and everything is a fair system where institutions reform via the voice of the people. Mainly communistic. 

Conflict theory is capitalistic where things aren't equal and things such as wealth inequality exists, big on class conflict.

# Marxism - 

[[Marxism]] is an example of conflict theory. It is created by [[Karl Marx]](1818 - 1883) It is the belief that society is based off class divisions between those who benefit from the economic system and those who don't. Marx defined most western countries as being capitalist, it allows for the basis of communism to start and the means of production provide a basis. It also  critiques capitalism, emphasising class struggle between the bourgeoisie (owners) and the proletariat (workers), and advocates for a classless society achieved through revolutionary means.
#### Strengths - 
1. Exposes inequality & class conflict
2. Reveals hidden power/ideology
3. Critical, challenges status quo / norms 
4. More just/equal system them capitalism 
5. Redues debt

#### Weaknesses - 
1. Oversimplified Class Divisions 
2. Failed predictions around proletariate overthrowing 
3. abolishes religion and individuality with value consensus 
4. overlooks other important variables of society like gender

# Functionalism - 

### Overview - 

 [[Emile Durkheim]] (1858 - 1917 ) was a french sociologist who was the founding father of [[Functionalism]]. [[Functionalism]] states that each aspect of society is interdependent and contributes to functioning as a whole, they argue that people behave the way they do because society and its social institutions have socialised them into a value consensus.
"Man is a moral being, only because the lives in society. Let all social life disappear and morality will disappear with it."  
It is a Structural Theory with value consensus 

#### Organic Analogy - 

Talcott Parsons proposed that a living organism has organs arranged in systems which lay a role to keep the whole healthy, society is the same and has institutions which are linked together and which have functions to keep society healthy. 

#### Strengths - 

1. shows how different institutions operate in complementary manner to produce overall social stability

2. shows the importance of shared culture as the basis of social order

3. explains how society minimises conflict through [[Socialisation]] and [[Social Control]]

#### Weaknesses - 

1. being maintained by capitalism which is a socially and economically unjust society

2. social stability is based on false consciousness created by institutions like the nuclear family, education, legal and political systems

3. Puts government into role of absolute power and allows them to make changes to state institutions without voting 

4. Value consensus doesn't allow for individuality
