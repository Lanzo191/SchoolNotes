### Questions to answer 

#### What are the negative consequences that he argues have come from a lack of rites of passage? 

- The author Tim Lott argues there are a multitude of negative consequences that happen to people that don't undergo any rite of passage for example; self harm and self starvation and joining gangs. 

#### What examples of rites of passage did men and women used to have?

- He gives the examples of both genders being separated from the other whilst men would "Live in the wilderness" and women would either undergo genital mutilation or education separate from men 

#### Newer smaller rites of passage

- These include drivers tests, graduations, adoption of uniform
