#Marxism #Media

### Overview 
- The Marxist([[Marxism]]) belief is that the media is owned by the bourgeoisie and they instruct editors to put across messages to the audience that spread the **Dominant Ideology** e.g Richard Desmond and Rupert Murdoch. 

- Miliband (1969) argued that editors and journalists depend on owners so they will not use autonomy to resist publishing the bourgeoisie ideology 

- The media reinforces the dominant position of the ruling lass in a capitalist society, this maintains the ruling class hegemony and feeds into the proletariats false class consciousness 

- in the 1997 Election the Sun endorsed Tony Blair and contributed heavily to his victory. The police also used media to spread falsehoods, and the media (seeking sensationalist headlines) failed to challenge those narratives, Right learning sections of the media (particularly the sun) had a vested in interest in reinforcing the narrative of football hooliganism 

- Repressive State Apparatus - refers to institutions like the government, police, and military that maintain control over the working class through coercion and violence, serving the interests of the ruling class. These apparatuses intervene in politics to suppress any threats to the established social order.
