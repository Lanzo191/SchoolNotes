#[[Neo-Marxism]] #Media 
### Overview 

- Journalists have more freedom then typical [[Marxism]] suggests and the media agenda is not directly controlled by the owners (Journalists tend to share the world views of the owner and voluntarily spread the Dominant Ideology) 

- The GUMG (Glasgow Media University Group) claims that media professionals tend to believe in the consensus views and ideas because they are generally unthreatening.
