Feminists argue that the most fundamental division in society is between men and women. Society is **Patriarchal** (male-dominated).

## Sylvia Walby: The Six Structures of Patriarchy
Walby argues that [[Patriarchy]] is not just one thing, but a system of six interacting structures that keep women disadvantaged.

1.  **Paid Employment:** Women are paid less (Gender Pay Gap), occupy lower status jobs ("Glass Ceiling"), and are segregated into "caring" roles.
2.  **Household Production:** Women perform the majority of unpaid domestic [[Labour]] and childcare (The "Dual Burden"), benefiting men.
3.  **The State:** Government policies historically favoured men (e.g., tax laws, lack of childcare support).
4.  **Male Violence:** Domestic violence and the threat of rape control women's behaviour and limit their freedom.
5.  **Sexuality:** Women's sexuality is policed (double standards: "stud" vs "slag"), while male sexuality is normalised.
6.  **Culture:** Media and education reinforce gender stereotypes (e.g., "weak/emotional" women vs "strong/rational" men).

## Types of Feminism on Inequality

### 1. Liberal Feminism
*   **Cause:** Lack of appropriate laws and outdated attitudes.
*   **Solution:** Legal reform (Equal Pay Act, Sex Discrimination Act) and education.
*   **View:** Progress is being made ("March of Progress").

### 2. Marxist Feminism
*   **Cause:** Capitalism. Women serve capitalism as a "Reserve Army of [[Labour]]" (hired in booms, fired in busts) and by reproducing the next generation of workers for free.
*   **Solution:** Overthrow capitalism.

### 3. Radical Feminism
*   **Cause:** Men. Men benefit from women's unpaid work and sexual availability.
*   **Solution:** Separatism (living apart from men) or "Political Lesbianism".
